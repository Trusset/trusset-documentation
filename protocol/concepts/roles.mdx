---
title: "Roles and signatures"
description: "Which party signs each step of a lending market, from deployment to settlement."
---

Every write on a market is a transaction that one party signs from its own wallet. This page maps each step to that party, so a bank and a fintech can see where their work starts and where the other's begins. [How lending works](/protocol/concepts/how-lending-works) defines the objects named here.

Trusset never signs for another party. The API builds each transaction, the named party signs and broadcasts it, and the API verifies the mined receipt before recording anything. A request that carries key material is refused with `400 KEY_MATERIAL_REJECTED`.

## The parties

| Party | Usually | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Deployer | A tokenization platform, an issuer or a fintech that imported the token | The wallet that deployed the market. It nominates candidates for lender of record |
| Token agent | The issuer's tokenization platform | The agent or identity registry role on the collateral token, outside Trusset |
| Lender of record | A bank or licensed lender | The operator wallet, and every market role it names at adoption |
| Price signer | A valuation agent, fund administrator or the bank's pricing desk | `ORACLE_SIGNER` on the market's oracle |
| Depositor | An investor using a fintech's app or the bank's own channel | Vault shares |
| Distributor | The fintech whose instance built the deposit | A share of the interest its depositors' capital earns |
| Borrower | A client of the fintech or the bank | The position and its collateral |
| Liquidator | A collateral desk, a liquidation agent or a liquidation bot | `MARKET_LIQUIDATOR` on the market |
| Router operator | A broker or trading desk | `ROUTER_OPERATOR` on the market's liquidation router |
| Register provider | The keeper of the token's register | The register entry, outside Trusset |

[Operators](/endpoints/operators/introduction#roles) lists the ten market, router and oracle roles the lender of record hands out, with what each one can change.

## Who signs each step

| Step | Signed by | Endpoint |
|---|---|---|
| Deploy the market | Deployer | [Deploy Market](/endpoints/lending/deploy-market) |
| Nominate a candidate | Deployer, or Trusset's factory owner | [Nominate Candidate](/endpoints/lending/nominate-candidate) |
| Authorize the market's adapter and router on the token | Token agent or identity registry operator | [Configure Market](/endpoints/lending/configure-market) |
| Take the market | Lender of record | [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role) |
| Set risk limits, terms and the reserve ratio | The issuer role the lender of record named | [Update Market Config](/endpoints/lending/update-config), [Set Term Config](/endpoints/lending/set-term-config) |
| Install the borrower and provider registers | Market admin | [Set Identity Gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates) |
| Publish terms of use | No signature. A database write by the lender of record's instance | [Save Terms](/endpoints/lending/save-terms) |
| Push a price | Price signer | [Sync Oracle Price](/endpoints/lending/sync-oracle) |
| Fund the pool directly | Operator wallet | [Add Liquidity](/endpoints/lending/add-liquidity) |
| Deposit into a vault | Depositor | [Deposit](/endpoints/vaults/deposit) |
| Open a loan, draw more, add collateral | Borrower | [Open Loan](/endpoints/lending/open-loan), [Borrow More](/endpoints/lending/borrow-more), [Add Collateral](/endpoints/lending/add-collateral) |
| Repay | Any wallet. The collateral always returns to the borrower | [Repay Loan](/endpoints/lending/repay) |
| Start a liquidation | Liquidator | [Liquidate Loan](/endpoints/lending/liquidate) |
| Sell seized collateral and settle | Router operator | [Withdraw Collateral for Sale](/endpoints/lending/withdraw-collateral-for-sale), [Settle Liquidation](/endpoints/lending/settle-liquidation) |
| Pause the market or halt prices | Guardian, or the admin for a pause | [Pause Market](/endpoints/lending/pause-market), [Halt Prices](/endpoints/lending/halt-prices) |
| Collect and pay distributor fees | Any wallet. The vault pays the distributor, never the signer | [Collect Distributor Fees](/endpoints/vaults/collect-distributor-fees), [Pay Distributor](/endpoints/vaults/pay-distributor) |
| Record the pledge in the register | Register provider, in its own register | [List Pledges](/endpoints/asset-register/list-pledges) supplies the facts |

## Where each audience starts

A bank's first signature is the adoption, and from then on its wallets sign every configuration change. [Launch a credit line](/protocol/guides/launch-a-credit-line) walks through them in order.

A tokenization platform signs the deployment and, as the token agent, the authorizations on the token. [Open a market for your asset](/protocol/guides/open-a-market) covers both.

A fintech app signs nothing on the market itself. Its clients sign deposits, loans and repayments from their own wallets, and the app's instance builds each transaction. [Embed earn and borrow](/protocol/guides/embed-earn-and-borrow) shows the flow.
