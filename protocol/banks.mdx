This page is for banks, credit institutions and other licensed lenders that act as lender of record on a Trusset market. It lists what you control, the order to work in, and the page or tool for each step.

## What you control

A market takes no loans until a lender of record adopts it, and from then on it runs on your rules. Trusset approves each lender of record. An instance it has not approved is refused with `CURATOR_NOT_APPROVED`.

**Which markets you take.** Fintechs and tokenization platforms deploy markets and nominate you, or offer them to any approved lender. You review the asset data and the issuer's offering before you sign, and you can decline.

**Who holds each role.** Adoption names the admin, the issuer, the collateral agent, the price signer, the sale recipient and the liquidation operator. Each is a wallet of yours or of an agent you appoint. [Operators](/endpoints/operators/introduction#roles) lists all ten roles.

**The risk limits.** Collateral factor, liquidation threshold, close factor, penalty, caps, loan terms and the reserve ratio. The contract refuses any loan outside them.

**Who borrows and who funds.** The borrower register gates every loan, and the provider register gates every vault depositor. Each can be an identity registry, a verification profile or a whitelist.

**How collateral is sold.** You declare the realization mode at adoption: exchange sale, issuer redemption or auction. The sale recipient is fixed with it.

**Where the capital comes from.** Your operator wallet funds the pool directly. Investors fund it through your vaults, which reach only the markets you approved.

## Your path

1. **Set up an instance.** Start on a free development instance and register the wallet that will sign. See [Instances](/protocol/infrastructure/instances) and [Custody](/protocol/infrastructure/custody). Production instances need access granted by Trusset.
2. **Find and review a market.** [List Queued Markets](/endpoints/operators/list-queued-markets) shows what your instance can take. [Get Queued Market](/endpoints/operators/get-queued-market) returns the asset data, the offering and a deterministic review.
3. **Take the role.** [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role) builds the adoption and [Confirm Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/confirm-lender-role) records it. Adoption is final, so check every address in it first.
4. **Configure and publish.** [Get Configuration Status](/endpoints/lending/get-configuration-status) lists what is outstanding. Set the limits with [Update Market Config](/endpoints/lending/update-config) and publish your terms of use with [Save Terms](/endpoints/lending/save-terms).
5. **Gate it.** Install the registers with [Set Identity Gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates). To receive KYC files from a fintech's verifier, follow the [KYC Courier bank walkthrough](/tools/kyc-courier/bank-walkthrough).
6. **Price it.** Your price signer pushes prices with [Sync Oracle Price](/endpoints/lending/sync-oracle) before the last one turns stale, 24 hours by default.
7. **Fund it.** Deposit with [Add Liquidity](/endpoints/lending/add-liquidity), then open a vault in the [Issuer Portal](https://issuers.trusset.org) and connect it with [Set Vault Liquidity](/endpoints/lending/set-vault-liquidity).
8. **Watch it.** [Hooks](/endpoints/lending/list-hooks) run risk monitors on a schedule. [Get Liquidatable Loans](/endpoints/lending/get-liquidatable-loans) and [Get Market Metrics](/endpoints/lending/get-metrics) give the live picture.
9. **Realize collateral.** Grant the liquidator role with [Set Liquidator Role](/endpoints/operators/set-liquidator-role) or connect a [liquidation bot](/endpoints/lending/list-bots). Settlement follows the route you declared. See [Liquidations](/endpoints/lending/introduction#liquidations).
10. **Collect what you earned.** The operator share leaves through [Sweep Operator Fees](/endpoints/lending/sweep-operator-fees). The insurance fund is yours to top up and, after notice, to withdraw.

[Launch a credit line](/protocol/guides/launch-a-credit-line) walks the same path with the decision you make at each step.

## Tools for banks

| Tool | What it does for a bank | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| Issuer Portal | Instances, API keys, vaults and day-to-day monitoring, without code | [issuers.trusset.org](https://issuers.trusset.org) |
| KYC Courier | Receives KYC files from verifiers as ciphertext only the bank can open | [Bank walkthrough](/tools/kyc-courier/bank-walkthrough) |
| Liquidation bots | Contracts the bank deploys and funds to liquidate on its markets | [Liquidation Bots](/endpoints/lending/list-bots) |
| Hooks | Risk monitors and signed notices on every liquidation | [Hooks](/sdk/lending/hooks) |
| SDK `lending` module | Typed client for markets, roles, liquidations and vaults. Ships with 0.4.0, not yet released | [Lending SDK](/sdk/lending/overview) |

## Where fintechs fit

Fintechs bring the asset and the clients. A tokenization platform deploys the market and nominates you. An app brings depositors to your vaults and borrowers to your markets, and earns the fixed distributor share once you approve it with [Set Distributor Approval](/endpoints/vaults/set-distributor-approval). Neither can change a parameter you set. [Trusset for fintechs](/protocol/fintechs) shows their side of the same market.
