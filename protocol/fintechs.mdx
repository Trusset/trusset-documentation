This page is for fintechs. Tokenization platforms want the assets they issued to work as loan collateral. Apps, brokers and wealth platforms want to add earn and borrow for their clients. Most fintechs follow one of the two tracks below, some follow both.

## What you keep, and what the lender decides

You keep the client relationship, the interface and the onboarding. Your clients sign every deposit, loan and repayment from their own wallets, and your instance builds each transaction for them.

The lender of record decides the rest: the rates, the loan-to-value limits, who may borrow and how seized collateral is sold. A market you deploy takes no loans until a bank adopts it, and once adopted, only the bank's roles change its parameters. [Roles and signatures](/protocol/concepts/roles) draws the line step by step.

## Track 1: bring an asset to lending

For tokenization platforms and issuers whose clients hold a token they would rather borrow against than sell.

1. **Import the token.** [Detect Token](/endpoints/tokenization/detect-token), then [Import Token](/endpoints/tokenization/import-token). A market can only be deployed against a token your instance imported.
2. **Describe it.** Publish the profile with [Update Profile](/endpoints/tokenization/update-profile) and the instrument record lenders read with [Save Instrument](/endpoints/tokenization/save-instrument). A token without a published profile is refused at deployment with `TOKEN_PROFILE_REQUIRED`.
3. **Deploy the market.** [Deploy Market](/endpoints/lending/deploy-market) picks the collateral mode, the price source and the opening rate and term offering, and nominates the banks that may take it.
4. **Authorize it on the token.** The token's agent or identity registry operator signs the steps [Get Setup Steps](/endpoints/lending/get-setup-steps) lists. That is often your platform.
5. **Hand it to a bank.** A nominated bank takes the market with [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role). [Get Nominations](/endpoints/lending/get-nominations) shows who may.
6. **Keep the register in step.** Whoever keeps the token's register records each pledge from [List Pledges](/endpoints/asset-register/list-pledges). The register entry is the operative act, and it stays with the register provider.

[Open a market for your asset](/protocol/guides/open-a-market) walks this track with the decisions at each step.

## Track 2: add earn and borrow to your app

For apps, brokers, neobanks and wealth platforms.

1. **Onboard clients.** Get each client's wallet through the gate the bank chose. Use a hosted [ID link](/endpoints/customers/create-id-link) if you have no KYC process, [direct verification](/endpoints/customers/verify-identity) if you ran KYC yourself, or [KYC-STARKs](/tools/kyc-starks/overview) for proof-backed claims. [Onboarding and access](/protocol/concepts/onboarding) shows which path fills which gate.
2. **Send the KYC file when the bank asks.** A bank that gates on a whitelist can name you as its KYC source. Seal each client's file with KYC Courier, and the bank lists the wallet once it approves the file. See the [verifier walkthrough](/tools/kyc-courier/verifier-walkthrough).
3. **Offer earn.** [List Vaults](/endpoints/vaults/list-vaults) returns every published vault on your network. Show the operating bank from `operatedBy` wherever you show the vault. [Deposit](/endpoints/vaults/deposit) builds the client's approval and deposit and names your instance as distributor.
4. **Collect your share.** Once the vault's bank approves your instance as a distributor, it earns the distributor share of the interest its depositors' capital produces. Before that, deposits still go through and the share goes to the vault operator. [Get Earnings](/endpoints/vaults/get-earnings) shows it, and [Collect Distributor Fees](/endpoints/vaults/collect-distributor-fees) with [Pay Distributor](/endpoints/vaults/pay-distributor) pays it out.
5. **Offer borrow.** Quote with [Get Max Borrow](/endpoints/lending/get-max-borrow), then build the loan with [Open Loan](/endpoints/lending/open-loan). On a `FREEZE` market the collateral stays in the client's wallet. Borrowing runs on markets your instance holds, such as one you deployed in track 1.
6. **Show positions.** [Get Lending Positions](/endpoints/customers/get-lending-positions) lists one client's loans and debt. [Get Position Statement](/endpoints/lending/get-position-statement) returns a PDF with the verified transaction trail.

[Embed earn and borrow](/protocol/guides/embed-earn-and-borrow) walks this track with the failure cases your app has to handle.

## Tools for fintechs

| Tool | What it does for a fintech | Start here |
|---|---|---|
| SDK | Typed TypeScript client. The published 0.1.0 carries `customers`; `lending` and `tokens` ship with 0.4.0, not yet released | [SDK](/sdk/introduction) |
| KYC Courier | Seals a client's KYC file so only the bank can open it, and delivers it | [Verifier walkthrough](/tools/kyc-courier/verifier-walkthrough) |
| KYC-STARKs | Proves facts about a client, such as age or residence, without sharing the KYC data | [KYC-STARKs](/tools/kyc-starks/overview) |
| Issuer Portal | Instances, API keys and imported tokens, without code | [issuers.trusset.org](https://issuers.trusset.org) |

## Where banks fit

Every market needs a lender of record before it lends, and every vault belongs to one. The bank sets the terms your clients see and carries the license. [Trusset for banks](/protocol/banks) shows their side of the same market.
