Trusset is credit infrastructure for real-world assets. Banks act as lender of record on lending markets, and fintechs put earn and borrow inside their own apps. Trusset builds every transaction and never signs one on a client's behalf.

Banks start at [For banks](/protocol/banks), fintechs at [For fintechs](/protocol/fintechs). Questions go to <ContactEmail />.
