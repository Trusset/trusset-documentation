---
title: 'Pause Market'
api: 'POST /seller-financing/api/markets/{marketId}/pause'
description: 'Calldata that stops new listings, purchases and liquidations'
---

<Note>
  Financing is in beta and runs on Ethereum Sepolia instances only for now (`DEVELOPMENT` instances on Ethereum). A `PRODUCTION` instance, or one on another network, answers `503 SELLER_FINANCING_UNAVAILABLE`. See [Financing](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction).
</Note>

Builds `pause` on the market for a holder of the operator role to sign. A paused market refuses new listings, purchases and liquidations with `EnforcedPause`. Everything that settles existing business stays open: cancellations, repayments, asset claims and withdrawals. See [Pausing](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction#pausing).

Resume with [Unpause Market](/endpoints/seller-financing/unpause-market).

<Warning>
  A pause also stops liquidation. Interest keeps accruing on a loan in default while the market is paused, but the seller cannot withdraw from the sale until the market is unpaused.
</Warning>

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/seller-financing/list-markets).</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="from" type="string" required>The wallet that signs: a holder of `OPERATOR_ROLE` on the market, the operator named at deployment unless the admin moved the role. Without it the answer is `400 FROM_REQUIRED`. Any other wallet answers `409 TX_WOULD_REVERT` with `AccessControlUnauthorizedAccount`, and `details.requiredRole` and `details.roleHolders` name the role and the wallets that hold it now.</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="action" type="string">`SIGN_TRANSACTIONS`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="chainId" type="integer">The chain the step is built for.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="steps" type="array">One step, `pause` on the market, with `gasLimit`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="confirmStepIndex" type="integer">`0`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="preflight" type="object">`{ simulated: true, gasEstimate }`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="signer" type="object">`{ role, holders, holdersComplete }`. `role` is `OPERATOR_ROLE`. `holders` lists the wallets that hold it now, `null` when they could not be read. `holdersComplete` is `false` when the market's role history was read in part, so a holder may be missing.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `from` is malformed, or the body carries another field. `error.details` names it |
| `FROM_REQUIRED` | `400` | `from` is missing. The message names who signs: a wallet that holds `OPERATOR_ROLE` on the market |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID that your instance reaches on its network |
| `TX_WOULD_REVERT` | `409` | `EnforcedPause` when the market is already paused, or `AccessControlUnauthorizedAccount` when `from` lacks the operator role, with `details.requiredRole` and `details.roleHolders` |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The chain could not be read. Nothing was built. Retry shortly |
| `SELLER_FINANCING_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The instance is a `PRODUCTION` instance or not on Ethereum |
| `PAUSE_BUILD_FAILED` | `500` | Unexpected failure. Nothing was built. Retry, or contact support with the request ID |
