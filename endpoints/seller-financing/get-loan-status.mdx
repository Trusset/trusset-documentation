---
title: 'Get Loan Status'
api: 'GET /seller-financing/api/markets/{marketId}/listings/{listingId}/status'
description: 'What an active loan owes now, when the next payment is due, and whether it is in default'
---

<Note>
  Seller Financing is in beta and runs on Ethereum Sepolia instances only for now (`DEVELOPMENT` instances on Ethereum). A `PRODUCTION` instance, or one on another network, answers `503 SELLER_FINANCING_UNAVAILABLE`. See [Seller Financing](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction).
</Note>

Reads the loan of an `ACTIVE` listing from the market contract at the current block: the interest due now, the payoff, the next due date with its amount, whether the loan can be liquidated, and whether the asset can still be delivered to the buyer. A listing in any other status answers with its status and `active: false`. A `REPAID` one adds `assetLeg`, and a `LIQUIDATED` one adds `liquidation`, the account of the liquidation read from the market's `LoanLiquidated` event.

Interest accrues by the second, so every figure here grows until the next payment. See [Due dates, grace and default](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction#due-dates-grace-and-default).

## Paying a period on time

Interest cannot be paid in advance. A payment before `nextDue.dueAt` settles only the interest accrued until then, and the excess goes to principal, so the period still closes with interest owed. Pay once `nextDue.dueAt` has passed, and before `liquidatableAt`.

That window is the listing's grace period: `liquidatableAt` is `nextDue.dueAt` plus the grace period plus one second. A listing with several interest periods therefore carries a grace period of at least one day, and [Create Listing](/endpoints/seller-financing/create-listing) refuses less with `GRACE_PERIOD_REQUIRED`. On a single-period listing with a grace period of 0, pay off in full before maturity instead.

A loan whose interest periods are shorter than its runtime and whose grace period is under one day carries `graceWarning`. On such a loan a payment mined before `nextDue.dueAt` settles only the interest accrued until it is mined, so the interest that accrues between the payment and the due date stays owed and the loan can be liquidated from `liquidatableAt`. See [Due dates, grace and default](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction#due-dates-grace-and-default).

Send at least `interestDue` plus the repay fee to [Repay](/endpoints/seller-financing/repay). The fee is carved out of the payment, and it is the smaller of the market's `repayFee` and 0.5 percent of the amount applied. Leave a small margin for the interest that accrues until the payment is mined: anything above the interest due goes to principal.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/seller-financing/list-markets).</ParamField>

<ParamField path="listingId" type="string" required>The listing's ID on the market.</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="id" type="string">The listing ID.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">The listing's status.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="active" type="boolean">`true` while the status is `ACTIVE`. The fields below are returned only then, except `settlementModel`, `assetLeg`, `heldPayment` and `liquidation`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="settlementModel" type="string">`CREDIT_AT_PAYOFF` or `DELIVERY_AGAINST_PAYMENT`, `null` when unread. See [Market generations](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction#market-generations).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="heldPayment" type="object">On a `REPAID` listing of a delivery-against-payment market: the held price, `deliverBy` and `unwindableAt`, as on [Get Listing](/endpoints/seller-financing/get-listing#response-fields).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="interestDue" type="string">Interest owed now, base and premium together, in the payment token's smallest unit.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="interestDueFormatted" type="string">The same in whole units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="payoff" type="object">What a full repayment would pay now: `{ interest, principal, fee, total, totalFormatted }`, in smallest units, with `total` the sum of the other three. [Repay](/endpoints/seller-financing/repay) with `amount: "full"` approves `total` plus a margin.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="nextDue" type="object">
      The first due date whose interest is not yet covered, projected on the principal outstanding now.
      <Expandable>
        <ResponseField name="dueAt" type="integer">Unix time of that due date. When every period's interest up to maturity is paid, it is the maturity.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="interestAmount" type="string">Interest charged up to `dueAt` and not yet paid, in smallest units. `"0"` when only the principal is left.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="interestAmountFormatted" type="string">The same in whole units.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="principalAmount" type="string">The principal outstanding when `dueAt` is the maturity, `"0"` otherwise.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="principalAmountFormatted" type="string">The same in whole units.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="isLiquidatable" type="boolean">`true` when the loan is in default and the seller or the operator can liquidate it now. `null` when unread.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="liquidatableAt" type="integer">Unix time the loan becomes liquidatable if nothing more is paid: one second after the grace period of `nextDue.dueAt`. In the past once the loan is in default.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="assetLeg" type="object">On an `ACTIVE` or `REPAID` listing: whether the asset can still be delivered to the buyer, as on [Get Listing](/endpoints/seller-financing/get-listing#response-fields). With `deliverable: false`, the asset claim and the delivery are refused with `409 ASSET_NOT_DELIVERABLE`, and on a first-generation market a repayment that completes the loan too. See [Asset leg](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction#asset-leg).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="graceWarning" type="object">Only on a loan whose interest periods are shorter than its runtime and whose grace period is under one day: `code` (`GRACE_PERIOD_BELOW_FLOOR`), `gracePeriod`, `interestPeriod` and `minimum` in seconds, `dueAt`, `liquidatableAt`, `interestAccruingBeforeDueAt` (the interest that accrues from now until `dueAt`, which a payment mined now leaves owed at the due date) with its formatted twin, and `message`. Inside the grace window `message` names what is owed now and by when; once the loan can be liquidated it says so, and past maturity it says that only a full payoff stops a liquidation.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="liquidation" type="object">
      On a `LIQUIDATED` listing: the account of the liquidation, read from the market's `LoanLiquidated` event. Amounts are in the payment token's smallest unit, each with a `...Formatted` twin.
      <Expandable>
        <ResponseField name="readable" type="boolean">`false` when the event could not be read from the market's event log just now, with `note`. Read again shortly.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="liquidatedAt" type="integer">Unix time of the block that mined the liquidation. `null` when the block could not be read.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="liquidatedBy" type="string">The wallet that signed it.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="liquidatedByRole" type="string">`SELLER` or `OPERATOR`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="transactionHash" type="string">The liquidation transaction.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="blockNumber" type="integer">Its block.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="refundable" type="string">The escrow settled: the downpayment plus the principal the buyer had paid.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="compensation" type="string">1.5 percent of the downpayment, rounded up, kept by the seller.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="interestTaken" type="string">Interest owed at the liquidation and taken from the escrow.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="trussetShare" type="string">20 percent of the compensation and the interest taken, booked as Trusset's infrastructure fees.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="sellerCredit" type="string">What the liquidation credited to the seller.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="buyerRefund" type="string">What the liquidation credited to the buyer.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="interestPaidTotal" type="string">All interest on the loan, the interest taken included.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="interestPaidBefore" type="string">Interest the buyer paid before the liquidation. It is not refunded.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="seller" type="object">`{ address, amount, amountFormatted, claimableNow, claimableNowFormatted }`: the seller's credit from this liquidation and everything the seller can withdraw from the market now, credits from other sales included. `claimableNow` is `null` when unread.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="buyer" type="object">The same for the buyer, with the refund as `amount`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="asset" type="string">Where the asset went: in freeze mode the lock is released in the seller's wallet (on a delivery-against-payment market, except what the seller's other live sales still need frozen); in custody mode the adapter returns it to the seller or keeps it in escrow for the seller ([Get Listing](/endpoints/seller-financing/get-listing) shows `collateralEscrow`).</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="nextStep" type="string">Each party withdraws with [Withdraw](/endpoints/seller-financing/withdraw); the path is given with the market ID.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="note" type="string">The account in words.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<Warning>
  When the loan's figures cannot be read, an active listing answers with `id`, `status`, `active: true` and `assetLeg` only. Missing figures are unread, not zero. Retry before acting on them.
</Warning>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "https://api.trusset.org/seller-financing/api/markets/cmsg4x7k20003sf9q1d8v6b2e/listings/4/status" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here"
```
```typescript TypeScript
const res = await fetch(
  `https://api.trusset.org/seller-financing/api/markets/${marketId}/listings/${listingId}/status`,
  { headers: { 'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here' } }
);
const { data } = await res.json();
if (data.active && data.nextDue) {
  console.log(`Next due ${new Date(data.nextDue.dueAt * 1000).toISOString()}: ${data.nextDue.interestAmountFormatted}`);
  console.log(`In default from ${new Date(data.liquidatableAt * 1000).toISOString()}`);
}
```
</RequestExample>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `INVALID_LISTING_ID` | `400` | `listingId` is not a positive integer of up to 19 digits |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID that your instance reaches on its network |
| `LISTING_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No listing with this ID on the market |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market could not be read. Retry shortly |
| `SELLER_FINANCING_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The instance is a `PRODUCTION` instance or not on Ethereum |
| `LOAN_STATUS_FAILED` | `500` | Unexpected failure. Retry, or contact support with the request ID |
