---
title: 'Get Claimable'
api: 'GET /seller-financing/api/markets/{marketId}/claimable/{address}'
description: 'What a wallet can withdraw from the market now'
---

<Note>
  Seller Financing is in beta and runs on Ethereum Sepolia instances only for now (`DEVELOPMENT` instances on Ethereum). A `PRODUCTION` instance, or one on another network, answers `503 SELLER_FINANCING_UNAVAILABLE`. See [Seller Financing](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction).
</Note>

Returns the payment-token balance the market holds for one wallet and that wallet can withdraw with [Withdraw](/endpoints/seller-financing/withdraw). The market pays nothing out on its own: every amount it owes waits here until its owner withdraws it.

What adds to a wallet's balance:

- **Seller.** Its share of every interest payment, when the payment arrives. The full price, when the loan is repaid in full. The compensation and the interest owed, less Trusset's share, when it liquidates.
- **Buyer.** Its refund, when the seller liquidates.

One balance covers every listing of the wallet on this market.

When the balance is not zero, `credits` breaks it down: every credit booked to the wallet since it last withdrew, read from the market's event log, each with its listing, its source and its transaction. `reconciled` is `true` when they add up to `claimable`.

On a custody market the answer also carries `collateralEscrow`: the asset the custody adapter holds in escrow for the wallet, because the token refused to return it on a cancellation or a liquidation. It is reclaimed with [Claim Escrowed Collateral](/endpoints/seller-financing/claim-escrowed-collateral), not with [Withdraw](/endpoints/seller-financing/withdraw).

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/seller-financing/list-markets).</ParamField>

<ParamField path="address" type="string" required>The wallet to read.</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="account" type="string">The wallet, checksummed.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="claimable" type="string">What it can withdraw now, in the payment token's smallest unit.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="claimableFormatted" type="string">The same in whole units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="collateralEscrow" type="string">Custody markets only. The asset the custody adapter holds in escrow for the wallet, in the asset token's smallest unit. `"0"` when there is none, `null` when the adapter could not be read. Absent on a freeze market.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="collateralEscrowFormatted" type="string">The same in whole units of the asset. `null` when unread or when the asset's decimals could not be read.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="credits" type="object">
      Only when `claimable` is not zero: what the balance is made of.
      <Expandable>
        <ResponseField name="readable" type="boolean">`false` when the market's event log could not be read just now. `items` is then `null`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="reconciled" type="boolean">`true` when the items add up to `claimable`. `false` when the log was read in part or a credit landed after the read; read again shortly.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="items" type="array">The credits since the wallet last withdrew, oldest first: `{ listingId, source, amount, amountFormatted, transactionHash, blockNumber }`. `source` is `INTEREST_SHARE` (the seller's share of an interest payment), `PAYOFF` (the price credited at a payoff on a first-generation market), `DELIVERY` (the held price credited at delivery), `LIQUIDATION_CREDIT` (the seller's compensation and interest at a liquidation, less Trusset's share), `LIQUIDATION_REFUND` (the buyer's refund at a liquidation) or `UNWIND_REFUND` (the buyer's refund when a sale is unwound).</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="note" type="string">What the items show, or why they do not add up yet.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "https://api.trusset.org/seller-financing/api/markets/cmsg4x7k20003sf9q1d8v6b2e/claimable/0x09BD3f5c18488e492a7bC3C7CCfc6cc837f511e8" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here"
```
```typescript TypeScript
const res = await fetch(
  `https://api.trusset.org/seller-financing/api/markets/${marketId}/claimable/${wallet}`,
  { headers: { 'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here' } }
);
const { data } = await res.json();
if (data.claimable !== '0') console.log(`${data.claimableFormatted} ready to withdraw`);
```
</RequestExample>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `address` is malformed, the zero address, or fails its EIP-55 checksum |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID that your instance reaches on its network |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market could not be read. Retry shortly |
| `SELLER_FINANCING_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The instance is a `PRODUCTION` instance or not on Ethereum |
| `CLAIMABLE_READ_FAILED` | `500` | Unexpected failure. Retry, or contact support with the request ID |
