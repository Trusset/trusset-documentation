---
title: 'Repay'
api: 'POST /seller-financing/api/markets/{marketId}/listings/{listingId}/repay'
description: 'Calldata for a partial repayment or the full payoff of an active loan'
---

<Note>
  Seller Financing is in beta and runs on Ethereum Sepolia instances only for now (`DEVELOPMENT` instances on Ethereum). A `PRODUCTION` instance, or one on another network, answers `503 SELLER_FINANCING_UNAVAILABLE`. See [Seller Financing](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction).
</Note>

Builds a repayment on an `ACTIVE` loan: an exact approval on the payment token, then `repay`. Any wallet can repay any active loan, and the payment is booked to the loan. `from` is the payer.

The market applies a payment in this order:

1. The repay fee is carved out of it: the smaller of the market's `repayFee` and 0.5 percent of the amount applied.
2. The rest settles the interest due up to the moment of payment.
3. What remains reduces the principal and is held in escrow until the loan completes or is liquidated.

The seller is credited its share of the interest at once, and Trusset 20 percent of it. Interest cannot be paid in advance. To pay a period on time, see [Paying a period on time](/endpoints/seller-financing/get-loan-status#paying-a-period-on-time).

The payment that brings the principal to zero closes the loan. The listing reads `REPAID`, and the buyer can claim the asset with [Claim Asset](/endpoints/seller-financing/claim-asset). On a first-generation market the seller is credited the full price at that moment. On a delivery-against-payment market the market holds it until the asset is delivered, and the response says so in `settlement`. See [Market generations](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction#market-generations).

## When the asset cannot be delivered

Before building, the API checks the listing's asset leg and returns it as `assetLeg`. See [Asset leg](/endpoints/seller-financing/introduction#asset-leg). While the asset can no longer be delivered to the buyer (`assetLeg.deliverable: false`), a repayment that would complete the loan, `amount: "full"` or an amount at or above the payoff, is treated by market generation:

- **First-generation market.** Refused with `409 ASSET_NOT_DELIVERABLE`: it would credit the seller the full price while the delivery to the buyer reverts.
- **Delivery-against-payment market.** Built, with the leg's text in `warning`: the price stays held until delivery, and 30 days after the payoff the buyer can unwind the sale with [Unwind Sale](/endpoints/seller-financing/unwind-sale).
- **Generation unreadable.** `503 CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE`, nothing built.

A partial repayment always builds, and carries the leg's text in `warning`. A leg that could not be read (`checked: false`) never refuses a repayment.

## Loans with less than a day of grace

On a loan whose interest periods are shorter than its runtime and whose grace period is under one day, a partial repayment carries `graceWarning`, and its `message` is appended to `warning`. A payment mined before the next due date settles only the interest accrued until it is mined, so the interest that accrues until the due date stays owed, and with no grace the loan can be liquidated one second after the due date. `graceWarning` names the due date, `liquidatableAt` and that interest. Paying off in full closes the loan and carries no such warning. [Purchase](/endpoints/seller-financing/purchase) no longer builds such a listing; the warning covers loans that were bought before or outside the API.

## Paying off in full

With `amount: "full"`, the API reads the payoff now and approves it plus a 0.5 percent margin and one smallest unit, for the interest that accrues until the payment is mined. The market takes the payoff at the moment of mining and never more than the approved amount. A last step resets the allowance to zero, so no unused margin stays approved.

<Warning>
  If the transaction is mined much later than it was built, the margin may not cover the interest accrued meanwhile. The payment then applies in full and the loan stays `ACTIVE` with a small remainder. Read [Get Loan Status](/endpoints/seller-financing/get-loan-status) after mining and pay the remainder.
</Warning>

An `amount` at or above the payoff is also built as a full repayment, with `fullRepayment: true` and the closing reset, but without the margin: the approval is exactly `amount`. An `amount` equal to the payoff when the request was built will almost always leave a remainder, because interest accrues until the payment is mined. Send `"full"` to close a loan.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/seller-financing/list-markets).</ParamField>

<ParamField path="listingId" type="string" required>The listing's ID on the market.</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="amount" type="string" required>
  `"full"` to pay off the loan, or the most the payment may take, as a decimal string in whole units of the payment token, for example `"128"`. It must be greater than zero. The market never takes more than the payoff, so an amount above it takes only the payoff.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="from" type="string" required>The paying wallet, which signs the steps, normally the buyer. It must hold the payoff for a full repayment, otherwise `amount`. Without it the answer is `400 FROM_REQUIRED`.</ParamField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://api.trusset.org/seller-financing/api/markets/cmsg4x7k20003sf9q1d8v6b2e/listings/4/repay" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"amount": "full", "from": "0x09BD3f5c18488e492a7bC3C7CCfc6cc837f511e8"}'
```
```typescript TypeScript
const res = await fetch(
  `https://api.trusset.org/seller-financing/api/markets/${marketId}/listings/${listingId}/repay`,
  {
    method: 'POST',
    headers: { 'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here', 'Content-Type': 'application/json' },
    body: JSON.stringify({ amount: 'full', from: await buyer.getAddress() })
  }
);
const { data } = await res.json();
for (const step of data.steps) {
  const tx = await buyer.sendTransaction({ to: step.to, data: step.data, value: 0, gasLimit: step.gasLimit });
  await tx.wait();
}
```
</RequestExample>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `INVALID_LISTING_ID` | `400` | `listingId` is not a positive integer of up to 19 digits |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `amount` is neither `"full"` nor a decimal string, or is finer than the payment token's decimals, or `from` is malformed. `error.details` names the field |
| `FROM_REQUIRED` | `400` | `from` is missing. The message names who signs: the wallet that pays, normally the buyer |
| `INVALID_AMOUNT` | `400` | `amount` is zero |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID that your instance reaches on its network |
| `LISTING_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No listing with this ID on the market |
| `INVALID_STATUS` | `409` | The listing is not `ACTIVE`. `details.status` names its status |
| `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` | `409` | `from` holds less of the payment token than the payment needs: the payoff for a full repayment, otherwise `amount`. `details` carries `token`, `owner`, `balance` and `needed` |
| `ASSET_NOT_DELIVERABLE` | `409` | First-generation market: the repayment would complete the loan while the asset can no longer be delivered to the buyer. `details.assetLeg` carries the check. A partial repayment still builds, and a delivery-against-payment market builds the payoff with a `warning` |
| `TX_WOULD_REVERT` | `409` | The repayment would revert. Without an approval step, every revert of the simulated repayment answers here, including `SafeERC20FailedOperation` and `PaymentTokenNotSupported` from a token that charges a fee on transfer. With an approval step, a refusal by the payment token itself makes the API simulate the approvals and the repayment together: a repayment that still reverts there answers here with the decoded refusal, and only when that bundle cannot be simulated is the build let through with `preflight.simulated: false`. Other refusals, such as a text reason from a paused token, answer here either way |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The chain could not be read, including the market implementation when the asset cannot be delivered and the payment would complete the loan. Nothing was built. Retry shortly |
| `SELLER_FINANCING_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The instance is a `PRODUCTION` instance or not on Ethereum |
| `REPAY_BUILD_FAILED` | `500` | Unexpected failure. Nothing was built. Retry, or contact support with the request ID |
