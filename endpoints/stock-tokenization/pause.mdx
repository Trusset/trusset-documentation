---
title: 'Pause Token'
api: 'POST /stocks/api/manage/{tokenAddress}/pause'
description: 'Halt all transfers on a stock token contract'
---

Pauses the token contract, preventing all transfers until unpaused. Issuance and redemption are also blocked while paused. Typically used during corporate events, regulatory holds, or emergency situations.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="tokenAddress" type="string" required>
  Address of the stock token contract.
</ParamField>

## Request Body

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="success" type="boolean">Calldata was built successfully.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="txData" type="object">
      The unsigned transaction. `to` is the token contract, `data` is the encoded `pause` call, and `value` is always `"0"`.
    </ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<Note>
  Nothing is submitted on your behalf. Sign `txData` with a wallet holding the required role on the token and broadcast it yourself.
</Note>
