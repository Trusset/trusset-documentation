---
title: 'Deposit'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/vaults/{vaultId}/deposit'
description: 'Approve-and-deposit calldata that names your instance as the depositor''s distributor'
---

Builds the two transactions a deposit needs: an ERC-20 approval for the vault to pull its asset, a settlement token (stablecoin), then the deposit itself. The depositor's wallet signs both, in order, and receives vault shares at the current share price. On a vault that supports distributor attribution, the deposit also names your instance as the depositor's distributor.

When a market the vault funded wrote off distributor accrual that the vault has not recorded yet, one `recordMarketLoss` step per such market comes first. Recording the loss before the deposit keeps the interest this deposit earns from being charged with a loss from before it arrived. Any wallet may sign those steps.

The vault admits a depositor only when every register it reads verifies the wallet. That is the vault's own identity register, when the vault names one, and the provider register of every market the vault funds or has consented to draw for. The vault operator's register provider and each market's register provider admit depositors.

A vault without a register of its own takes its depositor gate from its markets. On the current vault implementation it adopts the provider register of the first market it funds or consents to draw for, and keeps it for good, also after it stops funding that market. Every deposit and share transfer is then examined against the adopted register as well. Until it has adopted one, a vault that funds no market with a provider register refuses every deposit and share transfer on-chain (`DepositorGateRequired`), and the API refuses such a deposit first with `DEPOSITOR_GATE_REQUIRED`. The API also prepares no deposit into a vault without a register of its own on an earlier implementation, which would admit any wallet.

`DEPOSITOR_NOT_VERIFIED` names the register that refused the wallet: the vault's own, the one it adopted, or a market's provider register together with the market. When that register is the eligibility register of one of your verification profiles, the refusal names the profile as well. Onboard the wallet under that profile and admit it with [Request Admission](/endpoints/customers/request-admission) before the deposit. [Get Wallet Eligibility](/endpoints/customers/get-wallet-eligibility) shows where a wallet stands.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="vaultId" type="string" required>Vault ID.</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="amount" type="string" required>
  Amount to deposit as a decimal string, for example `"1000"`. Digits with an optional fraction, greater than zero. Denominated in the vault's asset and parsed at `assetDecimals`, so a finer fraction answers `VALIDATION_ERROR`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="holderAddress" type="string">
  Wallet that will sign. When it is a `0x` address, the API checks it against the vault's register and, on a vault with distributor attribution, against the register the vault adopted when it has none of its own and each market's provider register. It refuses with `DEPOSITOR_NOT_VERIFIED` when the deposit would revert, and with `ELIGIBILITY_REMOVAL_PENDING` when the wallet's admission to one of those registers waits for removal. It also checks that the wallet holds `amount` of the asset, and reads whether the wallet is already bound to a distributor. Omit it to skip these checks; the contract enforces the registers either way. Any other value is ignored.
</ParamField>

## When a deposit is refused on the vault's own state

Two refusals are about the vault rather than the depositor. Both stop shares being issued against a number nobody can stand behind, and neither affects redemptions, which stay open throughout.

A deposit is refused with `VAULT_BOOK_INCOMPLETE` when a market the vault funds is not answering. The vault cannot total its own book, so it would issue shares at a price computed from a partial book. The message names the market. It clears when that market answers again, or when the operator writes it off.

A deposit is refused with `VAULT_WIPED_OUT` when shares are in issue against assets totalling zero. A deposit into that vault would be absorbed by the existing holders rather than buying a position. A vault whose value is gone cannot be recapitalized, and the operator has to run a new one.

<Warning>
  These checks, and the pause check, read the vault's live state. When that read fails, the API still returns calldata. The current vault implementation refuses all three cases on-chain. The earlier one, `supportsNotice: false` on [Get Vault](/endpoints/vaults/get-vault), does not refuse a wiped-out vault, so do not sign there while `onChain.readFailed` is `true`.
</Warning>

## Distributor attribution

The instance whose API key builds a deposit is that depositor's distributor, and a market's [Deposit to Vault](/endpoints/lending/deposit-to-vault) route attributes the same way. The deposit step names your instance's distributor wallet in `deposit(amount, distributor)`. That is its primary verified wallet, or its earliest verified wallet when none is primary. The vault binds the signing wallet to the first distributor a deposit names and never rebinds it. Every share the holder has or later receives is then attributed to that distributor. A share transfer moves the attribution to the recipient's distributor, or to none.

Each market sets aside a distributor share: 5 percent of repaid interest after Trusset's 15 percent infrastructure fee, and 5 percent of each fixed transaction fee. It credits that share to its liquidity providers per share, and the vault's part is its slice. The vault passes the part earned by attributed shares to their distributors and the rest to the vault operator. [Get Earnings](/endpoints/vaults/get-earnings) shows what your instance has earned.

On the current vault implementation a deposit may bind a new holder only to the vault owner or to a distributor the vault operator approved. When your instance's wallet is not approved, the API names no distributor unless the wallet is already bound, so the deposit still goes through. The operator approves distributors with [Set Distributor Approval](/endpoints/vaults/set-distributor-approval). If the approval is withdrawn after the calldata is built, a deposit that would bind a new holder to your wallet reverts and nothing moves.

The `attribution` block states what this deposit does:

| `binding` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `BINDS_NOW` | The wallet is not bound yet. This deposit binds it to your instance's distributor wallet |
| `ALREADY_BOUND` | The wallet is already bound to your instance |
| `BOUND_ELSEWHERE` | The wallet is bound to another distributor, named in `distributor`, which keeps the credit |
| `NOT_APPROVED` | The vault operator has not approved your instance's distributor wallet. The deposit names no distributor, so a wallet not yet bound stays unbound and its share goes to the vault operator |
| `UNATTRIBUTED` | Your instance has no verified wallet, so the deposit names no distributor |
| `null` | `holderAddress` was omitted, so the binding could not be read. When the deposit names your wallet, a wallet not yet bound binds to it and a bound one keeps its distributor |

The binding follows the wallet that signs the deposit, not `holderAddress`. Name the signing wallet, or the checks and the `binding` reading describe the wrong wallet.

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/vaults/cmssh8t2u0003cghxkx1w1fwd/deposit" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"amount": "1000", "holderAddress": "0x6a6cba16dad34f7ea7ecbcdbf050f8146b942d6b"}'
```
```typescript TypeScript
const base = 'https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/vaults';
const headers = { 'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here', 'Content-Type': 'application/json' };

const res = await fetch(`${base}/${vaultId}/deposit`, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ amount: '1000', holderAddress: await signer.getAddress() })
});
const { data } = await res.json();
if (data.attribution.warning) console.warn(data.attribution.warning);

let depositHash = '';
for (const [index, step] of data.steps.entries()) {
  const tx = await signer.sendTransaction(step.transaction);
  await tx.wait();
  if (index === data.confirmStepIndex) depositHash = tx.hash;
}

await fetch(`${base}/${vaultId}/confirm-tx`, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ txHash: depositHash, txType: 'DEPOSIT' })
});
```
</RequestExample>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `amount` is missing, not a positive decimal string, finer than the asset's decimals, or too large. `error.details` names the field |
| `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` | `400` | `holderAddress` holds less of the asset than `amount`, so the deposit would revert after the approval. `details` carries `holder`, `balance` and `required` |
| `DEPOSITOR_NOT_VERIFIED` | `403` | `holderAddress` is not verified on the vault's identity register, on the register the vault adopted, or on the provider register of a market the vault funds. The message names the register, the market where a market's register refused, and your verification profile when the register is one of your profiles' eligibility registers. Show it verbatim |
| `IDENTITY_GATE_RETIRED` | `409` | Returned instead of `DEPOSITOR_NOT_VERIFIED` when the register that refuses `holderAddress` is the previous Trusset ID Register, which takes no new identities. `details` carries `register`, `gate`, `retired: true` and `currentRegistry`. With `gate: vault`, the vault's own register or the register it adopted refused: a vault keeps its register for life, so deposit through a vault gated on the current register. With `gate: provider`, the provider gate of a market the vault funds refused, named in `details.marketAddress`: that market's admin moves the gate to the current register with [Set Identity Gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#the-previous-trusset-id-register), then retry |
| `VAULT_NOT_PUBLISHED` | `403` | The vault is unpublished and your instance sees it only as a distributor of record. It takes deposits only through its operator's instance until published. Redemptions stay open |
| `VAULT_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No such vault, or it is not visible to your instance |
| `ELIGIBILITY_REMOVAL_PENDING` | `409` | The admission of `holderAddress` to one of the registers the vault examines it against waits for removal, so no new deposit is prepared for it. `details` carries `register` and `wallet`, plus `reason` and `profileKey` when the admission is your instance's |
| `DEPOSITOR_GATE_REQUIRED` | `409` | No register would examine the depositor: the vault has no register of its own, has adopted none and funds no market with a provider register, or it runs an earlier implementation without a register of its own |
| `DEPOSITS_PAUSED` | `409` | The operator paused deposits. Redemptions stay open |
| `VAULT_NOT_DEPLOYED` | `409` | The vault has no on-chain address on record |
| `VAULT_BOOK_INCOMPLETE` | `409` | A market the vault funds is not answering, so its book cannot be totalled |
| `VAULT_WIPED_OUT` | `409` | Shares are in issue against zero assets |
| `VAULT_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The vault implementation, its markets, the register it adopted, whether it admits your distributor wallet, whether a market wrote off accrual the vault has not recorded, or your standing in an unpublished vault could not be read. Retry shortly |
| `STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The admissions behind a register could not be checked for a pending removal. Retry shortly |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The chain could not be read, so nothing was decided. Retry shortly |
| `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The vault records or your instance's verified wallets could not be read. Retry shortly |
| `VAULT_DEPOSIT_FAILED` | `500` | Unexpected failure. Retry, or contact support with the request ID |
