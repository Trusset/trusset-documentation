---
title: 'Archive Customer'
api: 'DELETE /customers/api/manage/{customerId}'
description: 'Soft-delete a customer by marking it as archived'
---

Archives a customer record. Archived customers are excluded from list queries by default but can be included by passing `includeArchived=true`. This is a soft delete: no data is permanently removed.

<Warning>
  Archiving changes the record only. The identity on the registry and its claims stay in place, so a verified wallet keeps passing identity checks. If it should stop, revoke it with [Revoke Identity](/endpoints/customers/revoke-identity) before archiving, and do the same for any linked wallet the registry holds an identity for.
</Warning>

An archived record answers `404` on [Get Customer](/endpoints/customers/get), [Update Customer](/endpoints/customers/update), the wallet endpoints and [Link Wallet](/endpoints/customers/link-wallet), and drops out of [Batch Get Countries](/endpoints/customers/batch-countries). [Get Lending Positions](/endpoints/customers/get-lending-positions) still reads it, and loans already attributed to it stay attributed. A new loan cannot name it as its `clientReference`.

Its linked wallets stay attached and stay reserved, so no other customer on your instance can add them. Its `externalId` is released for a new record.

There is no restore endpoint on the API. [Create Customer](/endpoints/customers/create) with the archived record's primary wallet restores the record and overwrites its fields from the request body.

A company record is refused while individuals that are not archived are linked to it. Unlink them first by sending `companyId: null` to Update Customer, or archive them.

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No such customer on your instance, or the record is already archived |
| `COMPANY_HAS_MEMBERS` | `409` | The record is a company with individuals linked to it that are not archived |
| `ARCHIVE_FAILED` | `500` | The record could not be archived |
