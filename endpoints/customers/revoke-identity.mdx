---
title: 'Revoke Identity'
api: 'POST /customers/api/identity/revoke'
description: 'Revoke a wallet on the identity register and mark the customer revoked'
---

Revokes a wallet's identity on the register your instance writes to, and marks the customer record `revoked`. Use it when a customer fails ongoing compliance checks or their verification must be invalidated.

<Warning>
  On an ERC-3643 register, revoking deletes the wallet's entry from the register and nothing else. The ONCHAINID and its KYC claim stay as they are. To invalidate the claim as well, call [Revoke Claim](/endpoints/customers/revoke-claim) first. Once the entry is deleted, the claim can no longer be found through the wallet.

  A T-REX 5 register works the other way round: revoking revokes the KYC claim and leaves the register entry. See [On a T-REX 5 register](#on-a-t-rex-5-register).
</Warning>

Both registers answer with one transaction. What it does depends on `registerKind`:

| `registerKind` | Transaction | Effect | Who sends it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `TRUSSET` | `revokeIdentity` on the register | The identity stays on the register and reads as `revoked` | A wallet with `REGISTER_OPERATOR_ROLE` |
| `TREX` | `deleteIdentity` on the register | The wallet's entry is removed from the register | An agent of the register |
| `TREX`, `trexVersion: 5` | `revokeClaimByDigest(digest)` on the trusted issuer identity that signed the wallet's KYC claim | The KYC claim is revoked, so the wallet stops counting as verified on every register that trusts this issuer. Verifying again signs a new claim | A MANAGEMENT key (purpose 1) of the trusted issuer identity |

Call it once without `txHash` to get the calldata, and again with `txHash` once the transaction is mined. The record is updated on the second call.

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="walletAddress" type="string" required>
  The wallet to revoke. Checksummed or lowercase.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signerAddress" type="string">
  ERC-3643 only. Returned as `signerAddress`, in place of your primary wallet. The API does not check it.

  On a T-REX 5 register it is checked: it must hold a MANAGEMENT key on the issuer identity of the claim, or the call answers `403 SIGNER_ROLE_MISSING`, and the message names the wallets that hold one. Omitted, the first of your instance's wallets that holds one is returned.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  The hash of the mined transaction. Send it to confirm the revocation and update the record. Omit it to receive the calldata.
</ParamField>

## Before the calldata

On a Trusset register, the API refuses with `REGISTER_OPERATOR_ROLE_MISSING` when it can read that none of your instance's wallets holds the role. Grant it with **Grant on-chain roles** on the wallet page of the issuer app (see [Customers](/endpoints/customers/introduction)). On the Trusset ID Register, which every instance without a register of its own shares, it refuses with `IDENTITY_HELD_BY_ANOTHER_ISSUER` when the identity was registered by a wallet that is not one of your instance's, and with `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE` when the register cannot be read to tell. A wallet with no active identity there is refused with `409 IDENTITY_NOT_REGISTERED`, because revoking it would revert on chain.

On an ERC-3643 register, it refuses with `REGISTRY_AGENT_MISSING` when none of your instance's wallets is an agent. It refuses with `NOT_REGISTERED` when the wallet is not on the register.

### On a T-REX 5 register

A T-REX 5 register never sees `deleteIdentity` from this endpoint, because deleting an identity there bars the wallet for good. The call revokes the wallet's KYC claim instead, with one `revokeClaimByDigest` step on the trusted issuer identity that signed it, signed by a MANAGEMENT key of that issuer identity. The ONCHAINID and the register entry stay, and [Verify Identity](/endpoints/customers/verify-identity) signs a new claim to verify the wallet again.

Besides `REGISTRY_AGENT_MISSING`, the build refuses with `NOT_REGISTERED` when the wallet carries no KYC claim from a trusted issuer of the register, `ALREADY_REVOKED` when that claim is revoked already, `CLAIM_ISSUER_UNREADABLE` when the issuer does not answer as an ONCHAINID 3 identity, so the claim's digest cannot be computed, and `CLAIM_MANAGER_MISSING` when none of your instance's wallets holds a MANAGEMENT key on the issuer identity. The confirm leg accepts a `revokeClaimByDigest` on a trusted issuer of the register, sent directly or through a smart-account wallet, whose digest is a claim of this wallet.

## Confirm

The confirm leg checks that the transaction is mined, succeeded, and calls `revokeIdentity` or `deleteIdentity` on the register for the wallet you named. A receipt for a different wallet is refused with `TX_TARGET_MISMATCH`, and nothing changes. On the Trusset ID Register the transaction must be signed by a verified wallet of your instance, or the confirm is refused with `UNAUTHORIZED_SIGNER`. The revocation is recorded once in your instance's verification history as a `REVOKE` entry with the transaction and its signer.

It then sets the customer record for that wallet to `revoked` with `revokedAt`, archived or not. When no record exists, nothing is written and the call still succeeds.

Revoking on your instance register does not take the wallet off an eligibility register. The confirm queues that removal, with reason `REVOKED`, from every eligibility register the wallet is on, and cancels its admissions still waiting. Until your wallet signs the removal, lending markets and vaults that read the eligibility register still admit the wallet. See [Eligibility registers](/endpoints/customers/introduction#eligibility-registers).

```typescript TypeScript
const url = 'https://api.trusset.org/customers/api/identity/revoke';

await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ walletAddress, txHash: tx.hash })

```

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `INVALID_ADDRESS` | `400` | `walletAddress` is missing or not a valid address |
| `REGISTER_OPERATOR_ROLE_MISSING` | `403` | Trusset register: none of your instance's wallets holds `REGISTER_OPERATOR_ROLE` |
| `IDENTITY_HELD_BY_ANOTHER_ISSUER` | `403` | Trusset ID Register: the identity was registered by a wallet that is not one of your instance's |
| `IDENTITY_NOT_REGISTERED` | `409` | Trusset ID Register: the wallet holds no active identity, so there is nothing to revoke. Calldata leg only |
| `UNAUTHORIZED_SIGNER` | `403` | Trusset ID Register, confirm leg: the transaction was not signed by a verified wallet of your instance |
| `REGISTRY_AGENT_MISSING` | `403` | ERC-3643: none of your instance's wallets is an agent of the register |
| `NOT_REGISTERED` | `409` | ERC-3643: the wallet is not on the register. T-REX 5: the wallet carries no KYC claim from a trusted issuer of the register |
| `ALREADY_REVOKED` | `409` | T-REX 5: the wallet's KYC claim is already revoked |
| `CLAIM_ISSUER_UNREADABLE` | `409` | T-REX 5: the claim issuer does not answer as an ONCHAINID 3 identity, so its claim's digest cannot be revoked from here |
| `CLAIM_MANAGER_MISSING` | `403` | T-REX 5: none of your instance's wallets holds a MANAGEMENT key on the claim issuer identity |
| `SIGNER_ROLE_MISSING` | `403` | T-REX 5: `signerAddress` holds no MANAGEMENT key on the claim issuer identity. The message names the wallets that do |
| `OWN_REGISTRY_NOT_CONFIGURED` | `409` | The instance is bound to its own register but no valid register address is set |
| `REGISTRY_PROBE_FAILED` | `503` | Your own register could not be read to tell its kind |
| `INVALID_TX` | `400` | `txHash` is not 32-byte hex |
| `TX_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | The transaction is not mined yet |
| `TX_REVERTED` | `400` | The transaction reverted |
| `TX_WRONG_TARGET` | `400` | The transaction does not write to the register |
| `TX_WRONG_FUNCTION` | `400` | The transaction does not call `revokeIdentity` or `deleteIdentity` |
| `TX_TARGET_MISMATCH` | `400` | The transaction revokes a different wallet |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | ERC-3643: the transaction could not be read. Trusset ID Register: the identity could not be read to check who registered it. Retry |
| `REVOKE_FAILED` | `400`, `500` | Any other failure. `400` when a chain call failed |
