---
title: 'Revoke Identity'
api: 'POST /customers/api/identity/revoke'
description: 'Revoke a wallet on the identity register and mark the customer revoked'
---

Revokes a wallet's identity on the register your instance writes to, and marks the customer record `revoked`. Use it when a customer fails ongoing compliance checks or their verification must be invalidated.

<Warning>
  On an ERC-3643 register, revoking deletes the wallet's entry from the register and nothing else. The ONCHAINID and its KYC claim stay as they are. To invalidate the claim as well, call [Revoke Claim](/endpoints/customers/revoke-claim) first. Once the entry is deleted, the claim can no longer be found through the wallet.

  A T-REX 5 register works the other way round: revoking revokes the KYC claim and leaves the register entry. See [On a T-REX 5 register](#on-a-t-rex-5-register).
</Warning>

<Warning>
  On the instance ID register, revoking withdraws only the attestation of the wallet that signs. When another trusted attester of your register still vouches for the wallet, it stays verified, and the confirm says so in `warnings`. A register admin or compliance officer freezes the wallet in the issuer app to stop it for your whole instance. See [On the instance ID register](#on-the-instance-id-register).
</Warning>

Every register answers with one transaction. What it does depends on `registerKind`:

| `registerKind` | Transaction | Effect | Who sends it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `INSTANCE` | `revoke` on the identity hub | The signing attester's own attestation reads as revoked. The register stops counting it | The trusted attester whose attestation it is |
| `TRUSSET` | `revokeIdentity` on your Trusset-compatible register | The identity stays on the register and reads as `revoked` | A wallet with `REGISTER_OPERATOR_ROLE` |
| `TREX` | `deleteIdentity` on the register | The wallet's entry is removed from the register | An agent of the register |
| `TREX`, `trexVersion: 5` | `revokeClaimByDigest(digest)` on the trusted issuer identity that signed the wallet's KYC claim | The KYC claim is revoked, so the wallet stops counting as verified on every register that trusts this issuer. Verifying again signs a new claim | A MANAGEMENT key (purpose 1) of the trusted issuer identity |

Call it once without `txHash` to get the calldata, and again with `txHash` once the transaction is mined. The record is updated on the second call.

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="walletAddress" type="string" required>
  The wallet to revoke. Checksummed or lowercase.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signerAddress" type="string">
  On the instance ID register, the wallet you will sign with. When it holds no active or suspended attestation about the wallet, the call answers `409 NOTHING_TO_REVOKE`, because its revocation would revert. Omitted, the calldata is built without that check.

  On an ERC-3643 register, returned as `signerAddress`, in place of your primary wallet. The API does not check it.

  On a T-REX 5 register it is checked: it must hold a MANAGEMENT key on the issuer identity of the claim, or the call answers `403 SIGNER_ROLE_MISSING`, and the message names the wallets that hold one. Omitted, the first of your instance's wallets that holds one is returned.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  The hash of the mined transaction. Send it to confirm the revocation and update the record. Omit it to receive the calldata.
</ParamField>

## Before the calldata

### On the instance ID register

The API refuses with `409 INSTANCE_REGISTER_REQUIRED` when your instance has not created its register yet, and with `403 ATTESTER_MISSING` when it can read that the register trusts none of your instance's registered wallets as an attester. A register admin trusts a wallet on the wallet page of the issuer app. See [Attesters and trust](/endpoints/customers/introduction#attesters-and-trust).

It reads the register first. A wallet the register reports no unrevoked attestation for is refused with `409 IDENTITY_NOT_REGISTERED`, and a register that cannot be read with `503 CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE`. With `signerAddress`, a signer that holds no active or suspended attestation about the wallet is refused with `409 NOTHING_TO_REVOKE`.

The calldata calls `revoke` on the identity hub. The hub lets a wallet revoke only its own attestation, so send it from the attester that wrote the one you want to withdraw. Attestations other trusted attesters wrote about the same wallet stay as they are.

### On a Trusset-compatible register of your own

The API refuses with `REGISTER_OPERATOR_ROLE_MISSING` when it can read that none of your instance's wallets holds the role. Your register's owner grants it.

### On an ERC-3643 register

The API refuses with `REGISTRY_AGENT_MISSING` when none of your instance's wallets is an agent. It refuses with `NOT_REGISTERED` when the wallet is not on the register.

### On a T-REX 5 register

A T-REX 5 register never sees `deleteIdentity` from this endpoint, because deleting an identity there bars the wallet for good. The call revokes the wallet's KYC claim instead, with one `revokeClaimByDigest` step on the trusted issuer identity that signed it, signed by a MANAGEMENT key of that issuer identity. The ONCHAINID and the register entry stay, and [Verify Identity](/endpoints/customers/verify-identity) signs a new claim to verify the wallet again.

Besides `REGISTRY_AGENT_MISSING`, the build refuses with `NOT_REGISTERED` when the wallet carries no KYC claim from a trusted issuer of the register, `ALREADY_REVOKED` when that claim is revoked already, `CLAIM_ISSUER_UNREADABLE` when the issuer does not answer as an ONCHAINID 3 identity, so the claim's digest cannot be computed, and `CLAIM_MANAGER_MISSING` when none of your instance's wallets holds a MANAGEMENT key on the issuer identity. The confirm leg accepts a `revokeClaimByDigest` on a trusted issuer of the register, sent directly or through a smart-account wallet, whose digest is a claim of this wallet.

## Confirm

The confirm leg checks that the transaction is mined, succeeded, and revokes the wallet you named: `revoke` on the identity hub for the instance ID register, `revokeIdentity` or `deleteIdentity` on any other register. A receipt for a different wallet is refused with `TX_TARGET_MISMATCH`, and nothing changes. On the instance ID register a `revokeBatch` is refused with `TX_WRONG_FUNCTION`, and a revocation signed by a wallet the register does not trust as an attester with `403 UNTRUSTED_ATTESTER`. On a Trusset-compatible register of your own the signer is logged, not checked. The revocation is recorded once in your instance's verification history as a `REVOKE` entry with the transaction and its signer.

It then sets the customer record for that wallet to `revoked` with `revokedAt`, archived or not. When no record exists, nothing is written and the call still succeeds.

On the instance ID register the confirm then asks the register whether the wallet is still verified. When another trusted attester still vouches for it, the answer carries a `STILL_VERIFIED_BY_ANOTHER_ATTESTER` warning naming that attester. The customer record is set to `revoked` all the same, but tokens, markets and vaults that read the register or its [profile gates](/endpoints/customers/introduction#profile-gates) keep admitting the wallet until it is frozen on the register or the other attester revokes too.

Revoking on your own ERC-3643 register does not take the wallet off an eligibility register. The confirm queues that removal, with reason `REVOKED`, from every eligibility register the wallet is on, and cancels its admissions still waiting. Until your wallet signs the removal, lending markets and vaults that read the eligibility register still admit the wallet. See [Eligibility registers](/endpoints/customers/introduction#eligibility-registers). A profile gate on the instance ID register reads the attestations directly, so it needs no removal.

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `INVALID_ADDRESS` | `400` | `walletAddress` is missing or not a valid address |
| `INSTANCE_REGISTER_REQUIRED` | `409` | Your instance is in Trusset mode and has not created its instance ID register yet |
| `ATTESTER_MISSING` | `403` | Instance ID register: none of your instance's registered wallets is a trusted attester of the register |
| `IDENTITY_NOT_REGISTERED` | `409` | Instance ID register: the register reports no unrevoked identity for the wallet, so there is nothing to revoke. Calldata leg only |
| `NOTHING_TO_REVOKE` | `409` | Instance ID register: `signerAddress` holds no active or suspended attestation about the wallet, so its revocation would revert. Calldata leg only |
| `IDENTITY_HUB_NOT_CONFIGURED` | `501` | The identity hub is not deployed on your instance's network |
| `UNTRUSTED_ATTESTER` | `403` | Instance ID register, confirm leg: the revocation was signed by a wallet the register does not trust as an attester |
| `REGISTER_OPERATOR_ROLE_MISSING` | `403` | Trusset-compatible register of your own: none of your instance's wallets holds `REGISTER_OPERATOR_ROLE` |
| `REGISTRY_AGENT_MISSING` | `403` | ERC-3643: none of your instance's wallets is an agent of the register |
| `NOT_REGISTERED` | `409` | ERC-3643: the wallet is not on the register. T-REX 5: the wallet carries no KYC claim from a trusted issuer of the register |
| `ALREADY_REVOKED` | `409` | T-REX 5: the wallet's KYC claim is already revoked |
| `CLAIM_ISSUER_UNREADABLE` | `409` | T-REX 5: the claim issuer does not answer as an ONCHAINID 3 identity, so its claim's digest cannot be revoked from here |
| `CLAIM_MANAGER_MISSING` | `403` | T-REX 5: none of your instance's wallets holds a MANAGEMENT key on the claim issuer identity |
| `SIGNER_ROLE_MISSING` | `403` | T-REX 5: `signerAddress` holds no MANAGEMENT key on the claim issuer identity. The message names the wallets that do |
| `OWN_REGISTRY_NOT_CONFIGURED` | `409` | The instance is bound to its own register but no valid register address is set |
| `REGISTRY_PROBE_FAILED` | `503` | Your own register could not be read to tell its kind |
| `INVALID_TX` | `400` | `txHash` is not 32-byte hex |
| `TX_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | The transaction is not mined yet |
| `TX_REVERTED` | `400` | The transaction reverted |
| `TX_WRONG_TARGET` | `400` | The transaction does not write to the identity hub or the register |
| `TX_WRONG_FUNCTION` | `400` | The transaction does not call `revoke` on the hub, or `revokeIdentity` or `deleteIdentity` on the register |
| `TX_TARGET_MISMATCH` | `400` | The transaction revokes a different wallet |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | ERC-3643: the transaction could not be read. Instance ID register: the register could not be read to check the wallet. Retry |
| `REVOKE_FAILED` | `400`, `500` | Any other failure. `400` when a chain call failed |
