---
title: 'Open Disclosure'
api: 'POST /customers/api/disclosure-requests/{id}/open'
description: 'Prove that a disclosure was decrypted, so Trusset deletes its ciphertext'
---

Acknowledges that you decrypted and stored a disclosure. The proof is `openProof`, a random value the holder's browser placed inside the plaintext. Trusset holds only its SHA-256 hash, the header's `openCommitment`, so only someone who decrypted the envelope can send the matching value.

Your [local tool](/tools/kyc-courier/overview) calls this once every check below has passed and the result is on your disk. A disclosure that fails a check is refused with [Reject Disclosure](/endpoints/customers/reject-disclosure) instead. You call this yourself only when you open disclosures with your own implementation.

<Warning>
  When the proof matches, Trusset deletes the ciphertext and `envelope.status` becomes `OPENED`. The envelope cannot be downloaded again, and getting the facts again takes a new request. Store the decrypted disclosure first and acknowledge second, as the tool does.
</Warning>

## What your tool checks first

Before it acknowledges, the tool works through the envelope from [Download Disclosure Envelope](/endpoints/customers/get-disclosure-envelope) in this order:

1. The header decodes, and its `requestId`, `walletAddress`, `recipientKeyId`, `scope` and `predicates` equal the request.
2. It recomputes `envelopeDigest`, rebuilds the consent typed data from the request with that digest, and checks that the stored signature recovers to the request wallet.
3. It decrypts with the record key whose ID is `recipientKeyId`. A failure is `UNDECRYPTABLE`.
4. The SHA-256 of the plaintext equals `payloadDigest`, and the SHA-256 of `openProof` equals `openCommitment`. Otherwise `DIGEST_MISMATCH`.
5. The plaintext parses strictly, with the right `version`, `requestId` and `wallet`.
6. Each disclosure is version `3.1.0` and names the request wallet, and passes `verify_disclosure` of the [KYC STARK tool](/tools/kyc-starks/verification#a-single-disclosed-leaf) 3.1 under the operator key the identity hub publishes for its attester and key ID. The wallet's attestation by that attester on the hub must have the disclosure's root. A failure is `PROOF_INVALID`.
7. The disclosures answer the scope, as set out on [Create Disclosure Request](/endpoints/customers/create-disclosure-request#scopes). Otherwise `INCOMPLETE`.

Only then does it store the result and send `openProof`. What you hold after that is the value each proof shows, such as a date of birth or a country, under your own data protection duties.

## What opening does

- `envelope.status` becomes `OPENED`, `held` becomes `false`, and `openedAt` and `purgedAt` are set.
- Trusset deletes `enc` and the ciphertext. The header, the digests, the size, the consent and the dates stay.
- The opening is written to the customer audit log as `DISCLOSURE_OPENED`, with `envelopeDigest`.

Sending the proof again for an envelope that is already `OPENED` changes nothing and answers with the request, so a retry after a lost response is safe. Nothing is written on chain, no customer record changes, and no webhook is sent.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
  The request `id`. Letters and digits only, at most 100 characters.
</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="openProof" type="string" required>
  The `openProof` field of the decrypted plaintext: 32 bytes as 64 lowercase hex characters.
</ParamField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `id` contains characters other than letters and digits, or is longer than 100 characters |
| `OPEN_PROOF_MISMATCH` | `400` | `openProof` is missing, is not 64 lowercase hex characters, or does not hash to the header's `openCommitment`. Checked before the envelope's status |
| `NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No request with this ID was made by your instance |
| `ENVELOPE_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | The request carries no envelope |
| `ENVELOPE_NOT_OPENABLE` | `409` | The envelope is `REJECTED` or `UNCLAIMED`. `details.envelopeStatus` is its status |
| `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` | `429` | Your instance went over its budget of 120 disclosure request changes per minute |
| `OPEN_DISCLOSURE_FAILED` | `500` | The opening could not be recorded |
