---
title: 'Bind Wallet'
api: 'POST /customers/api/onboarding/{customer}/wallet'
description: 'Give the onboarded customer the wallet they will hold their identity with'
---

Binds a wallet to the customer and to the onboarding. Call it when the customer connects a wallet in your app, before or after the review. A wallet sent with [Start Onboarding](/endpoints/customers/start-onboarding) is bound the same way.

What the binding does depends on where the onboarding stands:

| Onboarding | Effect |
|---|---|
| `TRUSSET`, check still open | The wallet is recorded. An approval later finishes the onboarding with it |
| `TRUSSET`, `AWAITING_WALLET` | The registration is queued now and the status turns `AWAITING_SIGNATURE`. When your register already verifies the wallet, it turns `COMPLETED` |
| `KYC_SOURCE`, `AWAITING_WALLET` | The status turns `AWAITING_RECORD`. A record your source already delivered for the wallet is picked up at once |

A customer holds one primary wallet. Binding the wallet the customer already holds is accepted and changes nothing. The register is read once to see whether your instance already verified the wallet, and nothing is written on chain.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="customer" type="string" required>
  The customer `id`, your `externalId` or the reference key.
</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="walletAddress" type="string" required>
  `0x` followed by 40 hex characters. Stored and returned checksummed.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  The onboarding after the binding. [Get Onboarding](/endpoints/customers/get-onboarding#response-fields) describes every field.
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://api.trusset.org/customers/api/onboarding/client-48213/wallet" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here" \
  -d '{ "walletAddress": "0x96C5822784130360F904372b83E130be4A1894D6" }'
```
```typescript TypeScript
const response = await fetch(
  'https://api.trusset.org/customers/api/onboarding/client-48213/wallet',
```

</RequestExample>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `INVALID_ADDRESS` | `400` | `walletAddress` is missing, malformed or the zero address |
| `COUNTRY_BLOCKED` | `403` | The profile blocks the customer's country, so nothing was bound or queued |
| `CUSTOMER_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No customer of your instance matches `customer` |
| `CUSTOMER_WALLET_MISMATCH` | `409` | The customer already holds another wallet. Change it on the customer record first |
| `WALLET_MISMATCH` | `409` | The identity check is bound to another wallet |
| `WALLET_IN_USE` | `409` | The wallet belongs to another customer of your instance, an archived one included, as its primary or as a linked wallet |
| `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` | `429` | More than 120 onboarding writes in the minute |
| `ONBOARDING_WALLET_FAILED` | `500` | The binding failed on the server |
