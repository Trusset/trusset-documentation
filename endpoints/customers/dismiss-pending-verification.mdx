---
title: 'Dismiss Pending Verification'
api: 'POST /customers/api/identity/pending/{id}/dismiss'
description: 'Close a queued KYC approval without registering it'
---

<Warning>
  A dismissal cannot be undone, and no endpoint restores the row. [Build Pending Registration](/endpoints/customers/build-pending-registration) and [Confirm Pending Registration](/endpoints/customers/confirm-pending-registration) act only on `PENDING` rows. The approval's `kycHash`, expiries and residency claim therefore never reach the register from the queue.

  Verifying the wallet later with [Verify Identity](/endpoints/customers/verify-identity) or [Batch Verify](/endpoints/customers/batch-verify) settles only `PENDING` rows. The follow-ups of a confirmed row therefore never run for this one. Its hosted verification checkout does not turn `VERIFIED`, stock escrow deposits waiting on its ID link are not bound to the wallet, and the customer is not emailed.

  A new KYC approval for the customer queues a fresh row. The dismissed one stays as it is.
</Warning>

Closes one queued approval from [List Pending Verifications](/endpoints/customers/list-pending-verifications) without registering it. Use it for an approval you have decided not to put on chain. The row turns `DISMISSED` and stays listable under that status.

Nothing else changes. No transaction is built, nothing on chain moves, and the customer record keeps its status. A `REGISTER_SWITCH` row's customer therefore still reads `verified`, although nothing put the wallet on your new register.

The dismissal is written to the customer audit log as `ON_CHAIN_VERIFICATION_DISMISSED`, with the row `id` and its `source`.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="id" type="string" required>
  The row `id` from [List Pending Verifications](/endpoints/customers/list-pending-verifications). The row must still be `PENDING`.
</ParamField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No `PENDING` row with this `id` on your instance, including one already confirmed or dismissed, or the instance no longer exists |
| `PENDING_DISMISS_FAILED` | `500` | The row could not be updated |
