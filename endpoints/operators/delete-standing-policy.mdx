---
title: 'Delete Standing Policy'
api: 'DELETE /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/standing-policies/{policyId}'
description: 'Remove a standing policy for good'
---

Deletes one standing policy and returns the record as it was. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone. To stop a policy from matching markets while keeping it, switch it off with `enabled: false` on [Update Standing Policy](/endpoints/operators/update-standing-policy) instead, which is the reversible pause.

Deleting a policy changes nothing on chain and nothing on a market already adopted under it. Its template stays in the [terms library](/endpoints/lending/list-terms-templates), and a market that published it keeps its terms of use.

Only an account Trusset approved as a lender of record can write policies. Any other account is refused with `403 CURATOR_NOT_APPROVED`.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="policyId" type="string" required>
  Policy ID from [List Standing Policies](/endpoints/operators/list-standing-policies), 1 to 100 characters.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="policy" type="object">The deleted policy as it was stored, with the fields of a row on [List Standing Policies](/endpoints/operators/list-standing-policies#response-fields) except `template`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `policyId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `STANDING_POLICY_DELETE_FAILED` | `400` | The policy could not be deleted for a reason that carries no code of its own |
| `CURATOR_NOT_APPROVED` | `403` | Trusset has not approved this account to act as a lender of record |
| `STANDING_POLICY_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No policy with this ID exists on this instance, also when another request removed it while this one ran |
| `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` | `429` | More than 10 writes in the minute |
