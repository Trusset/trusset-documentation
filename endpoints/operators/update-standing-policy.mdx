---
title: 'Update Standing Policy'
api: 'PUT /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/standing-policies/{policyId}'
description: 'Change a standing policy, bind another template, or switch it off and on'
---

Changes one standing policy. Send only the fields to change: a field you leave out keeps its value. This is a database write: nothing is signed and nothing goes on chain. See [List Standing Policies](/endpoints/operators/list-standing-policies) for what a policy does.

Switching a policy off with `enabled: false` is the reversible pause: the policy stays stored and matches no market until it is switched on again. [Delete Standing Policy](/endpoints/operators/delete-standing-policy) removes it for good.

Only an account Trusset approved as a lender of record can write policies. Any other account is refused with `403 CURATOR_NOT_APPROVED` before the body is read.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="policyId" type="string" required>
  Policy ID from [List Standing Policies](/endpoints/operators/list-standing-policies), 1 to 100 characters.
</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="kind" type="string">
  `DEPLOYER` or `COLLATERAL_TOKEN`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="address" type="string">
  The deployer wallet or collateral token, `0x` followed by 40 hex characters, not the zero address. Stored lowercased. When another policy of this instance already names the same `kind` and `address`, the change is refused with `409 STANDING_POLICY_EXISTS`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="label" type="string | null">
  At most 120 characters. `null` or a blank label clears it.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="termsTemplateId" type="string | null">
  Binds another template of this instance's [terms library](/endpoints/lending/list-terms-templates), 1 to 100 characters. `null` binds none. An archived template is refused with `409 TEMPLATE_ARCHIVED`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="acceptanceTerms" type="object | null">
  Replaces the whole object: keys you leave out are removed, not kept. `null` clears it. The keys and their rules are those of [Create Standing Policy](/endpoints/operators/create-standing-policy#body-parameters), and any other key is refused with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="agreementPolicy" type="object | null">
  Replaces the loan agreement policy, with the rules of [Create Standing Policy](/endpoints/operators/create-standing-policy#body-parameters). `null` clears it. A change stamps `agreementPolicySetAt`; markets adopted before that keep what they have.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="enabled" type="boolean">
  `false` switches the policy off, `true` switches it back on. A policy whose bound template has been archived cannot be switched on: the request is refused with `409 TEMPLATE_ARCHIVED`, because a market adopted under it would go live without terms of use. Bind another template, or `null`, in the same request.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="policy" type="object">The policy after the change, with the fields of a row on [List Standing Policies](/endpoints/operators/list-standing-policies#response-fields) except `template`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `policyId` or a field breaks its type, format or length rule, or `acceptanceTerms` carries a key it does not take |
| `INVALID_STANDING_POLICY` | `400` | `address` or an address in `acceptanceTerms` is the zero address |
| `STANDING_POLICY_SAVE_FAILED` | `400` | The policy could not be saved for a reason that carries no code of its own |
| `CURATOR_NOT_APPROVED` | `403` | Trusset has not approved this account to act as a lender of record |
| `STANDING_POLICY_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No policy with this ID exists on this instance, also when another request removed it while this one ran |
| `TEMPLATE_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | `termsTemplateId` names no template of this instance |
| `STANDING_POLICY_EXISTS` | `409` | Another policy of this instance already names this `kind` and `address` |
| `TEMPLATE_ARCHIVED` | `409` | `termsTemplateId` names an archived template, or `enabled: true` switches on a policy whose template is archived without binding another in the same request |
| `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` | `429` | More than 10 writes in the minute |
