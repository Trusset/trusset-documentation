---
title: 'List Standing Policies'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/standing-policies'
description: 'List the standing lender-of-record policies of this instance'
---

Returns this instance's standing policies. A standing policy says in advance how this institution takes markets of one deployer, or of one collateral token, as lender of record. It never signs anything: taking the role is always one transaction signed by the lender's own wallet through [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role), so every answer carries `signatureStillRequired: true`.

A market matches a policy on its exact deployer wallet (`kind: DEPLOYER`) or its exact collateral token (`kind: COLLATERAL_TOKEN`). An enabled `DEPLOYER` policy wins over an enabled `COLLATERAL_TOKEN` policy, and a policy that is switched off never matches. A match does three things:

- The market carries the policy in `standingPolicy` on [List Queued Markets](/endpoints/operators/list-queued-markets) and [Get Queued Market](/endpoints/operators/get-queued-market).
- Its `acceptanceTerms` are what the issuer app pre-fills on the adoption form. A realization path applies only where the market's price source offers it: `EXCHANGE_SALE` only on a `MARKET`-priced market, `AUCTION` only on a market priced any other way.
- On [Confirm Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/confirm-lender-role), the template the policy binds is published as the market's terms of use while the market has none. See [Terms Library](/endpoints/lending/list-terms-templates).

Any key of the instance can read the policies. Writing them needs an account Trusset approved as a lender of record: see [Create Standing Policy](/endpoints/operators/create-standing-policy). Reads count against the 100 per minute budget and writes against the 10 per minute budget of [Rate limits](/endpoints/lending/introduction#rate-limits).

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="policies" type="array">
      Every policy of this instance, enabled ones first, newest first within each group.
      <Expandable>
        <ResponseField name="id" type="string">Policy ID. [Update Standing Policy](/endpoints/operators/update-standing-policy) and [Delete Standing Policy](/endpoints/operators/delete-standing-policy) take it.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="instanceId" type="string">The instance the policy belongs to.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="kind" type="string">`DEPLOYER` to match the wallet that deployed a market, `COLLATERAL_TOKEN` to match a market's collateral token.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="address" type="string">The deployer wallet or collateral token the policy matches, lowercased.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="label" type="string">Your name for the policy, or `null`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="termsTemplateId" type="string">The [terms library](/endpoints/lending/list-terms-templates) template published on a matching market at adoption, or `null`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="acceptanceTerms" type="object">
          The adoption values the issuer app pre-fills, or `null`. Every key is optional, and a key the policy does not set is absent. Addresses are lowercased.
          <Expandable>
            <ResponseField name="admin" type="string">The `admin` party of [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role#parties).</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="issuer" type="string">The `issuer` party.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="collateralAgent" type="string">The `collateralAgent` party.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="saleRecipient" type="string">The `saleRecipient` party.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="liquidationOperator" type="string">The `liquidationOperator` party.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="oracleOwner" type="string">The `oracleOwner` party.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="priceSigner" type="string">The `priceSigner` party.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="identityRegistry" type="string">The borrower register, as `identityRegistry` on Accept Lender Role.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="realizationMode" type="string">`EXCHANGE_SALE`, `ISSUER_REDEMPTION` or `AUCTION`.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="liquidityCommitment" type="string">Decimal amount of the borrow asset, with up to 18 decimals.</ResponseField>
            <ResponseField name="maxFixedRateBps" type="integer">Fixed-rate ceiling in basis points, 1 to 50000.</ResponseField>
          </Expandable>
        </ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="enabled" type="boolean">`false` while the policy is switched off. A switched-off policy matches no market.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="createdBy" type="string">The issuer account that created the policy, or `null`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="updatedBy" type="string">The issuer account that last changed it, or `null`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">ISO 8601.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string">ISO 8601.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="template" type="object">The bound template as `{ id, title, version, archived }`, or `null` when the policy binds none. `archived: true` means nothing will be published at adoption until another template is bound.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="isBank" type="boolean">Whether Trusset approved this account to act as a lender of record. Only such an account can write policies.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="signatureStillRequired" type="boolean">Always `true`. No policy takes a role on its own.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/standing-policies" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here"
```
```typescript TypeScript
const res = await fetch(
  'https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/standing-policies',
  { headers: { 'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here' } }
);
const { data } = await res.json();
const active = data.policies.filter((policy) => policy.enabled);
```
</RequestExample>
