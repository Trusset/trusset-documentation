---
title: 'Create Standing Policy'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/standing-policies'
description: 'Record how this institution takes markets of one deployer or one collateral token'
---

Creates a standing policy for one deployer wallet or one collateral token and answers `201`. This is a database write: nothing is signed and nothing goes on chain. What a policy does, and what it never does, is set out on [List Standing Policies](/endpoints/operators/list-standing-policies).

<Note>
  A policy never takes a role. Every market it matches is still adopted with one transaction signed by the lender's own wallet through [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role).
</Note>

Only an account Trusset approved as a lender of record can write policies. Any other account is refused with `403 CURATOR_NOT_APPROVED` before the body is read. An instance holds one policy per `kind` and `address`: a second one is refused with `409 STANDING_POLICY_EXISTS`, so change the existing one with [Update Standing Policy](/endpoints/operators/update-standing-policy).

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="kind" type="string" required>
  `DEPLOYER` to match markets deployed by `address`, or `COLLATERAL_TOKEN` to match markets whose collateral token is `address`. When a market matches an enabled policy of each kind, the `DEPLOYER` policy wins.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="address" type="string" required>
  The deployer wallet or collateral token, `0x` followed by 40 hex characters, not the zero address. Matched exactly and stored lowercased.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="label" type="string">
  Your name for the policy, at most 120 characters. Trimmed, and a blank label is stored as `null`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="termsTemplateId" type="string | null">
  A template of this instance's [terms library](/endpoints/lending/list-terms-templates), 1 to 100 characters. [Confirm Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/confirm-lender-role) publishes it as the terms of use of a market adopted under the policy while the market has none. An archived template is refused with `409 TEMPLATE_ARCHIVED`. Omit it or send `null` to bind none.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="acceptanceTerms" type="object | null">
  The adoption values the issuer app pre-fills for a matching market. Every key is optional. Any key not listed here is refused with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`. Leave a key out to leave it unset: `null` or an empty string as a value is refused with `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`. An empty object is stored as `null`.

  <Expandable>
    <ParamField body="admin" type="string">Address, not the zero address. The `admin` party of [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role#parties).</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="issuer" type="string">Address. The `issuer` party.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="collateralAgent" type="string">Address. The `collateralAgent` party.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="saleRecipient" type="string">Address. The `saleRecipient` party.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="liquidationOperator" type="string">Address. The `liquidationOperator` party.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="oracleOwner" type="string">Address. The `oracleOwner` party.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="priceSigner" type="string">Address. The `priceSigner` party.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="identityRegistry" type="string">Address. The borrower register, as `identityRegistry` on Accept Lender Role.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="realizationMode" type="string">`EXCHANGE_SALE`, `ISSUER_REDEMPTION` or `AUCTION`. It is pre-filled only where the market's price source offers it: `EXCHANGE_SALE` only on a `MARKET`-priced market, `AUCTION` only on a market priced any other way.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="liquidityCommitment" type="string">Decimal amount of the borrow asset as a string, with up to 18 decimals, for example `"250000"`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="maxFixedRateBps" type="integer">Fixed-rate ceiling in basis points, an integer from 1 to 50000.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="enabled" type="boolean" default="true">
  `false` creates the policy switched off. A switched-off policy matches no market.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="policy" type="object">The policy as written, with the fields of a row on [List Standing Policies](/endpoints/operators/list-standing-policies#response-fields) except `template`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

The queue reflects a write at once: [List Queued Markets](/endpoints/operators/list-queued-markets) is read fresh on the next call.

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | A field breaks its type, format or length rule, or `acceptanceTerms` carries a key not listed above |
| `INVALID_STANDING_POLICY` | `400` | `address` or an address in `acceptanceTerms` is the zero address |
| `STANDING_POLICY_SAVE_FAILED` | `400` | The policy could not be saved for a reason that carries no code of its own |
| `CURATOR_NOT_APPROVED` | `403` | Trusset has not approved this account to act as a lender of record |
| `TEMPLATE_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | `termsTemplateId` names no template of this instance |
| `STANDING_POLICY_EXISTS` | `409` | This instance already holds a policy of this `kind` for this `address`. Update that one instead |
| `TEMPLATE_ARCHIVED` | `409` | `termsTemplateId` names an archived template |
| `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` | `429` | More than 10 writes in the minute |
