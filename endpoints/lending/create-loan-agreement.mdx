---
title: 'Create Loan Agreement'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/loan-agreements'
description: 'Request a loan agreement as the borrower, or create one for a wallet as the bank'
---

Creates a loan agreement on a market whose policy is `OPTIONAL` or `REQUIRED`.

- **As the borrower side**, you request a `LOAN` agreement with the figures you want. It starts `REQUESTED` and the bank issues it, or it is issued at once when the policy has `autoIssue`, the figures are inside the market rules and the borrow amount is within `autoIssueMaxAmount`. On a market whose policy has `requestAccess: BANK_ONLY`, only the bank creates agreements and a request answers `403 AGREEMENT_REQUESTS_CLOSED`. The same request sent again while it waits returns the existing agreement with `created: false`.
- **As the bank**, you create a `LOAN` agreement for a wallet, or a `FRAMEWORK` agreement with a credit limit and an end date. It is issued at once: the schedule is frozen and the salt created. This needs the Scale plan.

The figures are checked against the market: the principal or limit at least the market minimum, the collateral within the per-borrower cap, and the principal within what the collateral supports at the current price. A new issued agreement of the same kind for the same wallet supersedes an earlier open one.

The schedule names no person. Send no names and no file: the body is checked for both.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>
  The market's record ID on your instance.
</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="borrowerWallet" type="string" required>
  The wallet that will borrow.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="kind" type="string">
  `LOAN` (default) or `FRAMEWORK`. Only the bank creates a `FRAMEWORK` agreement.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="collateralAmount" type="string">
  `LOAN` only, required. Collateral units as a decimal string.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="borrowAmount" type="string">
  `LOAN` only, required. The principal in the settlement asset, as a decimal string.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="limit" type="string">
  `FRAMEWORK` only, required. The credit limit in the settlement asset.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="validUntil" type="string">
  `FRAMEWORK` only. An ISO date or unix seconds, at least one day and at most 1830 days ahead. Left out, the framework runs for the policy's `frameworkValidityDays`; without that default it is required.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="payTo" type="string">
  The wallet that receives the proceeds, if not the borrower.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="externalReference" type="string">
  Your own case or file number: 1 to 64 letters, digits, dots, underscores or hyphens. No personal data.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="validityDays" type="number">
  Bank only. Shortens the offer for this agreement; never longer than the policy.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signerAddress" type="string">
  Bank only. The admin wallet named as lender in the schedule. Defaults to your first verified admin wallet.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="agreement" type="object">The agreement. See [Get Loan Agreement](/endpoints/lending/get-loan-agreement#response-fields).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="created" type="boolean">`false` when the same request already waited. HTTP `200` then, `201` otherwise.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="autoIssue" type="object">When the policy has `autoIssue`: `{ attempted, issued, reason }`. `reason` is `WAITING_FOR_BANK` when it stayed `REQUESTED`, or `ABOVE_AUTO_ISSUE_LIMIT` with `limit` when the borrow amount is above `autoIssueMaxAmount` and `attempted` is `false`; the bank then issues or declines it.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `DOCUMENT_CONTENT_REFUSED` | `400` | The body carries file content. Keep the file with you and send only the salted commitment |
| `PLAIN_HASH_REFUSED` | `400` | The body carries the plain hash of a file (`contentHash`, `sha256` and similar). Send the salted commitment instead |
| `PERSONAL_DATA_REFUSED` | `400` | The body carries a personal data field such as `name` or `email`. Use wallet addresses and `externalReference` only |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | A field is missing, has the wrong type, or is not part of this route |
| `INVALID_WALLET` | `400` | `borrowerWallet` or `payTo` is not an address |
| `INVALID_EXTERNAL_REFERENCE` | `400` | `externalReference` is not 1 to 64 safe characters |
| `INVALID_AGREEMENT_FIGURES` | `400` | A required amount is missing for the kind |
| `INVALID_VALID_UNTIL` | `400` | `validUntil` is not a date at least one day and at most 1830 days ahead |
| `AGREEMENT_FEATURE_REQUIRED` | `402` | The bank's plan does not include loan agreements |
| `AGREEMENT_BANK_ONLY` | `403` | Only the bank creates a `FRAMEWORK` agreement |
| `AGREEMENT_POLICY_OFF` | `409` | E-Signatures are off on this market. Open the loan as today, or ask the bank to switch them on |
| `AGREEMENT_REQUESTS_CLOSED` | `403` | The market's policy has `requestAccess: BANK_ONLY`, so only its bank creates agreements. Ask the bank to issue one |
| `AGREEMENT_REQUEST_LIMIT` | `409` | Your instance already has 3 open requests for this wallet, or 25 on this market. Withdraw the requests you no longer need, or wait for the bank |
| `MARKET_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE` | `409` | The market could not value the collateral just now. Retry once the price is fresh |
| `AGREEMENT_FIGURES_OUTSIDE_MARKET` | `422` | The figures break a market rule. `details.problems` names each |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE`, `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market or your instance's wallets could not be read just now. Nothing was decided. Retry shortly |
