---
title: 'Sync Oracle Price'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/sync-oracle'
description: 'Push a NAV price on-chain'
---

Writes a new price to the market's oracle. This is how a `NAV`- or `MARKET`-priced market moves its stored price: the market's price authority publishes a value and pushes it here. Between pushes, the price moves only when a transaction carries a [signed price](/endpoints/lending/sign-price).

An `ORACLE`-priced market reads an external feed instead, and its oracle refuses every attested price. This endpoint refuses it with `EXTERNAL_FEED_ACTIVE`.

A `FIXED` market holds the price set at deployment for its whole life, and it never goes stale. This endpoint can only mark that price down. See [Mark down a fixed price](#mark-down-a-fixed-price).

Like every other write on this surface, nothing is submitted for you. The call returns the unsigned transaction, you broadcast it, then confirm it with `txHash`. `price` is required on both calls.

<Warning>
  `updatePrice` is authorized against the wallet that sends it, which must be an authorized signer on the oracle or its owner. The API checks the resolved signer before building the calldata and refuses with `SIGNER_NOT_AUTHORIZED` if it is not authorized. Broadcast from the wallet named in `signer`. Check `signerAuthorized` and `priceSignerAuthorized` on [Get Oracle Status](/endpoints/lending/get-oracle-status) first.
</Warning>

Price authority does not have to be your instance's primary wallet. When the lender of record took the role it declared a `priceSigner`, and that address is what this endpoint resolves to when you name none. Pass `signerAddress` to check against a different key.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/lending/list-markets), not its contract address. See [Market IDs](/endpoints/lending/list-markets#market-ids).</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="price" type="number" required>
  Net asset value per collateral token, denominated in the market's borrow asset. Must be greater than zero, and a numeric string is accepted too. Required on the confirm call as well, where the value recorded comes from the mined transaction instead. More decimal places than the borrow asset carries fail with `SYNC_FAILED`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signerAddress" type="string">
  Wallet the authorization check runs against. Omit it and the market's declared `priceSigner` is used. When the market declares none, the instance's first verified wallet is. A value that is not a 0x-prefixed 40-character hex address is ignored. Not used when confirming with `txHash`, or when a recorded attestation is submitted.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  Hash of the mined transaction, sent together with `price` to confirm it and record the push. Omit it to receive the calldata. `null` or an empty string counts as omitted. Any other value that is not a 0x-prefixed 64-character hex string is refused with `VALIDATION_ERROR`, and nothing is built or recorded.
</ParamField>

## Submit a Recorded Attestation

A price signer that keeps its key outside this instance can sign the payload from [Sign Price](/endpoints/lending/sign-price) and post the signature to [Record Price Attestation](/endpoints/lending/record-price-attestation). A later push of exactly that price returns `updatePriceWithSignature` carrying the attestation. The oracle authorizes the key that signed, so any wallet may broadcast it and no authorization check runs on the sender.

The attestation is used only while it is unexpired, not yet pushed, and recorded through this instance. It must also be dated after the oracle's current price, be at most 15 minutes old, and still pass the oracle's own signature check. Otherwise the call falls back to a plain `updatePrice`. An attested price is dated at its signed timestamp, so its age runs from signing, not from broadcast.

## Mark down a fixed price

On a `FIXED` market the oracle owner or an authorized price signer can lower the price, and nothing can raise it again through a price update. `price` must be below the current fixed price. An equal or higher price is refused with `409 FIXED_PRICE_ACTIVE`, and `details` carries `currentPrice` and `markDownOnly: true`. A mark-down is bounded by the circuit breaker below, and no recorded attestation is ever carried, because the oracle accepts no signed price on a fixed market.

<Warning>
  A mark-down is final. The market lends and liquidates against the lower price as soon as the transaction mines, and no price update can restore the old one. Mark down only on a decision the lender of record stands behind, and push one step at a time when the move is large.
</Warning>

The deviation bound itself is set in the Issuer Portal and is not on this API. On a `FIXED` market the Issuer Portal only tightens it, because it is the only limit on how far one mark-down, or the mark-downs of one hour, can move the price. A wider bound is refused there with `409 FIXED_PRICE_BOUND_LOCKED`, and with `503 ORACLE_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` when the bound in force cannot be read. An oracle on the current implementation refuses a wider bound sent to it directly as well, reverting `FixedPriceActive`. A fixed oracle created before the factory carried that implementation runs the one it was created with until its owner upgrades it, and until then only the Issuer Portal holds the bound. Read the bound in force as `maxDeviationBps` on [Get Oracle Status](/endpoints/lending/get-oracle-status) before you rely on it.

When whether the oracle holds a fixed price cannot be read, the call answers `503 PRICE_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` and prepares nothing.

## Circuit Breaker

The oracle rejects a price that deviates from the current price by more than `maxDeviationBps`, defaulting to 5000 basis points, which is 50 percent. This guards against a fat-fingered NAV wiping out healthy loans in one transaction. The same limit also bounds the total movement within a rolling one-hour window, measured from the price that opened the window.

Every push-mode oracle is initialized with a positive price at deployment, so even the first push is measured against it. The API runs both checks before building calldata. A step too large is refused with `PRICE_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE`, and a push that would take the hour's movement past the limit with `PRICE_WINDOW_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE`.

Both refusals carry `currentPrice` and `maxDeviationPercent` on the error object. The window refusal adds `windowAnchorPrice`, `windowStartedAt`, `windowResetsAt`, `acceptedLow` and `acceptedHigh`. On a `FIXED` market they also carry `details.markDownOnly: true`.

A genuine repricing beyond the limit requires the oracle owner to widen `maxDeviationBps`, or a sequence of smaller steps. Steps that land within one hour count against the same window. Do not retry a rejected push unchanged. Surface the deviation to an operator, because a retry sends the same price into the same breaker.

<Note>
  A confirmed push is recorded on the market as an `ORACLE_SYNC` transaction, visible through [List Transactions](/endpoints/lending/list-transactions), and updates `lastOracleUpdate` on the market record. A push that is broadcast but never confirmed still appears in the `pushed` list on [Get Oracle History](/endpoints/lending/get-oracle-history), which mirrors the oracle's events.
</Note>

  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"price": 105.40}'

```typescript TypeScript
const url = `https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/${marketId}/sync-oracle`;
const headers = {
  'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here',
  'Content-Type': 'application/json'
};
const res = await fetch(url, { method: 'POST', headers, body: JSON.stringify({ price: 105.40 }) });
const { success, data, error } = await res.json();
if (!success) {
  if (error.code === 'PRICE_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE') await notifyOperator(error.message);
  throw new Error(error.code);
}
const tx = await priceSignerWallet.sendTransaction(data.transaction);
await tx.wait();
await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ price: 105.40, txHash: tx.hash })
});
```

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `MISSING_PRICE` | `400` | `price` absent, non-numeric, or not greater than zero, on the calldata call and the confirm call alike |
| `NO_ORACLE` | `400` | The market has no oracle recorded |
| `EXTERNAL_FEED_ACTIVE` | `400` | The market's oracle reads an external feed and refuses pushed prices |
| `WALLET_NOT_CONFIGURED` | `400` | No `signerAddress` was given, the market declared no price signer, this instance has no verified wallet, and no recorded attestation matched |
| `PRICE_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE` | `400` | The step from the on-chain price exceeds `maxDeviationBps` |
| `PRICE_WINDOW_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE` | `400` | The movement within the current one-hour window would exceed `maxDeviationBps`. The error names the accepted band |
| `SYNC_FAILED` | `400` | The update could not be prepared, or the confirm call failed verification. `message` carries the reason |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `txHash` is present but not a 0x-prefixed 64-character hex string. `details` names the field |
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `SIGNER_NOT_AUTHORIZED` | `403` | The resolved signer is neither an authorized signer nor the owner. Checked before the calldata is built; skipped when a recorded attestation is carried and when confirming |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `FIXED_PRICE_ACTIVE` | `409` | The market has a fixed price and `price` is not below it. `details` carries `currentPrice` and `markDownOnly: true`. Send a lower price |
| `PRICE_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | Whether the oracle holds a fixed price could not be read, so nothing was prepared. Retry shortly |

Confirming does not return the typed [transaction verification codes](/endpoints/introduction#confirm-a-transaction). A receipt that is missing, reverted, sent to another contract or calling another function answers `SYNC_FAILED`, with the verification message such as `Transaction not found or not yet mined`.
