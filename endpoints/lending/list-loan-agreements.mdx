---
title: 'List Loan Agreements'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/loan-agreements'
description: 'List the loan agreements of a market'
---

Lists the market's loan agreements, newest change first, at most 200. The bank sees every agreement of the market. Any other instance sees the agreements it requested and those naming one of its verified wallets. The list leaves out the salt; read one agreement for it. A `FRAMEWORK` agreement in force carries its remaining limit.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>
  The market's record ID on your instance.
</ParamField>

## Query Parameters

<ParamField query="status" type="string">
  One status, for example `SIGNED`. `EXPIRED` includes agreements whose offer ran out but are not marked yet.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="borrower" type="string">
  Only this wallet's agreements.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="loanId" type="string">
  Only agreements bound to this loan.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="agreements" type="object[]">Each as [Get Loan Agreement](/endpoints/lending/get-loan-agreement#response-fields) returns it, without `salt` and `acceptTypedData`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="total" type="number">How many are listed.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="truncated" type="boolean">`true` when more than 200 match. Narrow with a filter.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="viewer.isBank" type="boolean">Whether your instance sees every agreement.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `INVALID_AGREEMENT_FILTER` | `400` | `status` or `loanId` is not valid |
| `INVALID_WALLET` | `400` | `borrower` is not an address |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE`, `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market or your instance's wallets could not be read just now. Nothing was decided. Retry shortly |
