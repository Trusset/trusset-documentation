---
title: 'Get Loan Approvals'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/loan-approvals'
description: 'Read whether a market runs approve-only loans'
---

Returns the market's [approve-only loans](/endpoints/lending/loan-approvals) mode: whether the switch exists on this market and network, whether it is on, and what your instance may do.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>
  The market's record ID on your instance.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data.mode" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="supported" type="boolean">`false` means the market runs an implementation from before this feature. Its admin has to upgrade it through the seven-day notice first.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="available" type="boolean">`false` means the network does not carry the approvals factory yet.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="enabled" type="boolean">Whether the market requires the lender of record's approval for every draw.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="requested" type="boolean">`true` means the market's creator asked for approve-only loans at deployment and the lender of record has not switched them on yet.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="approvals" type="string | null">The approvals contract the market names, or `null`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="canonical" type="boolean | null">`false` means the market names an approvals contract that the Trusset approvals factory did not build. Loan requests, approvals and revokes are not available for such a contract. Open Loan and Borrow More still read it when they can.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="upgradeRequired" type="boolean">Whether the market has to be upgraded before approve-only loans can be switched on.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="approverRole" type="string">The `LOAN_APPROVER_ROLE` identifier: keccak256 of `LOAN_APPROVER_ROLE`. Grant it with the market's own `grantRole`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="maxValidHours" type="number">The longest an approval made through the API stays valid, in hours.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="viewer" type="object | null">`{ isBank, isAdmin, approverWallets }` for your instance, or `null`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
