---
title: 'Get Identity Gates'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/identity-gates'
description: 'Read the borrower and provider registers a market names and what each one gates'
---

Reports, read live from the chain, which identity registers the market names and what each one gates. Both registers are optional. Without a borrower register, the collateral token's own compliance alone governs borrowers. Without a provider register, each vault admits depositors through its own register alone.

Change the registers with [Set Identity Gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates).

## What Each Register Gates

On a current market, the two registers gate different parties at different doors.

The **borrower register** (`borrowerRegistry`) is examined by the market itself on every `openLoan` and `borrowMore`. Repaying, closing a loan, releasing collateral and claiming a surplus are never gated.

The **provider register** (`providerRegistry`) is not examined by the market. The market takes direct deposits only from the operator wallet and the vaults its admin registered, and asks no register about either. Every registered vault reads the market's provider register, beside its own register, before it admits a depositor or a share transfer. With no provider register, each vault admits depositors through its own register alone. Redemptions are never gated.

Every check is a live `isVerified(address)` call against the register, so a change on the register side takes effect on the next call. Registers answering a single `bool`, as ERC-3643 and ONCHAINID-based registers do, and registers answering `(bool, uint8)`, as the Trusset register does, are read alike. A register that cannot answer refuses every party.

## Gates Built on Verification Profiles

A register can be the eligibility register of one of your [verification profiles](/endpoints/customers/list-verification-profiles#eligibility-registers). It then admits exactly the wallets admitted under that profile, and `borrowerProfile` or `providerProfile` names the profile. A register that belongs to no profile of your instance reads `null` there, which says nothing about whom it admits.

A `MARKET` market lends to professional borrowers only, so its borrower register must qualify as a professional gate: every verification profile that admits wallets to it, on any instance of the market's network and environment, is limited to professional clients and in the `REGISTER` mode, and there is at least one. `borrowerGateCompliant` says whether it qualifies, and `borrowerGateProblem` says what is missing. While it is `false`, the API opens no loan and adds no liquidity on the market: [Open Loan](/endpoints/lending/open-loan), [Borrow More](/endpoints/lending/borrow-more), [Add Liquidity](/endpoints/lending/add-liquidity) and [Register Vault](/endpoints/lending/register-vault) refuse with `PROFESSIONAL_BORROWER_GATE_REQUIRED`, and so do vault allocations, draw caps and rebalances for the market. See [Professional borrowers](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#professional-borrowers).

`borrowerProfile` names only a profile of your own instance. A register can also be the eligibility register of a profile of another instance on the market's network and environment, for example when a lender of record lends against the professional register a deploying instance keeps. `borrowerProfileElsewhere` is how the lender of record sees that: `{ professionalOnly: true }` when every profile of another instance that admits wallets to the register is limited to professional clients, `{ professionalOnly: false }` when at least one is not. The other instance's profile is not named, and its key, name and requirements are not disclosed. A profile in the `EXTERNAL` mode admits nobody through Trusset and is not counted. `providerProfileElsewhere` does the same for the provider register.

Both read `null` when a profile of your own instance owns the register, which `borrowerProfile` or `providerProfile` then names, when no profile of another instance admits wallets to it, and when the profiles could not be read. Profiles of other instances also count for `borrowerGateCompliant`.

## Gates Built on Whitelists

A register can be a whitelist: a list created through [Create Whitelist](/endpoints/lending/create-access-list) that admits exactly the wallets its owner and managers listed and examines no identity. `borrowerAccessList` or `providerAccessList` then describes it. A whitelist of your own instance carries `id`, `name`, `purpose`, `ownerWallet` and `own: true`. A whitelist another instance keeps carries its `address` with `own: false`, and `id`, `name` and `purpose` read `null`.

A whitelist cannot be the borrower gate of a `MARKET` market and cannot gate a market on the earlier single-register implementation. See [Whitelists as gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#whitelists-as-gates).

`accessListSteps` offers the market's lender of record the step that lets it maintain a whitelist gate itself: becoming a manager of the list. There is one entry per whitelist the gates name, while the lender of record is neither the list's owner nor one of its managers, and none while the market has no lender of record. An offered step carries the `setManager` transaction for the list owner to sign. A step is withheld, with `blocked: true` and `code: ACCESS_LIST_SHARED`, when the list also serves the gate of a market of another lender of record, a vault or a vault group. As manager, the lender could admit and remove wallets there too, so `sharedWith` names those products and `reason` explains the alternative. Only products of the instance that keeps the list, of your instance and of the instances that record this market are counted.

## Older Market Generations

`generation: SINGLE_REGISTER` marks an earlier implementation that examines borrowers and providers against one register and offers no way to change it. `providerRegistry` then repeats `borrowerRegistry`, and `upgradeRequired` is `true`. The market admin moves it onto the current implementation with [Upgrade Market](/endpoints/lending/upgrade-market) before the registers can be chosen.

A two-register market from before the current generation reports `vaultOnlyLiquidity: false` and `providerGateReadBy: MARKET`. When its pool is open to third-party providers (`operatorOnlyLiquidity: false`), the market itself examines those providers against the provider register, which must then stay installed (`providerGateRequired: true`).

## The Previous Trusset ID Register

The Trusset ID Register on Sepolia moved to `0xEf244c4Bd4d71Bd9A30EDF6Edef7CCC72642291a` on 10 October 2026. A side still gated on the previous register, `0xE9114c40934f6fB6BB317935156b731f7A86D006`, carries `retired: true`, `currentRegistry` and `note` in `borrower` or `provider`:

```json
"borrower": {
  "address": "0xe9114c40934f6fb6bb317935156b731f7a86d006",
  "shape": "tuple",
  "usable": true,
  "retired": true,
  "currentRegistry": "0xef244c4bd4d71bd9a30edf6edef7ccc72642291a",
  "note": "This is the previous Trusset ID Register. It takes no new identities, so it refuses every customer verified since the register moved to the current Trusset ID Register (0xef244c4bd4d71bd9a30edf6edef7ccc72642291a)."
}
```

`usable` stays `true`, because the register still answers for the wallets verified on it before the move. Every customer verified since is refused at that door. The market admin moves the side with [Set Identity Gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#the-previous-trusset-id-register).

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `NO_MARKET_ADDRESS` | `400` | The market has no on-chain address recorded |
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `IDENTITY_GATES_READ_FAILED` | `500` | The registers could not be read and no more specific code applied |
| `IDENTITY_GATES_UNREADABLE` | `503` | The market's implementation or one of its register reads could not be answered by the chain. Retry |
| `STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | `MARKET` markets only. The verification profiles behind the borrower register could not be read, so `borrowerGateCompliant` could not be decided. Retry |
