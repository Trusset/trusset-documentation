---
title: 'Set Agreement Policy'
api: 'PUT /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/agreement-policy'
description: 'Switch loan agreements off, to optional or to required on a market'
---

Sets the market's loan agreement policy. A setting made here always wins: it replaces a policy that came from a standing policy, and no standing policy overwrites it later. Fields you leave out keep their current value.

Switching to `OPTIONAL` or `REQUIRED` needs the Scale plan on the bank's instance. Switching to `OFF` is always allowed. Agreements already issued stay as they are; `OFF` only stops the gate and the binding.

## Who can set it

The bank: an API key of an instance with a verified wallet that holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` on the market.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>
  The market's record ID on your instance.
</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="mode" type="string">
  `OFF`, `OPTIONAL` or `REQUIRED`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="validityDays" type="number">
  1 to 365. How long an issued `LOAN` agreement's offer stays open. Default 30.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="autoIssue" type="boolean">
  Issue a borrower's request at once when its figures are inside the market rules. Default `false`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="autoIssueMaxAmount" type="string">
  With `autoIssue` on, the largest borrow amount issued at once, as a decimal of the borrow asset such as `"50000"`. A larger request stays `REQUESTED` for the bank. `null` removes the ceiling. Default `null`. Left out, the current value stays.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="frameworkValidityDays" type="number">
  7 to 1830. How long a `FRAMEWORK` agreement runs when the bank creates one without `validUntil`. `null` means no default, so `validUntil` stays required. Default `null`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="requestAccess" type="string">
  Who may request agreements on this market. `ANY`, the default, lets every instance with access to the market request one for its borrowers. `BANK_ONLY` closes requests: only the bank creates agreements, and a request from another instance answers `403 AGREEMENT_REQUESTS_CLOSED`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signerAddress" type="string">
  The admin wallet to record as the setter. Defaults to your first verified admin wallet.
</ParamField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `DOCUMENT_CONTENT_REFUSED` | `400` | The body carries file content. Keep the file with you and send only the salted commitment |
| `PLAIN_HASH_REFUSED` | `400` | The body carries the plain hash of a file (`contentHash`, `sha256` and similar). Send the salted commitment instead |
| `PERSONAL_DATA_REFUSED` | `400` | The body carries a personal data field such as `name` or `email`. Use wallet addresses and `externalReference` only |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | A field is missing, has the wrong type, or is not part of this route |
| `INVALID_AGREEMENT_POLICY` | `400` | `mode`, `validityDays`, `autoIssue`, `autoIssueMaxAmount`, `frameworkValidityDays` or `requestAccess` is out of range |
| `AGREEMENT_FEATURE_REQUIRED` | `402` | Your plan does not include loan agreements. Upgrade to Scale, or keep the policy `OFF` |
| `AGREEMENT_BANK_ONLY` | `403` | No verified wallet of your instance holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` on the market |
| `SIGNER_NOT_MARKET_ADMIN` | `403` | `signerAddress` is not one of your verified admin wallets |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE`, `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market or your instance's wallets could not be read just now. Nothing was decided. Retry shortly |
