---
title: 'Get Loan Schedule'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/loan-agreements/{ref}/schedule.pdf'
description: 'Download the loan schedule annex as a PDF'
---

Returns the loan schedule as a one-page PDF annex for the bank's contract. It shows the agreement reference and the `scheduleHash` on every page, and carries no name and no salt. A `REQUESTED` agreement returns its provisional figures; the frozen schedule exists once the bank issues it.

The answer is `application/pdf` with a `Content-Disposition` of `Loan-Schedule-{ref}.pdf`.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>
  The market's record ID on your instance.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="ref" type="string" required>
  The agreement reference: `agr_` followed by 20 characters.
</ParamField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `AGREEMENT_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No agreement with this reference on this market, or your instance is not a party to it. List the agreements to find it |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE`, `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market or your instance's wallets could not be read just now. Nothing was decided. Retry shortly |
