---
title: 'Borrow More'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/{marketId}/borrow-more'
description: 'Draw additional funds against an existing loan'
---

Increases the borrowed amount on an active loan without posting more collateral. Only the loan's borrower can sign it: the contract reverts for any other sender. It also reverts if the resulting debt would exceed what the existing collateral supports at the current price.

Single transaction in both modes. No approval is involved because funds move out of the pool, not in.

<Warning>
  A loan carrying a stamped fixed rate cannot be drawn on again. The extra principal would ride on a rate stamped for a different amount at a different time, so the market refuses it. The call comes back as `FIXED_RATE_LOAN` and the message names the stamped rate. Open a new loan at current terms instead.
</Warning>

On a `MARKET`-priced market the API builds no further draw until the market's borrower register qualifies as a professional gate, the same check [Open Loan](/endpoints/lending/open-loan#professional-borrowers) runs. It refuses with `409 PROFESSIONAL_BORROWER_GATE_REQUIRED`, `details.field: "borrowerRegistry"`, right after the lender-of-record check. A loan already open is not touched: repaying, adding collateral and closing stay open. See [Professional borrowers](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#professional-borrowers).

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/lending/list-markets), not its contract address. See [Market IDs](/endpoints/lending/list-markets#market-ids).</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="loanId" type="integer" required>
  On-chain loan ID. A positive integer. Accepted as a JSON number or as a numeric string, so the value [Get Loan](/endpoints/lending/get-loan) returns passes straight back.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="string">
  Additional amount to borrow, as a positive decimal string in borrow asset units. The loan's debt grows by this amount. Required when building calldata, not needed on the confirm call.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signedPrice" type="object">
  EIP-712 signed price from [Sign Price](/endpoints/lending/sign-price). Omit to price against the stored oracle value. A market that prices from an external feed refuses a signed price with `EXTERNAL_FEED_ACTIVE`, and a market with a fixed price with `FIXED_PRICE_ACTIVE`.
  <Expandable>
    <ParamField body="price" type="string">Price in base units.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="timestamp" type="integer">Signing timestamp.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="validUntil" type="integer">Signature expiry.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="signature" type="string">EIP-712 signature.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  Hash of the transaction you broadcast for this operation. Send it to confirm the transaction and record the result. Omit it to receive the calldata.
</ParamField>

## Draws on a loan with a payee

A loan opened with a `payToAddress` pays every draw to that payee, never to the signing wallet. The payee was fixed when the loan was opened and cannot be changed. The calldata response names it in `paidTo` and `payeeAddress`, and its `warnings` state it. See [Pay the proceeds to another wallet](/endpoints/lending/open-loan#pay-the-proceeds-to-another-wallet).

The payee must still pass the market's borrower register. A payee the register no longer admits is refused with `400 PAYEE_NOT_VERIFIED`, and one whose removal is pending with `409 ELIGIBILITY_REMOVAL_PENDING`. Either refusal holds until the payee is admitted again, because no other wallet can take its place. Repaying, adding collateral and closing are not affected. When where the draw would be paid cannot be read, the call answers `503 MARKET_STATE_UNAVAILABLE`.

## Opening fee and vault draws

A market that charges an opening fee charges it on every draw, this one included. The debt grows by `amount` and the borrower receives `amount` less the fee, which is the flat fee or 0.5 percent of `amount`, whichever is smaller. The calldata response then carries `transactionFee`, shaped as on [Open Loan](/endpoints/lending/open-loan#what-the-loan-will-cost-and-when-it-is-due), and its message in `warnings`.

On a market that draws on vault liquidity, a draw larger than the pool's borrowable liquidity pulls the shortfall from the registered vaults in the same transaction. The API does not refuse such a draw for liquidity, because the market itself is the final gate there. The confirm response lists each vault draw in `vaultDraws`.

<Warning>
  Borrowing more moves the loan closer to its liquidation threshold. Read the resulting health factor from [Get Loan](/endpoints/lending/get-loan) afterwards, and expose it to the borrower before they commit.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Capacity is checked before any transaction is offered. An amount past what the collateral supports is refused with `EXCEEDS_MAX_BORROW`. With a `signedPrice`, that limit is computed at the signed price, because the contract applies it before it prices the collateral. On a market without vault liquidity, one the pool cannot fund is refused with `INSUFFICIENT_LIQUIDITY`. Compute headroom from [Get Max Borrow](/endpoints/lending/get-max-borrow) against the loan's collateral, less outstanding principal and accrued interest.
</Note>

While a Dutch auction is open against the loan, the contract reverts the draw. The API does not check for an open auction before offering calldata.

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `NO_MARKET_ADDRESS` | `400` | The market has no on-chain address recorded |
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `loanId` is not a positive integer, or `amount` is missing on the build call, not a positive decimal string, or carries more decimal places than the borrow asset supports |
| `LOAN_NOT_ACTIVE` | `400` | The loan is closed and can no longer be modified |
| `MARKET_PAUSED` | `400` | The market is paused. Its admin must unpause it |
| `IDENTITY_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | The borrower is no longer verified on the market's borrower register. `borrowMore` runs the same gate as `openLoan`, so a registration that lapsed mid-loan is refused here. `details` carries `register`, `requiredProfile` and `accessList`, as on [Open Loan](/endpoints/lending/open-loan) |
| `IDENTITY_GATE_RETIRED` | `409` | Returned instead of `IDENTITY_NOT_VERIFIED` when the borrower register that refuses the borrower is the previous Trusset ID Register. It takes no new identities, so the wallet cannot be added to it. `details` carries `register`, `gate` (`identity`), `retired: true` and `currentRegistry`. The market admin moves the gate to the current register with [Set Identity Gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#the-previous-trusset-id-register), then retry |
| `PAYEE_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | The loan's payee is no longer admitted to the market's borrower register. The payee cannot be changed, so draws stay refused until it is admitted again. `details` carries `payee`, `register`, `requiredProfile` and `accessList` |
| `PRICE_STALE` | `400` | The price is older than the oracle's staleness window. On an attested market, push a price with [Sync Oracle Price](/endpoints/lending/sync-oracle) or supply a `signedPrice`. On an external-feed market, wait for the feed to publish |
| `EXCEEDS_MAX_BORROW` | `400` | The request would take the loan past what its collateral supports, at the signed price when a `signedPrice` is supplied |
| `INSUFFICIENT_LIQUIDITY` | `400` | The market draws on no vault liquidity and holds less borrowable liquidity than the request, after its reserve earmark |
| `INVALID_SIGNED_PRICE` | `400` | The `signedPrice` is malformed, expired, dated in the future or older than the oracle's price age window, or it was signed by a wallet that is not an authorized signer on the market's oracle, which `details.signer` then names. Sign a fresh quote with [Sign Price](/endpoints/lending/sign-price) |
| `PRICE_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE` | `400` | The `signedPrice` moves further from the oracle's current price than its `maxDeviationBps` allows |
| `PRICE_WINDOW_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE` | `400` | The `signedPrice` falls outside the band the oracle accepts within its current one-hour window |
| `LOAN_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No loan with that ID exists on this market |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `MARKET_PENDING_CURATOR` | `409` | The market has no lender of record yet. See [Lender of record](/endpoints/lending/introduction#lender-of-record) |
| `PROFESSIONAL_BORROWER_GATE_REQUIRED` | `409` | The market is `MARKET`-priced and its borrower register does not qualify as a professional gate. `details.field` is `borrowerRegistry` |
| `FIXED_RATE_LOAN` | `409` | The loan carries a stamped fixed rate, so the market refuses an additional draw |
| `COLLATERAL_MATURED` | `409` | The collateral instrument passed its recorded maturity, and the record was asserted by a party to this market |
| `EXTERNAL_FEED_ACTIVE` | `409` | A `signedPrice` was sent to a market that prices from an external feed. Retry without it |
| `FIXED_PRICE_ACTIVE` | `409` | A `signedPrice` was sent to a market with a fixed price. Retry without it |
| `SIGNED_PRICE_OUTDATED` | `409` | The `signedPrice` is not dated after the price the oracle already holds, so the oracle would refuse it. Sign a fresh quote |
| `ELIGIBILITY_REMOVAL_PENDING` | `409` | The borrower's or the payee's admission to the borrower register is waiting for removal, so no new draw is prepared |
| `MARKET_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market could not be read to check its price source and borrower register, whether it is paused, its price, its transaction fees, or where the draw would be paid. Retry shortly |
| `STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The verification profiles behind a `MARKET` market's borrower register could not be read, or the admissions to the borrower register could not be checked for a pending removal. Retry shortly |
| `PRICE_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The oracle could not be read to check the `signedPrice`, so nothing was built. Retry shortly |
| `LOAN_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The loan or its borrow limit could not be read from the network. Retry shortly |

Confirming with `txHash` can also return any [transaction verification error](/endpoints/introduction#confirm-a-transaction).
