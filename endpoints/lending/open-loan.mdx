---
title: 'Open Loan'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/{marketId}/open-loan'
description: 'Pledge security tokens as collateral and borrow the settlement asset'
---

Opens an overcollateralized loan. The borrower pledges security tokens and receives the market's borrow asset, and the borrower's wallet signs every step. How the collateral is secured depends on the market's mode: `FREEZE` locks the tokens in the borrower's own wallet, `CUSTODY` escrows them in the adapter.

The maximum borrow is the collateral value multiplied by the market's collateral factor. Quote it first with [Get Max Borrow](/endpoints/lending/get-max-borrow). On a market that charges an opening fee, the borrower receives the borrow amount less the fee and owes the full borrow amount.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/lending/list-markets), not its contract address. See [Market IDs](/endpoints/lending/list-markets#market-ids).</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="collateralAmount" type="string">
  Security tokens to pledge, as a positive decimal string. Parsed at the collateral token's decimals. Required when building calldata. Not needed on the confirm call, where the pledge is read from the mined transaction.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="borrowAmount" type="string">
  Amount to borrow, as a positive decimal string. Parsed at the borrow asset's decimals. Must be at least the market's `minBorrowAmount`. This is the debt the loan records, before any opening fee is withheld from the payout. Required when building calldata, not needed on the confirm call.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="borrowerAddress" type="string">
  Wallet that will sign the loan and hold the collateral. Required when building calldata. Every collateral, identity and supply-cap check runs against this address, and there is no loan to resolve it from yet. Omitting it returns `BORROWER_ADDRESS_REQUIRED`. Not needed on the confirm call, where the borrower is taken from the mined transaction.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signedPrice" type="object">
  EIP-712 signed price from [Sign Price](/endpoints/lending/sign-price), applied atomically with the loan. Omit to price against the stored oracle value, which must not be stale. A market that prices from an external feed refuses a signed price with `EXTERNAL_FEED_ACTIVE`, and a market with a fixed price refuses one with `FIXED_PRICE_ACTIVE`, because its oracle accepts none.
  <Expandable>
    <ParamField body="price" type="string">Price in base units.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="timestamp" type="integer">Signing timestamp.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="validUntil" type="integer">Signature expiry.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="signature" type="string">EIP-712 signature.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="clientReference" type="string">
  The client this loan is for, as the customer's `referenceKey`, its `externalId` or its customer ID. Up to 120 characters, matched against the customers of your instance that are not archived. Create the record first with [Create Customer](/endpoints/customers/create).

  Send it on the build call and again on the confirm call. The build call checks the client before anything is signed and refuses an ineligible one with `409 CLIENT_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, see [Client of record](#client-of-record). The confirm call checks it again and records the attribution. A loan built without it can be signed and mined for a client the confirm call then refuses, and such a loan can only be recorded without a client. The fields `customerId`, `clientId`, `clientReferenceKey` and `externalId` are refused with `CLIENT_REFERENCE_FIELD`, and `onBehalfOf` with `KEY_MATERIAL_REJECTED`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="payToAddress" type="string">
  Wallet that receives the borrowed amount instead of the signing wallet. The signer stays the borrower of record. See [Pay the proceeds to another wallet](#pay-the-proceeds-to-another-wallet). Omitted, `null`, an empty string or the borrower's own address pays the borrower. Build call only: the confirm call reads the payee from the mined transaction.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="reattribute" type="boolean">
  Set `true` on the confirm call to move a loan that is already attributed to another client of your instance. Without it that confirm is refused with `CLIENT_REFERENCE_CONFLICT`. The change is recorded against both clients with the time it was made.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  Hash of the transaction you broadcast for this operation. Send it to confirm the transaction and record the result. Omit it to receive the calldata.
</ParamField>

## Transaction Shape by Mode

When `txHash` is omitted the response is always `SIGN_TRANSACTIONS` with a `mode` field, and the step count depends on the market.

**`CUSTODY`** returns two steps: `approve` on the collateral token for the adapter, then `openLoan`. Broadcast in order.

**`FREEZE`** returns one step: `openLoan`. No approval exists because the tokens never leave the borrower's wallet.

Both shapes carry `confirmStepIndex`, the index of the `openLoan` step whose hash confirms the loan, and `confirmWith`, which names this route and the body to confirm with.

## What the loan will cost and when it is due

The calldata response carries an `offering` block whenever the market runs loan terms or stamps a fixed rate. A market on the default curve with no term returns no `offering` at all. Show it before the borrower signs: it is the only place the due date and the stamped rate appear ahead of the loan existing.

<ResponseField name="offering" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="approximateDueAt" type="string">Unix seconds when this loan would fall due, computed at build time from the market's term config. Present only when the market runs terms. It is approximate because the clock starts when the transaction mines, not when the payload was built.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="termGracePeriodSeconds" type="string">Seconds after the due date before the term penalty applies. Present only when the market runs terms.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="termPenaltyBps" type="integer">The term penalty in basis points. Present only when the market runs terms.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fixedRateBps" type="integer">The rate this loan would be stamped with, in basis points. Present only when the market's interest model stamps a fixed rate. A stamped rate does not move with utilization for the life of the loan.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="stampedAtSigning" type="array">The figures stamped onto the loan when the transaction mines. Always `dueAt`, plus `fixedRate` when a fixed rate is stamped. A market that stamps terms per loan adds `termGracePeriod` and `termPenalty`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="termStamping" type="string">`PER_LOAN` when the market stamps the grace period and term penalty onto each loan at signing. `MARKET_CONFIG` when open loans follow the live term configuration instead. `UNKNOWN` when that could not be read.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="appliesToOpenLoans" type="array">Term settings a later configuration change would still reach on this loan. Empty on a `PER_LOAN` market, `termGracePeriod` and `termPenalty` otherwise.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

The opening fee is the other cost fixed at signing. A market may carry a flat opening fee, stamped when it was deployed and never changeable afterwards. Read it from `transactionFees` on [Get Fee Split](/endpoints/lending/get-fee-split). Each charge is the flat fee or 0.5 percent of the amount borrowed, whichever is smaller. When it is not zero, the calldata response carries a `transactionFee` block.

<ResponseField name="transactionFee" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="kind" type="string">`OPEN`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fee" type="string">The fee this loan would pay, in borrow asset units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="feeRaw" type="string">The same fee in the borrow asset's smallest unit.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="borrowed" type="string">The debt the loan records, equal to `borrowAmount`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="netDisbursed" type="string">What the borrower receives: `borrowed` less `fee`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="netDisbursedRaw" type="string">The same amount in the smallest unit.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="message" type="string">Plain-language statement of the fee, the debt and the payout.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Warnings

The calldata response carries a `warnings` array when a preflight found something the borrower should see but that does not block the loan. The array is absent when there is nothing to say. Object entries carry `kind`, `enforcement`, `message` and `note`.

| `kind` | What it means |
|---|---|
| `TERMS_NOT_PUBLISHED` | The lender of record has published no terms of use, so the loan is governed by none |
| `MATURITY_APPROACHING` | The collateral instrument's recorded maturity falls inside the warning window. Carries `maturityDate`, `daysRemaining`, `windowDays` and `assertedBy` |
| `MATURITY_RECORDED` | The instrument has a recorded maturity further out. Carries the same fields |
| `MATURITY_PASSED_FOREIGN_RECORD` | The recorded maturity has passed, but it was asserted by an instance that is not a party to this market, so the loan is not refused on it |
| `MATURITY_UNCHECKED` | The recorded maturity could not be read, so this preflight did not check it |

`enforcement` is `OFF_CHAIN` on every one of these. The contract does not know about them, so acting on a warning is the caller's decision. When the market charges an opening fee, the array also carries `transactionFee.message` as a plain string rather than an object. With a `payToAddress`, that string is the statement of who receives the proceeds instead, and `transactionFee.message` names the payee.

## Professional borrowers

On a `MARKET`-priced market the API builds no loan until the market's borrower register qualifies as a professional gate, and refuses with `409 PROFESSIONAL_BORROWER_GATE_REQUIRED`, `details.field: "borrowerRegistry"`. The register qualifies when at least one verification profile admits wallets to it, and every profile that does, on any instance of the market's network and environment, is limited to professional clients and has its admissions signed by its own instance. See [Professional borrowers](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#professional-borrowers).

The check runs right after the lender-of-record check, before the adapter, pause and term checks. It reads the market's price source and borrower register from the chain, and the profiles from Trusset's records. When either read fails the call answers `503`, `MARKET_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` or `STATE_UNAVAILABLE`, and builds nothing. The market contract does not apply this rule, so it holds only for transactions built through this API.

## Client of record

A `clientReference` is checked on the build call and on the confirm call, before anything else: before the market's own checks on the build call, and before the transaction is read on the confirm call. Send it on both. The build call is the one that refuses an ineligible client before anything is signed. The loan is refused with `409 CLIENT_NOT_ELIGIBLE` when the client:

- is recorded as `rejected` or `revoked` (`details.reason` is `REJECTED` or `REVOKED`);
- is recorded in a country that the verification profile it was onboarded under blocks, or that a profile behind the market's borrower register blocks (`COUNTRY_BLOCKED`). A profile in the `EXTERNAL` mode is not counted;
- is not a professional client, on a `MARKET`-priced market (`NOT_PROFESSIONAL`, or `NOT_VERIFIED` for a client typed professional whose verification on your instance is not complete).

A client counts as professional when one of its wallets is admitted to a profile of your instance limited to professional clients. It also counts when it is verified on your instance under a profile that is limited to professional clients or registers its investors as Professional or Eligible Counterparty. Verified on your instance means the record reads `verified` and carries the `verifiedAt` of a verification completed here (a verify, an ID link or a registration confirmed on this instance), with no revocation since. A `verified` status set through [Update Customer](/endpoints/customers/update) marks the record only and does not count. An admission recorded by another instance does not count either, even to this market's own borrower register. `details.clientReference` names the client.

The borrower register is read from the market at call time, so a register the market admin changed after the market was recorded is the one applied. When it cannot be read the call answers `503 MARKET_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` and builds nothing.

## Pay the proceeds to another wallet

`payToAddress` sends the borrowed amount, less any opening fee, to a wallet other than the one that signs. The signer stays the borrower of record: it provides the collateral, owes the debt, and receives every collateral return, any liquidation surplus and every escrow or surplus claim. The payee is fixed for the life of the loan and receives every later [Borrow More](/endpoints/lending/borrow-more) draw as well. The calldata calls the `openLoan` overload that takes the payee.

<Warning>
  The market examines the payee against its borrower register, exactly as it examines the borrower, at opening and at every later draw. A payee the register no longer admits blocks every further draw on the loan until it is admitted again, because the payee cannot be changed. Repaying, adding collateral and closing stay open.
</Warning>

The build call refuses a payee the transaction could not pay:

- A market whose implementation predates pay-to answers `409 PAY_TO_UNSUPPORTED`. Its admin upgrades it with [Upgrade Market](/endpoints/lending/upgrade-market). `payToSupported` on [List Markets](/endpoints/lending/list-markets) shows which markets take a payee.
- The payee may not be one of the market's own contracts or a contract it is wired to: the market, its liquidation router, its collateral adapter, its auction venue, its insurance fund, the settlement token (stablecoin), the collateral token, the oracle, the interest rate model or a vault registered on the market. That is refused with `400 PAY_TO_NOT_ALLOWED`, and `details.reason` names which. The market itself refuses the first five. It would pay the others, and the proceeds would be lost to the borrower while the full debt stays owed.
- A payee that the borrower register does not admit is refused with `400 PAYEE_NOT_VERIFIED`. `details` carries `payee`, `register`, and `requiredProfile` or `accessList` when the register is one of your profiles' eligibility registers or a whitelist. A payee whose removal from that register is pending is refused with `409 ELIGIBILITY_REMOVAL_PENDING`.

The calldata response then carries `payeeAddress` and a `payee` block, and its `warnings` state who receives what. The confirm call returns the payee the market recorded for the loan, read from its `LoanPayeeSet` event, and the ledger books the draw and the opening fee against the payee.

## Vault draws

Some markets draw on vault liquidity, shown as `useVaultLiquidity` on [Get Market Metrics](/endpoints/lending/get-metrics). There, a borrow larger than the pool's borrowable liquidity pulls the shortfall from the registered vaults inside the borrower's own transaction. The draw is the smallest amount that covers the shortfall with the reserve earmark intact. The confirm response lists each draw in `vaultDraws`.

The API refuses such a loan only when no draw could fund it. `INSUFFICIENT_LIQUIDITY` then names what the connected vaults can draw right now. A market whose implementation still holds a draw into an empty pool to the first-deposit floor refuses a smaller draw with `FIRST_DRAW_BELOW_FLOOR`, and the message names the remedies.

<Warning>
  Loans cannot be opened until the market has a lender of record and its collateral adapter is authorized on the token. A market still seeking a lender of record is refused on that ground first, with `MARKET_PENDING_CURATOR`. The token-side authorizations are the register provider's to arrange, so naming them as the obstacle would send the borrower to the wrong party. Once the role is taken, an unauthorized adapter rejects with `COLLATERAL_ADAPTER_NOT_AUTHORIZED` or `FREEZE_MODE_UNSUPPORTED_TOKEN`. See [Get Setup Steps](/endpoints/lending/get-setup-steps).
</Warning>

<Note>
  Minimum and maximum borrow are checked when the calldata is built, not when you confirm. Broadcasting an out-of-range amount reverts on-chain rather than returning `BELOW_MIN_BORROW` or `EXCEEDS_MAX_BORROW`. With a `signedPrice`, the maximum borrow is computed at the signed price, because the contract applies it before it prices the collateral.
</Note>

```typescript TypeScript
const url = `https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/${marketId}/open-loan`;
const headers = {
  'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here',
  'Content-Type': 'application/json'
};

const res = await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({
    collateralAmount: '1000',
    borrowAmount: '50000',
    borrowerAddress: await wallet.getAddress(),
    clientReference: 'CL-000417',
    signedPrice: {
      price: signed.price,
      timestamp: signed.timestamp,
      validUntil: signed.validUntil,
      signature: signed.signature
    }
  })
});
const { data } = await res.json();

const hashes: string[] = [];
for (const step of data.steps) {
  const tx = await wallet.sendTransaction(step.transaction);
  await tx.wait();
  hashes.push(tx.hash);
}

const confirmed = await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ txHash: hashes[data.confirmStepIndex], clientReference: 'CL-000417' })
});
const { data: loan } = await confirmed.json();
```

## Error Codes

The price, supply-cap, collateral-value and liquidity checks run in the order the contract reverts in. A refusal therefore names the condition the borrower would otherwise have hit on-chain.

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `BORROWER_ADDRESS_REQUIRED` | `400` | `borrowerAddress` was omitted while building calldata |
| `NO_MARKET_ADDRESS` | `400` | The market has no on-chain address recorded |
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `collateralAmount` or `borrowAmount` is missing on the build call, not a positive decimal string, or carries more decimal places than its token supports. Also a malformed address, `txHash` or `clientReference` |
| `CLIENT_REFERENCE_FIELD` | `400` | The body carries `customerId`, `clientId`, `clientReferenceKey` or `externalId`. Pass the value as `clientReference` |
| `INVALID_CLIENT_REFERENCE` | `400` | `clientReference` is blank |
| `INVALID_PAY_TO_ADDRESS` | `400` | `payToAddress` is not 0x followed by 40 hex characters, is the zero address, or fails its EIP-55 checksum |
| `PAY_TO_NOT_ALLOWED` | `400` | `payToAddress` is the market or a contract it is wired to. `details` carries `reason` (`MARKET`, `ROUTER`, `ADAPTER`, `AUCTION_MODULE`, `INSURANCE_FUND`, `SETTLEMENT_TOKEN`, `COLLATERAL_TOKEN`, `ORACLE`, `INTEREST_MODEL` or `VAULT`) and `payToAddress`. Name the wallet that should receive the proceeds |
| `PAYEE_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | The payee is not admitted to the market's borrower register. `details` carries `payee`, `register`, `requiredProfile` and `accessList`. Admit the payee, or omit `payToAddress` |
| `COLLATERAL_ADAPTER_NOT_AUTHORIZED` | `400` | The adapter lacks the agent role (`FREEZE`) or allow-listed or verified holder status (`CUSTODY`) on the collateral token |
| `FREEZE_MODE_UNSUPPORTED_TOKEN` | `400` | The token does not implement the ERC-3643 freeze interface. Use a `CUSTODY` market |
| `MARKET_PAUSED` | `400` | The market is paused. Its admin must unpause it |
| `IDENTITY_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | `borrowerAddress` is not verified on the market's borrower register. `details.register` names the register, `details.requiredProfile` the verification profile whose eligibility register it is, and `details.accessList` the whitelist it is, each `null` when it does not apply. With a profile, the message says to onboard and admit the wallet under it. With a whitelist, it says to have the list's owner or a manager list the wallet |
| `IDENTITY_GATE_RETIRED` | `409` | Returned instead of `IDENTITY_NOT_VERIFIED` when the borrower register that refuses `borrowerAddress` is the previous Trusset ID Register. It takes no new identities, so the wallet cannot be added to it. `details` carries `register`, `gate` (`identity`), `retired: true` and `currentRegistry`. The market admin moves the gate to the current register with [Set Identity Gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#the-previous-trusset-id-register), then retry |
| `BELOW_MIN_BORROW` | `400` | `borrowAmount` is under the market's `minBorrowAmount` |
| `PRICE_STALE` | `400` | The price is older than the oracle's staleness window. On an attested market, push a price with [Sync Oracle Price](/endpoints/lending/sync-oracle) or supply a `signedPrice`. On an external-feed market, wait for the feed to publish |
| `SUPPLY_CAP_EXCEEDED` | `400` | The pledge would push total collateral past `maxTotalCollateral` |
| `USER_SUPPLY_CAP_EXCEEDED` | `400` | The pledge would push this borrower past `maxUserCollateral` |
| `EXCEEDS_MAX_BORROW` | `400` | `borrowAmount` exceeds what the collateral supports, at the signed price when a `signedPrice` is supplied |
| `INVALID_SIGNED_PRICE` | `400` | The `signedPrice` is malformed, expired, dated in the future or older than the oracle's price age window, or it was signed by a wallet that is not an authorized signer on the market's oracle, which `details.signer` then names. Sign a fresh quote with [Sign Price](/endpoints/lending/sign-price) |
| `PRICE_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE` | `400` | The `signedPrice` moves further from the oracle's current price than its `maxDeviationBps` allows |
| `PRICE_WINDOW_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE` | `400` | The `signedPrice` falls outside the band the oracle accepts within its current one-hour window |
| `INSUFFICIENT_LIQUIDITY` | `400` | The pool, after its reserve earmark and any vault draw it could make, holds less than the loan needs |
| `FIRST_DRAW_BELOW_FLOOR` | `400` | The pool is empty and the vault draw that would fund this borrow is below the first-deposit floor this market's implementation applies to draws |
| `INSUFFICIENT_FREE_COLLATERAL` | `400` | On a `FREEZE` market, `borrowerAddress` holds fewer unfrozen tokens than `collateralAmount`. `details` carries `holder`, `balance`, `frozen` and `free` |
| `COLLATERAL_TRANSFER_RESTRICTED` | `400` | The collateral token's compliance rules block moving this collateral into the custody adapter |
| `CLIENT_REFERENCE_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | `clientReference` matches no customer of your instance |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `MARKET_PENDING_CURATOR` | `409` | The market has no lender of record yet. See [Lender of record](/endpoints/lending/introduction#lender-of-record) |
| `PROFESSIONAL_BORROWER_GATE_REQUIRED` | `409` | The market is `MARKET`-priced and its borrower register does not qualify as a professional gate. `details.field` is `borrowerRegistry`. See [Professional borrowers](#professional-borrowers) |
| `MARKET_TERM_EXPIRED` | `409` | The market's terminal due date has passed, so the contract refuses every new loan. Repayment and withdrawals stay open |
| `COLLATERAL_MATURED` | `409` | The collateral instrument passed its recorded maturity, and the record was asserted by a party to this market. `error.details` carries `maturityDate` |
| `EXTERNAL_FEED_ACTIVE` | `409` | A `signedPrice` was sent to a market that prices from an external feed. Retry without it |
| `FIXED_PRICE_ACTIVE` | `409` | A `signedPrice` was sent to a market with a fixed price, whose oracle refuses every signed price. Retry without it: the loan prices against the fixed price |
| `SIGNED_PRICE_OUTDATED` | `409` | The `signedPrice` is not dated after the price the oracle already holds, so the oracle would refuse it. Sign a fresh quote |
| `PAY_TO_UNSUPPORTED` | `409` | `payToAddress` was sent to a market whose implementation predates pay-to. Upgrade the market, or omit `payToAddress` |
| `ELIGIBILITY_REMOVAL_PENDING` | `409` | The borrower's or the payee's admission to the borrower register is waiting for removal, so no new loan is prepared for it. `details` carries `register` and `wallet`, plus `reason` and `profileKey` when the admission is your instance's |
| `CLIENT_NOT_ELIGIBLE` | `409` | The client named by `clientReference` may not carry this loan. `details.reason` is `REJECTED`, `REVOKED`, `COUNTRY_BLOCKED`, `NOT_PROFESSIONAL` or `NOT_VERIFIED`. See [Client of record](#client-of-record) |
| `CLIENT_REFERENCE_CONFLICT` | `409` | On confirm, the loan is already attributed to another client and `reattribute` was not `true` |
| `MARKET_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market could not be read to run a check, its price source, its borrower register, whether it can pay a third party and the contracts it is wired to included, so no transaction was prepared. Retry shortly |
| `STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The verification profiles behind a `MARKET` market's borrower register could not be read, or the admissions to the borrower register could not be checked for a pending removal. Retry shortly |
| `PRICE_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The oracle could not be read to check the `signedPrice`, so nothing was built. Retry shortly |

Confirming with `txHash` can also return any [transaction verification error](/endpoints/introduction#confirm-a-transaction).
