---
title: 'Issue Loan Agreement'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/loan-agreements/{ref}/issue'
description: 'Freeze the schedule of a requested loan agreement and create its salt'
---

Issues a `REQUESTED` agreement. The schedule is read again from the market and frozen, and a fresh 32-byte salt is created. You may lower the requested collateral or principal and shorten the offer, never raise them. The figures must be inside the market rules. Open requests and issued agreements of the same requester for that wallet that were created earlier and carry the same `externalReference` (or none, when this one has none) become `SUPERSEDED`. Newer requests, requests with another `externalReference` and signed or accepted agreements are never replaced. `agreement.supersedes` names the most recent replaced one, `agreement.superseded` lists all of them.

Then download the schedule with [Get Loan Schedule](/endpoints/lending/get-loan-schedule), attach it to your contract and sign it in your own tool.

## Who can call

The bank. Needs the Scale plan.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>
  The market's record ID on your instance.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="ref" type="string" required>
  The agreement reference: `agr_` followed by 20 characters.
</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="collateralAmount" type="string">
  A lower collateral amount than requested.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="borrowAmount" type="string">
  A lower principal than requested.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="validityDays" type="number">
  A shorter offer than the policy's, in days. A value above the policy's `validityDays` answers `INVALID_AGREEMENT_POLICY`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signerAddress" type="string">
  The admin wallet named as lender in the schedule.
</ParamField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `DOCUMENT_CONTENT_REFUSED` | `400` | The body carries file content. Keep the file with you and send only the salted commitment |
| `PLAIN_HASH_REFUSED` | `400` | The body carries the plain hash of a file (`contentHash`, `sha256` and similar). Send the salted commitment instead |
| `PERSONAL_DATA_REFUSED` | `400` | The body carries a personal data field such as `name` or `email`. Use wallet addresses and `externalReference` only |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | A field is missing, has the wrong type, or is not part of this route |
| `INVALID_AGREEMENT_POLICY` | `400` | `validityDays` is outside 1 to 365, or above the policy's `validityDays` (`details.max`) |
| `AGREEMENT_FEATURE_REQUIRED` | `402` | Your plan does not include E-Signatures |
| `AGREEMENT_BANK_ONLY` | `403` | No verified wallet of your instance holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` on the market |
| `SIGNER_NOT_MARKET_ADMIN` | `403` | `signerAddress` is not one of your verified admin wallets |
| `AGREEMENT_POLICY_OFF` | `409` | E-Signatures were switched off on this market. Switch them on first, or decline |
| `AGREEMENT_STATE` | `409` | The agreement is in a status that does not allow this. `details.status` names it; read the agreement for its next step |
| `MARKET_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE` | `409` | The collateral could not be valued just now. Retry once the price is fresh |
| `AGREEMENT_FIGURES_OUTSIDE_MARKET` | `422` | A collateral or borrow amount is above the request or outside a market rule. `details.problems` names each |
| `AGREEMENT_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No agreement with this reference on this market, or your instance is not a party to it. List the agreements to find it |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` | `429` | Too many writes in a short time. Retry after the interval in `Retry-After` |
| `AGREEMENT_POLICY_UNREADABLE` | `503` | The market's E-Signatures policy could not be read just now. Nothing was decided. Retry shortly |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE`, `SERVICE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market or your instance's wallets could not be read just now. Nothing was decided. Retry shortly |
