---
title: 'Identity Terms'
description: 'A versioned, signed statement of how the investors of a token are identified, carried in the asset data a lender acknowledges'
---

Identity Terms are a document an issuer or a platform publishes about the investors of its tokens. Each version states four things:

- **Who performs the KYC** and stands behind it: the obliged entity, its licence and supervisor, and the basis on which a lender may rely on it.
- **What a lending bank receives** about an investor, within how many days, and through which channel.
- **Which investor requirements apply**, and whether the borrower gate or a member enforces each one.
- **Which organisations take part**, and in which role.

The organisations named in a version sign it with their wallets. The issuer attaches a published version to a market's [asset data](/endpoints/lending/get-asset-data), where the lender of record reads it before it acknowledges the bundle. From then on every read of the market compares what the terms claim with the gates on chain.

<Note>
  Counsel has not yet confirmed the wording of the template. Until it has, treat every field as the parties' own statement and nothing more.
</Note>

## What they are not

- **They are not a gate.** Terms never install, change or remove a register. The gates enforce; the terms describe. The check compares the two and says where they differ.
- **They name no collateral.** Terms say nothing about a token, a market or a price source, so attaching them never checks a token list.
- **A signature is a declaration.** A member's wallet signature records which version that organisation declared. It allocates no liability by itself.
- **The reliance agreement makes them binding.** The agreement between the lender and the KYC provider cites the core hash of the version it relies on. Reliance and its liability stay in that agreement, and the lender stays responsible for its own due diligence.
- **Trusset is not a party.** Trusset supplies the template, the schema checks and the comparison with the chain. It signs nothing.
- **There is no personal data.** Members are organisations, and requirements are rules, not results. No field names an investor, and the schema refuses e-mail addresses, phone numbers and bank account numbers in every text field.
- **They count only once acknowledged.** Terms count for a market only after its lender of record acknowledges the asset data that carries them.

## Lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    [Create Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/create-identity-terms) opens a draft, and [Update Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/update-identity-terms) saves it as it grows. A draft may be incomplete. [Get Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/get-identity-terms) lists what is still missing in `draftProblems`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check">
    [Check Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/check-identity-terms) validates the draft and judges each claim. Without a borrower gate it judges only what no gate could ever back. Name a borrower gate to judge every gate claim against it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    [Publish Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/publish-identity-terms) validates the whole document, refuses a claim no gate can ever back, and freezes it as version N with its core hash. The draft is cleared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite and sign">
    [Create Invite](/endpoints/lending/create-identity-terms-invite) returns a one-time signing link per member. You send it to the member yourself. The member opens it with [Read Signing Link](/endpoints/lending/read-identity-terms-invite), signs with its wallet through [Sign Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/sign-identity-terms), or declines with a reason through [Decline Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/decline-identity-terms).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach">
    Name the published version as `identityTerms: { id, version }` on [Deploy Market](/endpoints/lending/deploy-market), or later on [Attest Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/attest-asset-data). Each attach judges the terms against the borrower gate the market has, or will have, and refuses a claim it does not back. [Preview Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/preview-asset-data) runs the same check before anything is built.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Acknowledge">
    The lender of record reads the terms in the bundle, then acknowledges it: at adoption with `assetDataHash` on [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role), or later with [Acknowledge Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/acknowledge-asset-data).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revise">
    [Revise Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/revise-identity-terms) opens a draft of version N+1 that names the core hash of version N in `supersedes`. Publish it, collect the signatures again, and re-attest the markets that should carry it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw">
    [Withdraw Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/withdraw-identity-terms) is final. Markets keep the copy they attested and show the warning `TERMS_WITHDRAWN`. No new market can attach the terms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| `status` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `DRAFT` | No version is published yet |
| `PUBLISHED` | At least one version is published. `hasDraft` says whether a revision is open |
| `WITHDRAWN` | Final. No edit, revision, invite or attachment |

A few rules hold throughout:

- An instance keeps at most 50 identity terms documents.
- Only the latest published version can be attached. An older one is refused with `IDENTITY_TERMS_SUPERSEDED`. `version` in `identityTerms` confirms which version you reviewed.
- A published version never changes. A signature belongs to one version, so members sign each new version again.
- Publishing a new version revokes every open signing link of the document.
- A terms id of another instance answers `404 IDENTITY_TERMS_NOT_FOUND`.

The ten instance routes sit under `https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/identity-terms` and take an instance API key in `X-API-Key`, with the lending service enabled. Every one of them also exists on the Issuer Portal face under `/lending-external-securities-v2/identity-terms`, where the issuer session authenticates the call and `x-instance-id` names the instance. Both faces take the same bodies and give the same answers. Reads, the check included, count against the 100 per minute budget, and writes against the 10 per minute budget. See [Rate limits](/endpoints/lending/introduction#rate-limits).

The three signing routes sit under `https://api.trusset.org/public/identity-terms` and take no key: the token in the link is the authority. They allow 20 reads and 6 writes per minute from one address, accept a body of at most 32 KB, and answer with `Cache-Control: no-store`.

A refusal on the ten instance routes carries `error.code`, `error.message` and `error.details`. `details.remedy` says what to do next, `details.field` names the field at fault, and a validation refusal lists every problem in `details.problems`. The three signing routes carry no `details`: they put `remedy` and `field` directly on `error`, beside `code` and `message`, and their `503` answers carry only `code` and `message`.

## Schema

Schema `trusset.identity-terms/1.0.0`. A draft holds these fields as far as they are written. The platform sets `schema`, `version` and `supersedes`, and `requirementsExt` comes only from an upload. Every other field is yours. A field the schema does not name is refused with `IDENTITY_TERMS_UNKNOWN_FIELD`.

### Document

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `schema` | `trusset.identity-terms/1.0.0`. Set by the platform |
| `name` | Required. Text, at most 200 characters |
| `version` | Set by the platform when the version is published: 1, then one more on each revision |
| `supersedes` | Set by the platform on a revision: the core hash of the version it replaces. Absent on version 1 |
| `chainId` | Required. The chain of your instance, filled in when the draft is created. Another chain is refused with `IDENTITY_TERMS_WRONG_CHAIN` |
| `validFrom` | Required. `YYYY-MM-DD` |
| `validTo` | Optional. `YYYY-MM-DD`, on or after `validFrom`. A version past this date cannot be attached (`IDENTITY_TERMS_EXPIRED`) |
| `governingLaw` | Required. ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country code, such as `DE` |
| `members` | Required. 2 to 12 organisations. See [Members](#members) |
| `reliance` | Required. See [Reliance](#reliance) |
| `evidence` | Required. What the KYC provider's statement about an investor rests on: `KYC_STARK_ROOT` (the root of a [KYC-STARKs](/tools/kyc-starks/overview) proof bundle), `REGISTER_ATTESTATION` (an attestation on an identity register) or `OFFCHAIN_RECORD` (a record kept off chain) |
| `delivery` | Required. How the lender receives what `reliance.dataToLender` promises: `KYC_COURIER` (a sealed record through the [KYC Courier](/tools/kyc-courier/overview)), `VERIFIER_SYSTEM` (the KYC provider's own system) or `MANUAL` |
| `whitelistManager` | Required. Index of the member that lists wallets on a whitelist gate |
| `requirements` | Required. See [Requirements](#requirements) |
| `requirementsExt` | Optional. `{ uri, hash }` of a JSON extension the platform pinned. See [Requirements extension](#requirements-extension) |
| `onKycExpiry` | Required. `BLOCK_NEW_CREDIT`: an investor whose KYC expired receives no new credit |
| `disputeContact` | Required. Index of the member to contact about the terms |

Members are counted from 0. Every field that points at a member takes its index, and an index past the last member is refused with `IDENTITY_TERMS_MEMBER_INDEX`.

### Members

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `roles` | Required. One or more of `ISSUER`, `PLATFORM`, `REGISTER`, `CUSTODIAN`, `KYC_PROVIDER`, `LENDER_OF_RECORD`, `COLLATERAL_AGENT`, `SETTLEMENT_ASSET_ISSUER`, `DISTRIBUTOR` and `OTHER` |
| `author` | `true` on exactly one member: the organisation of your instance that publishes the terms. Its `wallet` must be one of your instance's verified wallets |
| `legalName` | Required. Text, at most 200 characters |
| `lei` | Optional. ISO 17442 Legal Entity Identifier, 20 characters with a valid check sum |
| `registerId` | Optional. Commercial register number, at most 60 letters, digits and separators, such as `HRB 12345 B` |
| `wallet` | Required. A non-zero EVM address. Each member has its own wallet, because each member signs for itself |
| `gdprRole` | Required. `CONTROLLER`, `JOINT_CONTROLLER`, `PROCESSOR` or `NOT_APPLICABLE` |
| `website` | Optional. An `https` address of at most 512 characters, without user name or password |

A member with the `KYC_PROVIDER` role needs an `lei` or a `registerId`, and `reliance.licence` must then state its `type` and `supervisor` (`IDENTITY_TERMS_KYC_PROVIDER_INCOMPLETE`).

### Reliance

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `obligedEntity` | Required. Index of the member that performs the KYC and stands behind it. It must hold the `KYC_PROVIDER` role |
| `basis` | Required. `GWG_17` (section 17 of the German Money Laundering Act), `AMLR` (the EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation) or `OTHER` |
| `basisNote` | Text, at most 500 characters. Required when `basis` is `OTHER` |
| `licence` | `{ type, supervisor, registerUrl }`. `type` and `supervisor` are text of at most 120 characters, `registerUrl` an `https` address of the supervisor's public register |
| `agreement` | Required. `{ status, documentHash }`. `status` is `NONE`, `OFFERED` or `SIGNED`. `documentHash` is the hash of the signed reliance agreement as `0x` and 64 hex characters: required when `SIGNED`, refused when `NONE` |
| `dataToLender` | Required. One or more of `IDENTIFICATION`, `VERIFICATION`, `BENEFICIAL_OWNER`, `PURPOSE_AND_NATURE`, `PEP_SANCTIONS_RESULT`, `SOURCE_OF_FUNDS` and `DOCUMENT_COPIES_ON_REQUEST` |
| `deliveryDays` | Required. Whole days, 0 to 90, within which the lender receives that data on request |
| `retentionYears` | Required. Whole years, 1 to 30, for which the obliged entity keeps the records |
| `reKycMonths` | Required. Whole months, 1 to 120, between two reviews of an investor |
| `sanctionsScreening` | Required. Index of the member that screens investors against sanctions lists |
| `ongoingMonitoring` | Required. Index of the member that monitors the business relationship |
| `travelRule` | Optional. Index of the member that handles the transfer information the Travel Rule requires |

### Requirements

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| `clientCategoryMin` | Required. The lowest client category admitted: `RETAIL` (investor class 1), `PROFESSIONAL` (class 2) or `ELIGIBLE_COUNTERPARTY` (class 3) |
| `personTypes` | Required. `NATURAL`, `LEGAL` or both |
| `residence` | Required. `{ mode, countries }`. `mode` is `ALLOW` or `BLOCK`, `countries` one or more ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes, each once |
| `checks` | Required. One or more standard predicates, listed below |
| `maxKycAgeDays` | Required. Whole days, 1 to 3650: the oldest KYC an investor may rest on |
| `enforcement` | Required. Who enforces each of the five rules above. See [Enforcement](#enforcement) |

`checks` takes the names of the standard predicates of the [identity hub](/endpoints/customers/introduction#predicates). Attester-defined predicates are not accepted in this schema version (`IDENTITY_TERMS_CHECK_NOT_ALLOWED`).

| Bit | Predicate |
|---|---|
| 0 | `KYC_DONE` |
| 1 | `AML_CLEAR` |
| 2 | `SANCTIONS_CLEAR` |
| 3 | `PEP_CLEAR` |
| 4 | `ADULT` |
| 5 | `EEA_RESIDENT` |
| 6 | `NOT_US_PERSON` |
| 7 | `CONTRACT_HOLDER` |
| 8 | `RESIDENCY_ON_FILE` |
| 9 | `CITIZENSHIP_ON_FILE` |
| 10 | `TAX_RESIDENCY_ON_FILE` |
| 11 | `ACCREDITED` |

### Enforcement

`requirements.enforcement` holds one entry for each of `clientCategoryMin`, `personTypes`, `residence`, `checks` and `maxKycAgeDays`. A missing entry is refused with `IDENTITY_TERMS_ENFORCEMENT_INCOMPLETE`.

| `by` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `BORROWER_GATE` | The market's borrower gate enforces the rule on chain |
| `MEMBER` | The member named in `member` checks it off chain |

Any other `by` is refused with `IDENTITY_TERMS_FIELD_INVALID`.

```json
{
  "clientCategoryMin": { "by": "BORROWER_GATE" },
  "personTypes": { "by": "MEMBER", "member": 1 },
  "residence": { "by": "MEMBER", "member": 1 },
  "checks": { "by": "BORROWER_GATE" },
  "maxKycAgeDays": { "by": "BORROWER_GATE" }
}
```

A claim that the borrower gate enforces a rule is checked against that gate. See [Checks and verdicts](#checks-and-verdicts).

### Requirements extension

Rules longer than the schema holds go into an extension: a JSON document of at most 256 KB. Send it as text in `requirementsExtJson` on [Update Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/update-identity-terms). The platform pins the exact bytes to IPFS and writes `requirementsExt: { uri, hash }` into the draft, where `hash` is keccak256 of those bytes. The core hash therefore covers the extension through its hash. A `requirementsExt` the platform did not pin for these terms is refused with `IDENTITY_TERMS_EXT_INVALID`. The extension states rules, never results or contact details, and is refused when it looks like it holds personal data.

### Text and limits

A published version holds every text field in Unicode NFC, with runs of white space folded into one space. A text field is refused when it:

- holds a control or invisible character, such as a zero-width space or a text direction mark (`IDENTITY_TERMS_TEXT_INVALID`);
- mixes letters of different alphabets, so it could look exactly like another name (`IDENTITY_TERMS_TEXT_INVALID`);
- looks like it holds an e-mail address, a phone number or an IBAN (`IDENTITY_TERMS_PERSONAL_DATA`).

Saving a draft already refuses control characters, personal data, a text longer than 612 characters and nesting deeper than 8 levels. Publishing checks everything else.

| Limit | Value |
|---|---|
| Members | 2 to 12 |
| Saved draft | 64 KB |
| Requirements extension | 256 KB |
| Terms documents per instance | 50 |
| Signing link validity | 14 days |
| Countries | ISO 3166-1 alpha-2, 249 codes. User-assigned codes such as `XK` are not accepted |

## Core hash

The core hash identifies one version. Members sign it, the reliance agreement cites it, and the asset data carries it.

```
coreHash = keccak256( utf8( RFC8785( prune(core) ) ) )
```

- `prune` drops `null`, empty strings, empty lists and empty objects, recursively.
- RFC 8785 (JSON Canonicalization Scheme) sorts the members of every object and writes no white space.
- A published core is already normalized, so it hashes to the same value wherever it is re-serialized: set-like lists stand in a fixed order (the enum order of the schema, countries sorted), addresses are lower case, the LEI is upper case, and `author: true` appears on the author alone.

The same rule hashes the asset data bundle, so one implementation serves both.

Or with ethers v5 alone. The values in a core are objects, arrays, strings, whole numbers and booleans, so this short canonicaliser is the whole of RFC 8785 that the core needs:

```typescript TypeScript
import { utils } from 'ethers';

function prune(value: unknown): unknown {
  if (Array.isArray(value)) return value.map(prune).filter(v => !isEmpty(v));
  if (value && typeof value === 'object') {
    const out: Record<string, unknown> = {};
    for (const [key, item] of Object.entries(value)) {
      const pruned = prune(item);
      if (!isEmpty(pruned)) out[key] = pruned;
    }
    return out;
  }
  return value;
}

function isEmpty(value: unknown): boolean {
  if (value === null || value === undefined || value === '') return true;
  if (Array.isArray(value)) return value.length === 0;
  return typeof value === 'object' && Object.keys(value as object).length === 0;
}

function canonical(value: unknown): string {
  if (Array.isArray(value)) return `[${value.map(canonical).join(',')}]`;
  if (value && typeof value === 'object') {
    const entries = Object.entries(value as Record<string, unknown>).sort(([a], [b]) => (a < b ? -1 : a > b ? 1 : 0));
    return `{${entries.map(([key, item]) => `${JSON.stringify(key)}:${canonical(item)}`).join(',')}}`;
  }
  return JSON.stringify(value);
}

const coreHash = utils.keccak256(utils.toUtf8Bytes(canonical(prune(core))));
```

## Checks and verdicts

A check judges each of the five rules. A rule enforced by a member is never checked on chain. A rule enforced by `BORROWER_GATE` is judged against the borrower gate.

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ENFORCED_ON_CHAIN` | The gate enforces the rule as the terms state it |
| `CHECKED_BY_MEMBER` | The terms name a member that checks the rule off chain. Nothing on chain is compared |
| `CLAIM_NOT_BACKED` | The terms say the borrower gate enforces the rule, and the gate does not |
| `GATE_UNREADABLE` | The gate could not be read, or its profile is inactive and admits nobody, so the verdict is not known |

Each verdict carries `rule`, `by`, `member` for a member, `gate` (always `borrower`), `gateKind` and `gateAddress` for the borrower gate, a plain `reason`, and a `remedy` on `CLAIM_NOT_BACKED`.

### Gate kinds

The check reads the borrower gate, then works out what it is. That is the register named on [Check Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/check-identity-terms), the borrower register a deployment installs, or the one the market carries.

| Kind | What it is | What it can back |
|---|---|---|
| `NONE` | No register is installed | Nothing |
| `PROFILE_GATE` | The gate of a profile on an instance ID register, confirmed through the identity register factory of the network | The profile's minimum investor class, required predicates and maximum attestation age |
| `INSTANCE_REGISTER` | An instance ID register created by that factory | Its base predicates. It reads with investor class 0 and no maximum age |
| `TREX_REGISTRY` | A T-REX identity registry, including the eligibility register of a verification profile. Its proxy, its identity storage, their implementation authority and both implementations must run the deployed code of a T-REX release Trusset recognises, the storage must list the registry, a wallet nobody registered must be refused, and its topics registry must require at least one claim topic with a trusted issuer | A completed KYC (`KYC_DONE`) and nothing else |
| `ACCESS_LIST` | A [whitelist](/endpoints/lending/list-access-lists) | Nothing: it lists wallets and checks no identity rule |
| `UNKNOWN` | Anything else | Nothing |

An address that answers like a profile gate but is not the gate of a profile on a register created by the factory reads as `UNKNOWN`. So does every profile gate where the factory is not configured for the network. The archived Trusset ID Register reads as `UNKNOWN` too. So does a contract that answers the T-REX getters (`identityStorage`, `issuersRegistry`, `topicsRegistry`) but fails any of the `TREX_REGISTRY` conditions: answering the getters proves nothing about who it admits, so it backs no rule.

### How each rule is judged

A borrower gate that is a T-REX registry backs a rule only when it is `checks` and lists `KYC_DONE` alone. On a profile gate or an instance register:

| Rule | Backed on chain when |
|---|---|
| `clientCategoryMin` | The gate's minimum investor class reaches the category: 1 for `RETAIL`, 2 for `PROFESSIONAL`, 3 for `ELIGIBLE_COUNTERPARTY`. Every admitted wallet holds class 1 at least, so any such gate backs `RETAIL` |
| `checks` | The gate requires every predicate the terms list |
| `maxKycAgeDays` | The gate sets a maximum attestation age, and it is no longer than `maxKycAgeDays` |
| `residence` | Either an `ALLOW` list that covers every EEA country and a gate that requires `EEA_RESIDENT`, or a `BLOCK` list of `US` alone and a gate that requires `NOT_US_PERSON`. No gate can check any other country list |
| `personTypes` | Never. No gate tells a natural person from a legal person |

An instance register is read with investor class 0 and no maximum age, so it never backs `PROFESSIONAL` or `maxKycAgeDays`. The eligibility register of a professional verification profile is a T-REX registry: admission keeps it professional, not the contract. Terms that claim a professional floor on it name a `MEMBER` instead.

### When it is judged

| Where | Against | What happens to `CLAIM_NOT_BACKED` |
|---|---|---|
| [Check Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/check-identity-terms) without a borrower gate | No gate: only what no gate of that kind can ever back | Reported. Other `BORROWER_GATE` rules read `verdict: null` |
| [Check Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/check-identity-terms) with a borrower gate | That gate, read with `readAt.block` 0 | Reported. `notBacked` counts them |
| [Publish Identity Terms](/endpoints/lending/publish-identity-terms) | The same static check | Refused with `409 IDENTITY_TERMS_CLAIM_NOT_BACKED`. `details.verdicts` holds only the claims no gate can back |
| [Preview Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/preview-asset-data) and [Deploy Market](/endpoints/lending/deploy-market) | The borrower register the deployment installs, read with `readAt.block` 0 | Refused with `409 IDENTITY_TERMS_CLAIM_NOT_BACKED`. `details.verdicts` holds all five verdicts |
| [Attest Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/attest-asset-data) | The market's borrower register at the current block | Refused with `409 IDENTITY_TERMS_CLAIM_NOT_BACKED`. `details.verdicts` holds all five verdicts |
| [Get Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/get-asset-data) and [Get Queued Market](/endpoints/operators/get-queued-market) | The market's live borrower register, cached for 60 seconds per market | Reported. `notBacked` counts them |

A verdict of `GATE_UNREADABLE` from an inactive profile is shown and never refused. A gate that cannot be read at all refuses an attach with `503 IDENTITY_TERMS_GATES_UNREADABLE`. Retry it. On Check Identity Terms it is no error: `gatesReadable` reads `false` and the verdicts `GATE_UNREADABLE`.

## Warnings

A check, an attach and every read carry `warnings`, each `{ code, message }`. A warning never stops a call. A check knows no market, so it carries only `KYC_PROVIDER_UNSIGNED` and `TERMS_WITHDRAWN`.

| Code | When |
|---|---|
| `RETAIL_ON_MARKET_PRICE` | Attach and reads only, where the market's price source is known. The terms admit `RETAIL` investors on a market priced from a live market price. Its free-hand realization path is open against professional borrowers only. See [Borrower register on a MARKET market](/endpoints/lending/deploy-market#borrower-register-on-a-market-market) |
| `KYC_PROVIDER_UNSIGNED` | The obliged entity has not signed this version |
| `GATES_CHANGED` | Reads only. A gate on chain differs in kind or address from the gate recorded when the terms were attached. `slots` names the gates: `borrower`, `provider` or both |
| `TERMS_WITHDRAWN` | The author withdrew the terms. The market keeps the copy it attested |
| `LENDER_MEMBER_MISMATCH` | Reads only. A member with the `LENDER_OF_RECORD` role names a wallet that is not the lender's. `members` lists the indices. Before adoption, the reading instance's wallets count as the lender's |

## Hub cross-check

Where the network carries the [identity hub](/endpoints/customers/introduction#the-instance-id-register), the check reads the attester profile the obliged entity's wallet registered there and compares its LEI hash with keccak256 of the `lei` the terms name.

| `hub.result` | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `MATCH` | The hub profile of that wallet carries the same LEI |
| `MISMATCH` | It carries another LEI, or the terms name none |
| `NOT_REGISTERED` | The wallet has no attester profile on the hub |
| `null` | The hub could not be read. `readable` is `false` |

`hub` is `null` when no hub is configured for the network, or when the obliged entity names no wallet. A result is information for the lender, never a refusal.

## Signing

A member signs EIP-712 typed data over the core hash, its own index and its own wallet:

```
domain: { name: "Trusset Identity Terms", version: "1", chainId }
IdentityTermsSignature {
  bytes32 coreHash
  uint16  member
  address wallet
}
```

`chainId` is the chain the version names. [Read Signing Link](/endpoints/lending/read-identity-terms-invite) returns the typed data ready for `eth_signTypedData_v4`.

- **A wallet without code** signs with its key. The signature must recover the member's wallet and be in canonical low-s form.
- **A contract wallet**, such as a Safe, an ERC-4337 account or an EIP-7702 account, is checked with EIP-1271 `isValidSignature` over the typed-data digest. The signature may be up to 4,096 bytes.

The stored signature carries `kind`: `EOA` or `ERC1271`.

**Signing links.** [Create Invite](/endpoints/lending/create-identity-terms-invite) returns the link once. Trusset sends nothing: you pass the link to the member over your own channel. A link is valid for 14 days and for one answer. A new link for a member revokes that member's open link. Requests for the same member and version are serialized, so at most one open link per member and version holds even when they arrive in parallel. [Revoke Invite](/endpoints/lending/revoke-identity-terms-invite) sets an open link to `REVOKED`; a link that has already expired stays `EXPIRED`, and revoking it changes nothing and writes no audit entry. A refused signature does not use up the link. A link stops working when it is revoked, when the terms are withdrawn, or when a newer version is published.

**Declining.** A member that will not sign declines with a reason: `NOT_OUR_ROLE`, `WRONG_DATA` or `NOT_AGREED`. The author sees the reason and can send a new link, for example after a revision. A signature cannot be declined afterwards.

**Signatures after attaching.** The bundle carries the signatures on record when it is attested. A signature given later reaches the market only through a new attestation. The author's read of the market counts such signatures in `pendingSignatures`. Re-attest the same version with [Attest Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/attest-asset-data) to carry them.

## In the asset data

An attached version becomes the `identity` member of the market's asset data bundle, which then follows schema `1.3.0`. Schema `1.3.0` also carries the market's documents.

```json identity
{
  "terms": { "schema": "trusset.identity-terms/1.0.0", "name": "TokenForge investors under Concedus", "version": 1 },
  "coreHash": "0x8f380a6ce6479c4c1d957bc31fd844d2135dccfc10864fe9d06135b5246041aa",
  "signatures": [
    {
      "member": 0,
      "wallet": "0x1111111111111111111111111111111111111111",
      "kind": "EOA",
      "signature": "0x4a1f...1b",
      "signedAt": "2026-11-25T14:02:11Z"
    }
  ],
  "gates": {
    "borrower": { "address": "0x7d3a91c4e05b28f6a1c9d0e7b4f2a86c3e5d1b09", "kind": "PROFILE_GATE" },
    "provider": { "kind": "NONE" },
    "readAt": { "chainId": 11155111, "block": 0 }
  }
}
```

| Field | Contents |
|---|---|
| `terms` | The whole published core, as signed. Shortened above, as is the signature |
| `coreHash` | Its core hash. A bundle whose `terms` do not hash to it is refused |
| `signatures` | The signatures on record when the bundle was built, sorted by member, each `{ member, wallet, kind, signature, signedAt }`. `signedAt` is UTC in whole seconds. Left out when nobody has signed |
| `gates` | The gates read when the terms were attached: `borrower` and `provider`, each `{ address, kind }` or `{ kind: "NONE" }`, and `readAt: { chainId, block }`. Before deployment the borrower gate comes from the deployment parameters, `provider` is recorded as `{ kind: "NONE" }` and `block` is `0`, so the preview and the deployment produce the same bytes while no member signs in between. The signatures on record at that moment are part of the member, so a later signature changes the hash, and the Issuer Portal previews again |

`provider` is recorded so that a later change shows, and no rule is judged against it.

**A bundle without identity terms keeps its schema and its hash.** It is built under `1.1.0`, or `1.2.0` with documents, exactly as before. Only a bundle that carries `identity` uses `1.3.0`.

**Re-attesting.** On [Attest Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/attest-asset-data):

| `identityTerms` | Effect |
|---|---|
| Left out | The standing member is carried over byte for byte, as documents are |
| `null` | The member is removed |
| `{ id, version }` | That version is attached, checked against the market's borrower register at the current block |

A re-attest of the same version whose gates and signatures have not changed reuses the standing member, so `ATTESTATION_CURRENT` still answers a re-attest that would change nothing. `identityChanges` in the answer names what moves before anything is signed.

### What the lender reads

[Get Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/get-asset-data#identity-terms) and [Get Queued Market](/endpoints/operators/get-queued-market) carry an `identity` section whenever the attested bundle holds terms. It shows the terms and the members with their signatures, the gates recorded at attachment beside the live gates, the verdicts of the live check, the warnings and the hub result.

`changedSinceAccepted` compares the identity member of the attested bundle with the one in the acknowledged bundle. `true` means the terms, their signatures or their recorded gates changed since the lender's acknowledgement, and the market reads `DIVERGED`. `null` means nothing was acknowledged yet.

Before it acknowledges, the lender of record reads at least:

1. `identity.terms`: the obliged entity, the reliance basis and agreement status, `dataToLender`, `delivery` and the requirements.
2. `identity.verdicts` and `notBacked`: which rules a gate enforces and which a member checks.
3. `identity.warnings`, `KYC_PROVIDER_UNSIGNED` and `LENDER_MEMBER_MISMATCH` in particular.
4. `identity.hub`: whether the obliged entity's wallet carries the LEI the terms name.
5. `identity.coreHash`: the value its reliance agreement with the obliged entity cites.

Acknowledging the bundle hash acknowledges the terms with it. If the issuer later attaches other terms, the market reads `DIVERGED` until the lender acknowledges again.

## Audit

Every write is recorded in the instance's audit log: created, updated, published, revised, withdrawn, invite created, invite revoked, signed, declined, and attached once per attestation. A record holds ids, versions, hashes, member indices and the wallets of member organisations. It never holds a name.
