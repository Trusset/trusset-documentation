---
title: 'Get Pending Liquidations'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/liquidations/{marketId}/pending-liquidations'
description: 'List liquidations awaiting settlement'
---

Returns the market's liquidation records that are still open. Most are router records whose collateral sits at the router awaiting the manual settlement workflow. A record whose Dutch auction is still open appears too, under a `venue-` ID, until the auction sells out or hands its remainder to the router.

The list also carries records the market wrote off after its 7 day timeout while their router record is still open. They read `status: WRITTEN_OFF` and `writtenOff: true`. Their collateral can still be withdrawn, sold and settled. The proceeds reach the pool as late proceeds, and anything above the debt and the disclosed penalty still goes to the borrower, as on every realization. Do not call [Handle Liquidation Timeout](/endpoints/lending/handle-timeout) on such a row: the market has already written it off, and the call is refused with `ALREADY_SETTLED`.

Before reading, the endpoint reconciles against the chain. It adopts any liquidation the market recorded that the backend has not: a router record, and a liquidation that went to the Dutch auction venue, which is adopted from the market's own record under its `venue-` ID while its auction runs or after the auction sold the whole lot. A liquidation triggered outside this API, or confirmed only on another instance that shares the market, therefore appears here on the next read, dated by its seizure block and carrying the market's seized figures. It also closes records the router or the venue has settled since, including atomic sales, when the confirm call for that settlement never arrived, and dates them by the settlement block. `sync` reports how that went. The router records, the market's records and the auction cash are then read in one batched call.

A read never answers an empty list as an all-clear while the market holds a liquidation that no record here shows: `sync.state` is then `BEHIND` and `sync.unrecordedOpen` counts them.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/lending/list-markets), not its contract address. See [Market IDs](/endpoints/lending/list-markets#market-ids). At most 100 characters.</ParamField>

## On-Chain Data

`onChainData` is the authoritative view of what the router still holds.

<ResponseField name="onChainData" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="collateralAmount" type="string">Collateral originally received.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="collateralRemaining" type="string">Collateral still held. Decreases as it is withdrawn for sale.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="debtOwed" type="string">Debt to be covered, in borrow asset units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="proceedsCollected" type="string">Proceeds deposited so far, in borrow asset units. Increases with each reported partial sale.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="borrower" type="string">Borrower address.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="lendingMarket" type="string">Contract that delivered the collateral: the market, or its auction venue for the remainder of an expired auction.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="token" type="string">Collateral token.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="routerAddress" type="string">Router holding the position.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="timestamp" type="integer">Unix seconds the router received the collateral. This is when the 7 day write-off clock starts. For the remainder of an expired auction the clock started earlier, at the seizure into the auction.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="settled" type="boolean">Whether the router considers the liquidation closed.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="marketLiquidationId" type="string">Market-local ID.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

`onChainData: null` with `routerReadFailed: true` means the router record could not be read. With `routerReadFailed: false` on a response whose `readFailed` is `false`, it means the router has no such record.

Written-off rows also carry the router figures read when the list was built: `routerAddress`, `collateralRemaining`, `proceedsCollected`, `routerSettled`, `collateralStranded`, `reopenable`, `collateralAfterWriteOff` and `routerOpenAfterWriteOff`.

<Note>
  Track progress with `collateralRemaining` and `proceedsCollected` rather than `status`. Withdrawing collateral for sale and reporting partial sales both leave `status` at `PENDING`. Only settlement, a router close or a write-off changes it. A written-off row keeps `WRITTEN_OFF` through a later sale and settlement, and leaves this list once its router record closes.
</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/liquidations/{marketId}/pending-liquidations" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here"
```
```typescript TypeScript
const res = await fetch(
  `https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/liquidations/${marketId}/pending-liquidations`,
  { headers: { 'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here' } }
);
const { success, data, error } = await res.json();
if (!success) throw new Error(`${error.code}: ${error.message}`);

const dueForWriteOff = data.pendingLiquidations.filter((row) => !row.writtenOff && row.writeOff?.eligible);
const stillToSell = data.pendingLiquidations.filter((row) => row.writtenOff);
```
</RequestExample>

`writeOff` runs on the market's own clock, which for an auction remainder started before the router received the collateral. When the market record cannot be read, it falls back to the router's clock, which starts later. The `eligibleAt` that [Handle Liquidation Timeout](/endpoints/lending/handle-timeout) returns when it refuses is exact.

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `PENDING_READ_FAILED` | `503` | The records could not be read and there is nothing to show. Retry shortly |

A partial failure with rows to show answers `200` with `readFailed: true` instead.
