---
title: 'Approve Loan Request'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/loan-requests/{ref}/approve'
description: 'Approve a loan request with an on-chain approval'
---

Builds the `approve` transaction on the market's approvals contract, and confirms it on a second call with `txHash`. Trusset never signs: the market admin or a wallet holding `LOAN_APPROVER_ROLE` signs the calldata.

One approval admits one draw of at most the approved amount until `validUntil`. The draw consumes it.

## Who can call

The lender of record.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>
  The market's record ID on your instance.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="ref" type="string" required>
  The loan request reference: `lrq_` followed by 20 characters.
</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="amount" type="string | number">
  The amount to approve. Defaults to the requested amount and may only be lower.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="validHours" type="number" default="72">
  How long the approval stays valid, from 1 to 696 hours.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signerAddress" type="string">
  The wallet that will sign.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  Hash of the transaction you broadcast. Send it to confirm the transaction and record the result. Omit it to receive the calldata.
</ParamField>

## An approval in force is never replaced

While a usable approval is on chain for the request's borrower and loan, the build answers `409 APPROVAL_IN_FORCE`, and `error.details.approved` carries `{ amount, amountRaw, validUntil }` of that approval. Revoke it first with [Revoke Loan Approval](/endpoints/lending/revoke-loan-approval), then approve again. The revoke fails once the borrower drew, so an approval is never changed under a loan that is being opened.

A lapsed approval, one past its `validUntil`, can be replaced directly.

When the request reads `APPROVED` but its approval is no longer on chain, it was used or revoked. The build answers `409 APPROVAL_NOT_ON_CHAIN`, and a further loan needs a new request.

## Confirming

An approval is recorded only on the request it was signed for. The confirm answers `400 TX_NOT_VERIFIED` when the transaction is older than the approval already recorded on the request, or was signed before the request existed.

A mined approval can still be confirmed on a request that was withdrawn, declined or closed in the meantime, as long as that approval is still in force on chain. Otherwise the confirm answers `409 APPROVAL_NOT_ON_CHAIN`. A `USED` or `REVOKED` request takes no approval and answers `409 LOAN_REQUEST_STATE`.

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `AMOUNT_ABOVE_REQUEST` | `400` | `amount` is above the requested amount. Approve that amount or less |
| `INVALID_AMOUNT` | `400` | `amount` is zero |
| `INVALID_VALIDITY` | `400` | `validHours` is not between 1 and 696 |
| `TX_REVERTED` | `400` | The transaction reverted on chain |
| `TX_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | The transaction emits no `LoanApproved` for this borrower and loan from the market's approvals contract, it is older than the approval already recorded on the request, or it was signed before the request existed |
| `LOAN_APPROVAL_BANK_ONLY` | `403` | Only the lender of record approves |
| `SIGNER_NOT_AUTHORIZED` | `403` | `signerAddress` is not a verified wallet of your instance that holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` or `LOAN_APPROVER_ROLE` on the market |
| `APPROVAL_NOT_INSTANCE_BOUND` | `403` | The approval was signed by a wallet that is not verified for your instance |
| `TX_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | The transaction is not found or not mined yet |
| `APPROVAL_IN_FORCE` | `409` | A usable approval is on chain for this borrower and loan. `details.approved` carries `{ amount, amountRaw, validUntil }`. Revoke it first, then approve again |
| `APPROVAL_NOT_ON_CHAIN` | `409` | On the build call, the request reads `APPROVED` but its approval was used or revoked: a further loan needs a new request. On the confirm call, the approval of a withdrawn, declined or closed request is no longer in force on chain |
| `LOAN_REQUEST_STATE` | `409` | The build call takes a `REQUESTED`, `APPROVED` or `EXPIRED` request only. On the confirm call, the request is `USED` or `REVOKED` and takes no approval, or it changed in the meantime |
| `APPROVAL_WOULD_REVERT` | `409` | The approvals contract would refuse this approval as it stands, so nothing was built |
| `LOAN_APPROVAL_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The approvals of the market could not be read just now, so nothing was decided |
