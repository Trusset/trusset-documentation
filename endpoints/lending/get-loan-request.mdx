---
title: 'Get Loan Request'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/loan-requests/{ref}'
description: 'Read one loan request'
---

Returns one loan request with its status, the approval once it is given, and what your side does next.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>
  The market's record ID on your instance.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="ref" type="string" required>
  The loan request reference: `lrq_` followed by 20 characters.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data.request" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="ref" type="string">`lrq_` followed by 20 characters.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="kind" type="string">`OPEN` for a new loan, `BORROW_MORE` for an additional draw on an open loan.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="loanId" type="string | null">The loan an additional draw belongs to. `null` for a new loan.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="status" type="string">`REQUESTED`, `APPROVED`, `USED`, `DECLINED`, `WITHDRAWN`, `REVOKED`, `EXPIRED` or `CLOSED`. See [States](/endpoints/lending/loan-approvals#states).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="marketAddress" type="string">The market contract.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="borrowerWallet" type="string">The borrowing wallet.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="externalReference" type="string | null">Your own case number, if given.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amount" type="string">The requested amount, as a decimal string.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amountRaw" type="string">The requested amount in base units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="collateralAmount" type="string | null">The collateral amount named in the request, if any.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="symbol" type="string">Symbol of the borrow asset.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="decimals" type="number">Decimals of the borrow asset.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="approvedAmount" type="string | null">The approved amount, once approved.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="approvedAmountRaw" type="string | null">The approved amount in base units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="validUntil" type="string | null">When the approval runs out, as an ISO 8601 timestamp.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="approver" type="string | null">The wallet that signed the approval.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="approveTxHash" type="string | null">The approval transaction.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="decidedAt" type="string | null">When the lender of record decided.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="usedTxHash" type="string | null">The transaction that consumed the approval.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="usedAt" type="string | null">When the approval was consumed.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="createdAt" type="string">ISO 8601 timestamp.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="updatedAt" type="string">ISO 8601 timestamp.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="requestedByThisInstance" type="boolean">Whether your instance made the request.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="viewerRole" type="object">`{ bank, borrower }` for your instance.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="nextStep" type="string">What your side does next: `APPROVE_OR_DECLINE`, `WAIT_FOR_BANK`, `OPEN_LOAN`, `WAIT_FOR_BORROWER` or `NONE`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>
