---
title: 'Add Liquidity'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/{marketId}/add-liquidity'
description: 'Deposit the borrow asset into the pool directly and receive LP shares'
---

Deposits the market's borrow asset into the lending pool directly. The depositor receives LP shares at the current share price, and those shares accrue value as borrowers pay interest.

Two transactions are required: an ERC-20 approval for exactly the amount, then the deposit. Both come back as ordered steps.

## Who can deposit directly

On current markets only two kinds of address can call `addLiquidity`: the market's operator wallet and the vaults the market admin registered. The operator wallet starts as the wallet that took the lender-of-record role and moves only through [Rotate Operator Wallet](/endpoints/lending/rotate-operator-wallet). Read it as `operatorWallet` on [Get Market Metrics](/endpoints/lending/get-metrics), where `vaultOnlyLiquidity: true` marks a market under this rule.

Every other liquidity provider funds the market through one of the operator's vaults, with [Deposit](/endpoints/vaults/deposit) or [Deposit to Vault](/endpoints/lending/deposit-to-vault). The vault checks the depositor against its own register and the provider register of each market it funds. A direct deposit from any other wallet reverts on-chain, so the API refuses it first with `LIQUIDITY_VAULT_ONLY`.

No liquidity can be added to a `MARKET`-priced market until its borrower register qualifies as a professional gate: at least one verification profile admits wallets to it, and every profile that does, on any instance of the market's network and environment, is limited to professional clients and has its admissions signed by its own instance. Until then a deposit is refused with `409 PROFESSIONAL_BORROWER_GATE_REQUIRED`, also from the operator wallet. The check runs after the depositor checks, and the market contract does not apply it. See [Professional borrowers](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#professional-borrowers).

A market still running an implementation from before this rule answers with its own gate instead. `LIQUIDITY_OPERATOR_ONLY` means it takes liquidity only from its lender of record and the providers it admitted. `IDENTITY_REGISTRY_REQUIRED` means it is open to third parties but has no provider register yet. `IDENTITY_NOT_VERIFIED` means the wallet is not verified on the register the market checks, and `ELIGIBILITY_REMOVAL_PENDING` that its admission to that register is waiting for removal.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/lending/list-markets), not its contract address. See [Market IDs](/endpoints/lending/list-markets#market-ids).</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="amount" type="string">
  Amount to deposit as a positive decimal string, for example `"250000"`. Denominated in the market's borrow asset and parsed at its decimals. Required when building calldata. The confirm call needs only `txHash`: the recorded amount comes from the mined transaction, and an `amount` sent alongside is validated but not used.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="provider" type="string">
  Wallet that will sign the deposit. Required when building calldata on a current market: without it the deposit cannot be checked against the operator wallet and the registered vaults, and the call returns `PROVIDER_REQUIRED`. On a market running an earlier implementation it is optional, and supplying it runs that market's own gate before a transaction is offered. When supplied, the API also checks that the wallet holds `amount` of the borrow asset, and refuses with `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` when it holds less. Not needed on the confirm call, where the provider is taken from the mined transaction.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  Hash of the transaction you broadcast for this operation. Send it to confirm the transaction and record the result. Omit it to receive the calldata.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="action" type="string">`SIGN_TRANSACTIONS` when `txHash` is omitted.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="steps" type="array">Ordered transactions to broadcast. Returned when `txHash` is omitted. The first approves the market to pull exactly `amount` of the borrow asset, the second calls `addLiquidity`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="confirmStepIndex" type="integer">Index of the step to confirm with. Always `1`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="confirmWith" type="object">`{ endpoint, field }`: this route, with `txHash` as the field. Returned when `txHash` is omitted.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="txHash" type="string">Deposit transaction hash. Returned when confirming with `txHash`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="amount" type="string">Amount actually deposited, read from the `LiquidityAdded` event rather than from the request. Returned when confirming with `txHash`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## First-deposit minimum

The first deposit into an empty pool must be at least 1,000 whole units of the borrow asset, for example 1,000 USDC. Current markets expose the figure as `minInitialDeposit()`. A market first deployed before per-asset scaling existed applies 1,000,000,000 smallest units, which is 1,000 of a 6-decimal asset.

The same floor applies to the deposit that recapitalises a pool whose value bad debt has fully consumed. It is a dust floor on the first share issue. Later deposits have no minimum beyond the token's own precision.

<Warning>
  The minimum is checked when the calldata is built, not when you confirm. Broadcasting an undersized first deposit reverts on-chain rather than returning `BELOW_MIN_DEPOSIT`. `totalShares` on [Get Market Metrics](/endpoints/lending/get-metrics) reads `0.0` on an empty pool.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Depositors receive shares at the current share price, which already includes accrued interest. Depositing does not dilute existing providers.
</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/{marketId}/add-liquidity" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"amount": "250000", "provider": "0x2b6ed29a95753c3ad948348e3e7b1a251080ffb9"}'
```
```typescript TypeScript
const url = `https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/${marketId}/add-liquidity`;
const headers = {
  'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here',
  'Content-Type': 'application/json'
};

const res = await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ amount: '250000', provider: await operatorWallet.getAddress() })
});
const { data } = await res.json();

const hashes: string[] = [];
for (const step of data.steps) {
  const tx = await operatorWallet.sendTransaction(step.transaction);
  await tx.wait();
  hashes.push(tx.hash);
}

await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ amount: '250000', txHash: hashes[data.confirmStepIndex] })
});
```
</RequestExample>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `NO_MARKET_ADDRESS` | `400` | The market has no on-chain address recorded |
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `amount` is missing without `txHash`, is not a positive decimal string or carries more decimal places than the borrow asset supports, or `provider` or `txHash` is malformed |
| `BELOW_MIN_DEPOSIT` | `400` | This is the first deposit into an empty or fully consumed pool and it is under the floor |
| `MARKET_PAUSED` | `400` | The market is paused. Its admin must unpause it |
| `PROVIDER_REQUIRED` | `400` | `provider` was omitted on a market that takes direct deposits only from its operator wallet and registered vaults |
| `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` | `400` | `provider` holds less of the borrow asset than `amount`, so the deposit would revert after the approval. `details` carries `payer`, `balance` and `required`. Skipped when the balance cannot be read |
| `IDENTITY_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | Earlier implementations only. `provider` is not verified on the register the market checks. On the single-register implementation `details` carries `register`, `requiredProfile` and `accessList`, and the message names the verification profile or whitelist behind the register when there is one |
| `IDENTITY_GATE_RETIRED` | `409` | Earlier implementations only. Returned instead of `IDENTITY_NOT_VERIFIED` when the register that refuses `provider` is the previous Trusset ID Register, which takes no new identities. `details` carries `register`, `gate`, `retired: true` and `currentRegistry`. `gate` is `identity` on the single-register implementation, where the admin first moves the market on with [Upgrade Market](/endpoints/lending/upgrade-market), and `provider` on a market that reads its provider register itself. The admin then installs the current register with [Set Identity Gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates#the-previous-trusset-id-register) |
| `LIQUIDITY_VAULT_ONLY` | `403` | `provider` is neither the operator wallet nor a registered vault. Deposit through one of the operator's vaults |
| `LIQUIDITY_OPERATOR_ONLY` | `403` | Earlier implementations only. The market takes liquidity only from its lender of record and the providers it admitted |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `MARKET_PENDING_CURATOR` | `409` | The market has no lender of record yet. See [Lender of record](/endpoints/lending/introduction#lender-of-record) |
| `IDENTITY_REGISTRY_REQUIRED` | `409` | Earlier implementations only. The pool is open to third parties but carries no provider register yet |
| `ELIGIBILITY_REMOVAL_PENDING` | `409` | Earlier implementations only. `provider` is verified on the register the market checks, but its admission is waiting for removal, so no new liquidity is prepared for it. `details` carries `register` and `wallet` |
| `PROFESSIONAL_BORROWER_GATE_REQUIRED` | `409` | The market is `MARKET`-priced and its borrower register does not qualify as a professional gate. `details.field` is `borrowerRegistry` |
| `MARKET_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market could not be read to check the first-deposit minimum, whether it is paused, who may deposit, or its price source and borrower register. Retry shortly |
| `STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The verification profiles behind a `MARKET` market's borrower register, or the pending removals on the register that checks `provider`, could not be read. Retry shortly |

A deposit that reached the market through another contract, such as a multisig wallet, confirms from the market's `LiquidityAdded` event in the receipt. Confirming with `txHash` can also return any [transaction verification error](/endpoints/introduction#confirm-a-transaction).
