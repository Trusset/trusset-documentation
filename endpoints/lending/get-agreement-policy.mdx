---
title: 'Get Agreement Policy'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/agreement-policy'
description: 'Read whether loan agreements are off, optional or required on a market'
---

Returns the market's effective E-Signatures policy and where it comes from. A market without a policy reads `OFF` with `source: DEFAULT`. When the bank's standing policy carries agreement settings and the market was adopted after they were saved, the first read creates the market's policy from it (`source: STANDING_POLICY`). See [E-Signatures](/endpoints/lending/loan-agreements#standing-policies).

## Who can read

An API key of any instance that holds the market. `viewer` says whether your instance is the market's bank and, for the bank, whether its plan includes E-Signatures.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>
  The market's record ID on your instance.
</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="policy.mode" type="string">`OFF`, `OPTIONAL` or `REQUIRED`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.validityDays" type="number">How long an issued `LOAN` agreement's offer stays open, in days.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.autoIssue" type="boolean">Whether a request inside the market rules is issued at once.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.autoIssueMaxAmount" type="string | null">With `autoIssue`, the largest borrow amount issued at once, in the borrow asset. `null` means no ceiling.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.frameworkValidityDays" type="number | null">How long a `FRAMEWORK` agreement runs when the bank creates one without `validUntil`. `null` means no default.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.requestAccess" type="string">`ANY` when every instance with access may request agreements, `BANK_ONLY` when only the bank creates them.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.source" type="string">`DEFAULT`, `MANUAL` (set on this market) or `STANDING_POLICY`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.standingPolicyId" type="string | null">The standing policy the settings came from.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.setByWallet" type="string | null">The bank's admin wallet that set the policy, or the adopting wallet.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.setByThisInstance" type="boolean">Whether your instance set it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.enforcement" type="string">Always `BACKEND_CHECK`: `REQUIRED` is checked by Trusset before it builds a transaction, not by the contract.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.issuing" type="string | null">`AVAILABLE` while the bank's plan includes E-Signatures, `UNAVAILABLE` when it no longer does, `null` when `OFF` or when the plan could not be read. While `UNAVAILABLE`, no new agreement can be issued and requests answer `AGREEMENT_ISSUING_UNAVAILABLE`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.notice" type="string | null">The enforcement note in `REQUIRED` mode, or, while `issuing` is `UNAVAILABLE`, what that means for new loans and what the bank can do.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.viewer" type="object">`{ isBank, canConfigure, planAllows }`. `planAllows` is answered for the bank only.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="policy.standingPolicyPending" type="object">For the bank: a matching standing policy carries agreement settings but did not apply, with `reason` (`ADOPTED_BEFORE_POLICY`, `PLAN`, `POLICY_OFF`).</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `AGREEMENT_POLICY_UNREADABLE` | `503` | The policy could not be read just now. Nothing was decided. Retry shortly |
