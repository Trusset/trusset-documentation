---
title: 'Repay Loan'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/{marketId}/repay'
description: 'Repay loan debt, interest first then principal'
---

Repays part or all of a loan's outstanding debt. Accrued interest is settled before principal. A repayment that clears the whole debt closes the loan in the same transaction and releases the collateral, so no [Close Loan](/endpoints/lending/close-loan) call follows it.

Anyone can repay any loan through the approve-then-repay steps, so a third party can rescue a position approaching liquidation. The single-transaction permit path is the borrower's own. A repayment sent without a `signedPrice` reads no price, so neither a stale price nor a paused market blocks it.

<Warning>
  To close a loan, repay with `all: true`. A payment of exactly the debt quoted earlier leaves the interest accrued since then outstanding. On a market with a repayment fee it also leaves a fee-sized remainder open. The loan then stays active and keeps accruing.
</Warning>

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>Market ID.</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="loanId" type="integer" required>
  On-chain loan ID. A positive integer. Accepted as a JSON number or as a numeric string, so the value [Get Loan](/endpoints/lending/get-loan) returns passes straight back.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="string">
  Amount to pay, as a positive decimal string in borrow asset units. The contract caps the payment at the debt plus the repayment fee, so overpaying does not transfer more than that. Required unless `all` is `true` or you are confirming with `txHash`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="all" type="boolean">
  Repay the loan in full. The API reads the current debt and adds a 20 basis point accrual buffer plus the repayment fee to the offer. Interest accruing between the quote and the mine therefore cannot leave dust behind. Use this instead of computing a payoff yourself.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signedPrice" type="object">
  EIP-712 signed price from [Sign Price](/endpoints/lending/sign-price), carried on-chain by the repayment. A repayment is the most frequent transaction a market sees, so carrying a price here keeps the oracle fresh without a separate write. Omit it and the repayment stays on the entry point that no price can block, which is the path a borrower must always have. It is not carried on the permit path. On a market that prices from an external feed, leave it out. That oracle refuses attested prices, so the repayment reverts on-chain, and this endpoint does not check for it.
  <Expandable>
    <ParamField body="price" type="string">Price in base units.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="timestamp" type="integer">Signing timestamp.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="validUntil" type="integer">Signature expiry.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="signature" type="string">EIP-712 signature.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="permit" type="object">
  An EIP-2612 permit over the settlement asset, signed by the borrower with the market as spender. It replaces the approval, so the repayment is one transaction. Get the payload to sign from [Get Permit Quote](/endpoints/lending/get-permit-quote). See [Pay with a permit](#pay-with-a-permit) for the binding rules.
  <Expandable>
    <ParamField body="deadline" type="integer" required>Unix timestamp in seconds that the permit was signed with. It must still be in the future.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="v" type="integer" required>Signature recovery id, `27` or `28`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="r" type="string" required>32 bytes, hex.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="s" type="string" required>32 bytes, hex.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="value" type="string">The amount the permit was signed over, as a decimal string in borrow asset units. Required with `all: true`. With `amount` it may be omitted, and when sent it must equal `amount`.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  Hash of the transaction you broadcast for this operation. Send it to confirm the transaction and record the result. Omit it to receive the calldata.
</ParamField>

## Repayment fee

A market may carry a flat repayment fee, stamped when it was deployed and never changeable afterwards. Read it from `transactionFees` on [Get Fee Split](/endpoints/lending/get-fee-split). The payer bears it. The fee comes out of the payment first, and only the rest reduces the debt.

Each charge is capped at 0.5 percent of the debt it discharges. The contract caps a payment at the debt plus `min(repayFee, debt / 200)`, which is exactly what closes the loan. A partial payment reduces the debt by the payment less a fee of at most one 201st of it. When the fee is not zero, the calldata response carries `transactionFee` and states the effect in `warning`.

## Pay with a permit

The permit path exists only where the settlement asset provably implements EIP-2612. `permitSupported` on the standard response says whether this market's asset qualifies. It is probed once per settlement asset and stored on the market, not asked per request. A token with a different permit shape, such as DAI's, reads as unsupported, and a permit sent to it is refused with `PERMIT_UNSUPPORTED`.

The permit must be signed by the loan's borrower, name the market as spender, and carry the token's current nonce and a future deadline. The borrower's wallet also sends the transaction, because the market redeems the permit for the sender. A third party repays through the approve-then-repay steps instead.

`repayWithPermit` binds the permit to the amount it repays, so the signed value decides what the calldata carries:

- With `amount`, the permit covers exactly `amount`. A `permit.value` that differs is refused with `INVALID_PERMIT`.
- With `all: true`, `permit.value` is required, or the call returns `PERMIT_VALUE_REQUIRED`. It becomes the amount the calldata offers, and it must cover the live debt plus the repayment fee, or the call returns `INVALID_PERMIT`.
- A full-repayment value inside the 20 basis point buffer builds with a `warning`. If interest outgrows it before the transaction mines, the market takes the permit's value as a partial repayment and the loan stays open.

Before any calldata is offered, the API recovers the signature against the token's domain and the borrower's nonce. A signature that does not recover to the borrower is refused with `INVALID_PERMIT`. When the token's domain or the nonce cannot be read, the call returns `PERMIT_UNVERIFIABLE`.

## Transaction Shape

A partial repayment returns two steps: `approve` on the borrow asset, then `repay`.

A full repayment (`all: true`) returns three. The third resets the allowance to zero. `repay` caps the pull at what closes the loan, so without it the unused approval would stay live against the payer's funds after the loan closed.

With a `permit`, the approval disappears. A partial repayment is one step, `repayWithPermit`. A full repayment is two: `repayWithPermit`, then the same allowance reset, because the permit installs the whole signed value as an allowance and the repayment does not spend all of it.

The `repay` transaction carries a `gasLimit` of `600000` and `repayWithPermit` carries `650000`.

<Warning>
  The confirmable step is not always the last one, and it is not always at the same index. Read `confirmStepIndex` from the response rather than assuming a position. It is `1` on the approve-then-repay path and `0` on the permit path, and in both cases it names the step whose `functionName` starts with `repay`. Confirming with the allowance-reset hash returns `TX_WRONG_TARGET`.
</Warning>

  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="success" type="boolean">`true`, nested inside `data` on the calldata response.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="action" type="string">`SIGN_TRANSACTIONS`. Returned when `txHash` is omitted.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="steps" type="array">Ordered transactions to broadcast. Returned when `txHash` is omitted.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fullRepayment" type="boolean">Whether the payload settles the loan in full.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="confirmStepIndex" type="integer">Index of the step to confirm with. `1` on the approve-then-repay path, `0` on the permit path. Read it rather than assuming.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="confirmWith" type="string">`repay`, on both paths.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="permitSupported" type="boolean">Whether the settlement asset implements EIP-2612, so a `permit` could have been sent instead. Present on the approve-then-repay path.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="permitUsed" type="boolean">`true` on the permit path. Absent otherwise.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="outstandingDebt" type="string">Principal plus accrued interest at the time the payload was built, without the repayment fee.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="repayAmount" type="string">Amount the repay step offers. On a full repayment it includes the accrual buffer and the fee. On the permit path it is the permit's value.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="transactionFee" type="object">The repayment fee, present only when it is not zero. Carries `kind: "REPAY"`, `fee`, `feeRaw` and `message`, plus `debtReduction` on a partial repayment. On a full repayment `fee` is computed on the debt at build time.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="collateralRelease" type="object">Present on a full repayment of a `CUSTODY` loan when the collateral token would refuse to return the collateral, or when its verdict cannot be read. The repayment still succeeds, and the adapter escrows the collateral instead of delivering it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="warning" type="string">One string joining whichever apply: the permit headroom warning, the fee message and the collateral release message.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="txHash" type="string">Transaction hash. Returned when confirming.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="loanId" type="integer">Loan ID, echoed back. Returned when confirming.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="repaidAmount" type="string">Principal plus interest the payment settled, read from the `LoanRepaid` event rather than from the calldata. It excludes the repayment fee. On a full repayment it is lower than `repayAmount`, because the contract pulls only what closes the loan. Returned when confirming.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="collateralReturned" type="string">Collateral released when the repayment closed the loan. Present only then. Returned when confirming.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="collateralReturnEscrowed" type="boolean">`true` when that return was blocked on compliance and escrowed on the adapter instead of delivered. Claim it with [Claim Escrowed Collateral](/endpoints/lending/claim-escrowed-collateral).</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/{marketId}/repay" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"loanId": 5, "all": true}'
```
```typescript TypeScript
const url = `https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/${marketId}/repay`;
const headers = {
  'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here',
  'Content-Type': 'application/json'
};

const res = await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ loanId: 5, all: true })
});
const { data } = await res.json();

const hashes: string[] = [];
for (const step of data.steps) {
  const tx = await wallet.sendTransaction(step.transaction);
  await tx.wait();
  hashes.push(tx.hash);
}

await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ loanId: 5, txHash: hashes[data.confirmStepIndex] })
});
```
</RequestExample>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `NO_MARKET_ADDRESS` | `400` | The market has no on-chain address recorded |
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `loanId` is not a positive integer, neither `amount` nor `all` was supplied, an amount is malformed or carries more decimal places than the borrow asset supports, or a `permit` field has the wrong shape |
| `NO_OUTSTANDING_DEBT` | `400` | `all` was set on a loan that owes nothing |
| `LOAN_NOT_ACTIVE` | `400` | The loan is closed and can no longer be modified |
| `INVALID_PERMIT` | `400` | `permit.deadline` is not in the future, `permit.value` breaks the binding rules, or the signature does not recover to the borrower for this spender, value, nonce and deadline |
| `PERMIT_VALUE_REQUIRED` | `400` | A `permit` was sent with `all: true` but without `permit.value` |
| `TX_WRONG_TARGET` | `400` | The confirmed transaction neither targets the market nor emits `LoanRepaid` from it. On a full repayment, confirm the `repay` step |
| `TX_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | The confirmed transaction repays a different loan |
| `REPAY_FAILED` | `400` | The repayment could not be prepared and no more specific code applied |
| `LOAN_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No such loan on this market |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `PERMIT_UNSUPPORTED` | `409` | A `permit` was sent for a settlement asset that does not implement EIP-2612, or whose support could not be verified. Use the approve-then-repay steps |
| `LOAN_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The loan could not be read from the network right now. Retry shortly |
| `MARKET_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market's transaction fees could not be read. Retry shortly |
| `PERMIT_UNVERIFIABLE` | `503` | The settlement asset's permit domain or the borrower's nonce could not be read, so the signature could not be checked. Retry, or use the approve-then-repay steps |

Confirming with `txHash` can also return any [transaction verification error](/endpoints/introduction#confirm-a-transaction).
