---
title: 'Repay Loan'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/{marketId}/repay'
description: 'Repay loan debt, interest first then principal'
---

Repays part or all of a loan's outstanding debt. Accrued interest is settled before principal. A repayment that clears the whole debt closes the loan in the same transaction and releases the collateral to the borrower, so no [Close Loan](/endpoints/lending/close-loan) call follows it.

Any wallet can repay any loan, so a third party can rescue a position approaching liquidation or an omnibus wallet can pay for its customer. The collateral always returns to the borrower, never to the payer. Name the paying wallet in `payer`: the allowance, the token's approval rule and the repayment are then checked from that wallet before you sign. A repayment sent without a `signedPrice` reads no price, so neither a stale price nor a paused market blocks it.

<Warning>
  To close a loan, repay with `all: true`. A payment of exactly the debt quoted earlier leaves the interest accrued since then outstanding. On a market with a repayment fee it also leaves a fee-sized remainder open. The loan then stays active and keeps accruing.
</Warning>

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/lending/list-markets), not its contract address. See [Market IDs](/endpoints/lending/list-markets#market-ids).</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="loanId" type="integer" required>
  On-chain loan ID. A positive integer. Accepted as a JSON number or as a numeric string, so the value [Get Loan](/endpoints/lending/get-loan) returns passes straight back.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="string">
  Amount to pay, as a positive decimal string in borrow asset units. The contract caps the payment at the debt plus the repayment fee, so overpaying does not transfer more than that. An amount that covers the current debt plus the repayment fee closes the loan, so it is built as a full repayment: `fullRepayment` is `true`, a `CUSTODY` loan carries `collateralRelease` when the collateral would be escrowed, and an approval is followed by a step that clears the leftover allowance. An amount above what a full repayment can need is sized like `all: true`: the approval and the repayment carry the figure in `forecast`, not the amount sent, `requestedAmount` echoes what you sent, and `warning` says so. Required unless `all` is `true` or you are confirming with `txHash`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="all" type="boolean">
  Repay the loan in full. The API reads the live debt and sizes the payment to it plus the interest it can accrue before the transaction mines, plus the repayment fee. See [Sizing a full repayment](#sizing-a-full-repayment). Use this instead of computing a payoff yourself.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="payer" type="string">
  The wallet that signs and pays, `0x` and 40 hex characters. The API reads its allowance, checks whether its token accepts the approval, and simulates the plan from it. Omit it and the plan is checked from the loan's borrower instead, with a 24 hour sizing window and a `PAYER_NOT_CHECKED` warning when the plan would fail from the borrower. Required with a `permit`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="mode" type="string">
  How the payer's wallet signs the plan: `batch`, `permit` or `sequential`. Defaults to `permit` when a `permit` is sent and to `sequential` otherwise. See [Modes](#modes).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signedPrice" type="object">
  EIP-712 signed price from [Sign Price](/endpoints/lending/sign-price), carried on-chain by the repayment. A repayment is the most frequent transaction a market sees, so carrying a price here keeps the oracle fresh without a separate write. Omit it and the repayment stays on the entry point that no price can block, which is the path a borrower must always have. It is not carried on the permit path. A market that prices from an external feed refuses a signed price with `EXTERNAL_FEED_ACTIVE`, and a market with a fixed price with `FIXED_PRICE_ACTIVE`, because neither oracle takes one. Leave it out there.
  <Expandable>
    <ParamField body="price" type="string">Price in base units.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="timestamp" type="integer">Signing timestamp.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="validUntil" type="integer">Signature expiry.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="signature" type="string">EIP-712 signature.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="permit" type="object">
  An EIP-2612 permit over the settlement asset, signed by `payer` with the market as spender. It replaces the approval, so the repayment is one transaction. Get the payload to sign from [Get Permit Quote](/endpoints/lending/get-permit-quote). See [Pay with a permit](#pay-with-a-permit) for the binding rules.
  <Expandable>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  Hash of the repay transaction you broadcast, the step at `confirmStepIndex`, or of the one transaction a wallet bundled the steps into. Send it to confirm the transaction and record the result. Omit it to receive the plan.
</ParamField>

## Modes

The response always tells you what the wallet will be asked for in `walletPrompts.label` and why in `walletPrompts.why`. Show both before the first signature.

| Mode | Wallet prompts | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| `batch` | `1 confirmation` | The payer's wallet supports EIP-5792 atomic batching (`wallet_sendCalls` with `atomicRequired: true`). Send every non-optional step as one bundle. The bundle ends with an `approve(0)` that clears the allowance, so nothing stays approved. |
| `permit` | `1 signature + 1 transaction` | The settlement asset implements EIP-2612 (`permitSupported: true`) and the payer is a wallet without contract code. The permit signature stands in for the approval. |
| `sequential` | `1 transaction`, `2 transactions` or `3 transactions` | Every other wallet, a multisig or smart account without batching included. One transaction when the payer's standing allowance already covers the amount, two for the approval and the repayment, three when the token refuses to move a non-zero allowance straight to a new value. |

A `batch` plan must run as one atomic bundle. If the wallet settles it as separate transactions, check what was applied before you build again.

## Sizing a full repayment

Interest accrues every second, so a full repayment approves more than the debt you read now. The market pulls only what closes the loan, and the rest stays as allowance until a clearing step resets it. The approved amount is:

`debt now + interest over the window + repayment fee + 1 base unit`

The window depends on how long the payer's wallet can take to sign and mine:

| Case | Window |
|---|---|
| `mode: batch` | 30 days |
| `mode: permit` | 1 hour |
| A named `payer` without contract code | 1 hour |
| A named `payer` that is a contract (a multisig or smart account) | 24 hours |
| No `payer` named | 24 hours |

The interest over the window uses the loan's stamped fixed rate where it has one. On a variable-rate loan it uses the curve rate at full utilization, the highest rate the curve can charge, so the figure is an upper bound. A variable-rate loan also counts the interest the market has accrued but not booked yet. `forecast` carries every part of the figure, and `forecast.leftoverMax` is the most that can be left over as allowance. A partial `amount` is approved exactly. An `amount` above this figure is approved at the figure, so a typed amount never leaves a larger allowance on the market than `all: true` would.

## Transaction Shape

The steps come in this order. Read `confirmStepIndex` and `optionalStepIndexes` rather than counting positions.

| Step | `stepKind` | Present when |
|---|---|---|
| `approve(market, 0)` | `approveZeroFirst` | The payer holds a non-zero allowance below the amount and its token refuses a direct change. With no `payer` named, the borrower's standing allowance decides it. |
| `approve(market, amount)` | `approve` | The payer's standing allowance does not cover the amount, or no `payer` was named. |
| `repay` or `repayWithPermit` | none | Always. This is the step at `confirmStepIndex`. |
| `approve(market, 0)` | `clearAllowance` | An approval or a permit preceded a full repayment. Optional and listed in `optionalStepIndexes` in `sequential` and `permit` mode. Required in `batch` mode, where it also follows a partial repayment. |

The optional clearing step's description names the forecast's `leftoverMax` figure. The repayment is complete once the repay step confirms, without it. Clearing sets the wallet's whole allowance for the market to zero, including any allowance it keeps there for liquidity or other loans. When the standing allowance already covers the amount, no approval and no clearing step are built, and `remainingAllowanceAfter` says how much of it stays in place.

### Simulation and gas

Before anything is returned, the API simulates the plan with `eth_simulateV1` from the payer, or from the borrower when no `payer` is named: the steps up to the repayment, or every required step of a bundle.

- A named payer is checked against its balance first. A wallet that holds less than the loan owes now, or less than a partial `amount`, is refused with `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE`. Its `details` name `balance`, `required` (what the market pulls now), `upTo` (on a full repayment, the forecast amount), `shortfall` (what to add) and `fullRepayment`. The message says to add the shortfall or to repay at most the balance.
- A named payer whose approval would revert is refused with `APPROVAL_REFUSED`. A repayment that would revert is refused with `REPAY_SIMULATION_FAILED`. Both carry `details` with `payer`, `failedStep` and the decoded reason in `revert`, and the message adds what to do. Nothing is built.
- With no `payer` named, a revert does not refuse the call. The plan is returned with a `PAYER_NOT_CHECKED` entry in `warnings`, naming the failing step in `failedStep` and the decoded reason in `revert`. If the borrower pays, resolve that reason before signing. If another wallet pays, build again with it as `payer`.

The repay step carries a `gasLimit` of the gas the simulated repayment used plus 50 percent, never below `600000` for `repay` or `650000` for `repayWithPermit`. `gasLimitBasis` says how it was set:

- `simulated`: the plan was simulated and the repayment succeeded in it.
- `floor`: the plan could not be simulated, or the repayment did not succeed in it, so the step carries the floor. Estimate the repayment's gas once the approval is mined, and use the larger of that estimate and `gasLimit`. A `batch` plan always reads `floor` and its repay step carries no `gasLimit`: the wallet sizes the bundle.

<Warning>
  The confirmable step is not always at the same index. It is `0` on the permit path and when the standing allowance covers the amount, `1` after one approval and `2` after `approveZeroFirst`. Confirming with an approval hash returns `TX_WRONG_TARGET`.
</Warning>

## Repayment fee

A market may carry a flat repayment fee, stamped when it was deployed and never changeable afterwards. Read it from `transactionFees` on [Get Fee Split](/endpoints/lending/get-fee-split). The payer bears it. The fee comes out of the payment first, and only the rest reduces the debt.

Each charge is capped at 0.5 percent of the debt it discharges. The contract caps a payment at the debt plus `min(repayFee, debt / 200)`, which is exactly what closes the loan. A partial payment reduces the debt by the payment less a fee of at most one 201st of it. When the fee is not zero, the response carries `transactionFee` and states the effect in `warning`.

## Pay with a permit

The permit path exists only where the settlement asset provably implements EIP-2612. `permitSupported` on the response says whether this market's asset qualifies. It is probed once per settlement asset and stored on the market, not asked per request. A token with a different permit shape, such as DAI's, reads as unsupported, and a permit sent to it is refused with `PERMIT_UNSUPPORTED`.

The permit is the payer's own. `payer` is required, it must be a wallet without contract code, and it also sends the transaction, because the market redeems the permit for the sender. The permit must name the market as spender and carry the token's current nonce for the payer. Its deadline must lie between 2 and 31 minutes after the current block time, and its value can never be unlimited or above what this repayment can need.

`repayWithPermit` binds the permit to the amount it repays, so the signed value decides what the calldata carries:

- With `amount`, the permit covers exactly `amount`. A `permit.value` that differs is refused with `INVALID_PERMIT`. An `amount` above what a full repayment can need is treated as `all: true`, so the rules below apply to it.
- With `all: true`, `permit.value` is required, or the call returns `PERMIT_VALUE_REQUIRED`. It becomes the amount the calldata offers, and it must cover the live debt plus the repayment fee, or the call returns `INVALID_PERMIT`.
- A full-repayment value that covers the debt now but not the debt plus the interest it can accrue until the permit's deadline builds with a `warning`. If interest outgrows the value before the transaction mines, the market takes the permit's value as a partial repayment and the loan stays open. A value quoted by [Get Permit Quote](/endpoints/lending/get-permit-quote) and signed unchanged does not trigger it.

Before any calldata is offered, the API recovers the signature against the token's domain and the payer's nonce, and checks that the token accepts the permit. A signature that does not recover to the payer, or that the token refuses, is refused with `INVALID_PERMIT`. An expired permit is refused the same way: quote it again and sign the new one. When the token's domain or the nonce cannot be read, the call returns `PERMIT_UNVERIFIABLE`.

## Confirming

Send the hash of the repay step, or of the bundle's one transaction, as `txHash`, with the same `all` or `amount` the build was sent. The API verifies the receipt, records the repayment and, when the loan closed, the collateral release. It then reads the loan at the receipt's block.

- `loanClosed` is `true` when the loan is closed, `false` when it is still open, and `null` when the loan could not be read.
- `remainder` is present while the loan is still open. `remainder.offerRepayRemainder` is `true` when a full repayment (`all: true`, or an `amount` sized down to the forecast) left debt open: interest accrued past the amount the repayment carried. Repay it as a new full repayment, which is sized afresh. After a partial `amount` it is `false`, and `remainder.note` states what is still outstanding. A confirmation sent with neither `all` nor `amount` falls back to the size of what is left: dust smaller than one percent of the payment is treated as interest past the amount.

```bash cURL
curl -X POST "https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/{marketId}/repay" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"loanId": 5, "all": true, "payer": "0xabc7f1093d5e26b804a1c3f78de025916b47c0d3", "mode": "sequential"}'
```
```typescript TypeScript
const url = `https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/positions/${marketId}/repay`;
const headers = {
  'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here',
  'Content-Type': 'application/json'
};
const payer = await wallet.getAddress();

const res = await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ loanId: 5, all: true, payer, mode: 'sequential' })
});
const { data } = await res.json();
console.log(data.walletPrompts.label, data.walletPrompts.why);

const optional = new Set(data.optionalStepIndexes);
let repayHash = '';
for (const [index, step] of data.steps.entries()) {
  if (optional.has(index) && !clearLeftoverAllowance) continue;
  const tx = await wallet.sendTransaction(step.transaction);
  await tx.wait();
  if (index === data.confirmStepIndex) repayHash = tx.hash;
}

const confirmed = await fetch(url, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({ loanId: 5, txHash: repayHash })
}).then((r) => r.json());
if (confirmed.data.remainder?.offerRepayRemainder) {
  console.log('Dust left: repay it as a new full repayment');
}
```
</RequestExample>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `NO_MARKET_ADDRESS` | `400` | The market has no on-chain address recorded |
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | `loanId` is not a positive integer, neither `amount` nor `all` was supplied, an amount is malformed or carries more decimal places than the borrow asset supports, `mode` is not `batch`, `permit` or `sequential`, `payer` is not an address, or a `permit` field has the wrong shape |
| `INVALID_PAYER` | `400` | `payer` is the zero address, or a mixed-case address whose EIP-55 checksum does not match |
| `NO_OUTSTANDING_DEBT` | `400` | `all` was set on a loan that owes nothing |
| `LOAN_NOT_ACTIVE` | `400` | The loan is closed and can no longer be modified |
| `PAYER_REQUIRED` | `400` | The permit path was chosen without `payer` |
| `PERMIT_REQUIRED` | `400` | `mode: permit` was sent without a `permit` |
| `INVALID_PERMIT` | `400` | The permit has expired, its deadline is less than 2 or more than 31 minutes ahead, its value is unlimited, above what the repayment can need or breaks the binding rules, the signature does not recover to the payer for this spender, value, nonce and deadline, or the token refuses the permit |
| `PERMIT_VALUE_REQUIRED` | `400` | A `permit` was sent with `all: true` but without `permit.value` |
| `INVALID_SIGNED_PRICE` | `400` | The `signedPrice` is malformed, expired, dated in the future or older than the oracle's price age window, or it was signed by a wallet that is not an authorized signer on the market's oracle, which `details.signer` then names. Sign a fresh quote, or repay without it |
| `PRICE_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE` | `400` | The `signedPrice` moves further from the oracle's current price than its `maxDeviationBps` allows |
| `PRICE_WINDOW_DEVIATION_TOO_LARGE` | `400` | The `signedPrice` falls outside the band the oracle accepts within its current one-hour window |
| `TX_WRONG_TARGET` | `400` | The confirmed transaction neither targets the market nor emits `LoanRepaid` from it. Confirm the step at `confirmStepIndex`, or the one transaction a wallet bundled the steps into, never an approval |
| `TX_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | The confirmed transaction repays a different loan |
| `INSUFFICIENT_BALANCE` | `400` | The named payer holds less than the loan owes now, or less than the partial `amount`. `details` names `balance`, `required`, `upTo`, `shortfall` and `fullRepayment`. Add the shortfall to the wallet, repay at most its balance, or pay from a funded wallet |
| `REPAY_FAILED` | `400` | The repayment could not be prepared and no more specific code applied |
| `LOAN_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No such loan on this market |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `APPROVAL_REFUSED` | `409` | The settlement token refuses the approval this repayment needs from the named payer, both to the amount and to zero, or the simulated approval reverts. A paused token or a blocked wallet does this. `details` names the payer, the failed step and the decoded revert when the simulation refused it. Resolve it with the token issuer |
| `REPAY_SIMULATION_FAILED` | `409` | The repayment reverts when simulated from the named payer. `details` names the payer, `failedStep` and the decoded reason in `revert` (a market error, a common token error, an `Error(string)` reason or the custom error selector). Nothing was built |
| `PERMIT_REQUIRES_EOA` | `409` | The payer is a contract account, which the token's permit check does not accept in this form. Repay with `mode: batch` or `sequential` |
| `PERMIT_UNSUPPORTED` | `409` | A `permit` was sent for a settlement asset that does not implement EIP-2612. `503` when its support could not be verified. Use the approval steps |
| `EXTERNAL_FEED_ACTIVE` | `409` | A `signedPrice` was sent to a market that prices from an external feed. Retry without it |
| `FIXED_PRICE_ACTIVE` | `409` | A `signedPrice` was sent to a market with a fixed price. Retry without it |
| `SIGNED_PRICE_OUTDATED` | `409` | The `signedPrice` is not dated after the price the oracle already holds, so the oracle would refuse it. Sign a fresh quote, or repay without it |
| `ALLOWANCE_UNREADABLE` | `503` | The named payer's allowance for the market could not be read, so nothing was built. Retry shortly |
| `CHAIN_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | Whether the payer is a contract, or whether the token accepts the new approval, could not be read. Retry shortly |
| `LOAN_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The loan, its rate or the market's accrual could not be read from the network right now, so the repayment cannot be sized. Retry shortly |
| `MARKET_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The market's transaction fees could not be read, or, with a `signedPrice`, its price oracle. Retry shortly |
| `PRICE_STATE_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The oracle could not be read to check the `signedPrice`, so nothing was built. Retry shortly, or repay without it |
| `PERMIT_UNVERIFIABLE` | `503` | The settlement asset's permit domain, the payer's nonce or the token's answer to the permit could not be read, so the signature could not be checked. Retry, or use the approval steps |

Confirming with `txHash` can also return any [transaction verification error](/endpoints/introduction#confirm-a-transaction).
