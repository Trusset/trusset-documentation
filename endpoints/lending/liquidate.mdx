---
title: 'Liquidate Loan'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/liquidations/{marketId}/liquidate/{loanId}'
description: 'Seize collateral from a loan that is undercollateralized or past its term'
---

Liquidates a loan on either of two triggers. A loan qualifies when its health factor is below `1.0`, and it qualifies when its term has run past its effective due date plus grace, whatever its health. A market that makes open-ended loans has only the first trigger.

Where the seized collateral goes depends on the market's `useDutchAuction` setting.

With Dutch auctions **disabled**, collateral is seized and transferred to the market's liquidation router, and a pending liquidation is created on the market and on the router. The response carries the router's `liquidationId`, `collateralSeized` and `debtRepaid`, and settlement becomes a manual operator workflow.

With Dutch auctions **enabled**, the collateral goes to the market's auction venue and a Dutch auction opens. The market records its pending liquidation at the seizure, but the router holds nothing. The response carries `auctionId`, and `liquidationId` is the market's own record ID rather than a router ID. The router takes part only if the auction expires with collateral unsold and [Settle Expired Auction](/endpoints/lending/settle-expired-auction) hands the remainder to it. A market priced from a live market price cannot enable Dutch auctions, and none can run before an auction venue is wired.

<Warning>
  The signing wallet must hold `LIQUIDATOR_ROLE` on the market. Role state is reported in `readiness` but never blocks the calldata. You sign in your own environment, so the wallet this API knows about is not necessarily the one that will call. A wallet without the role has its transaction reverted on-chain rather than rejected here. Pass `signerAddress` to have `readiness` answer for the wallet you intend to use.
</Warning>

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>Market ID. At most 100 characters.</ParamField>
<ParamField path="loanId" type="integer" required>On-chain loan ID. Must be a positive integer.</ParamField>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="signedPrice" type="object">
  EIP-712 signed price built with [Sign Price](/endpoints/lending/sign-price) and signed by the oracle's authorized signer. The contract applies it atomically with the liquidation, and only when it is newer than the stored price. Omit it to price against the stored oracle value. A stale stored price is refused with `PRICE_STALE` even when a `signedPrice` is supplied. Push a NAV with [Sync Oracle Price](/endpoints/lending/sync-oracle) first.
  <Expandable>
    <ParamField body="price" type="string">Price in base units.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="timestamp" type="integer">Signing timestamp.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="validUntil" type="integer">Signature expiry.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="signature" type="string">EIP-712 signature. Without it the object is ignored and an empty signed price is sent.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="signerAddress" type="string">
  Wallet you intend to liquidate with. Used to resolve `readiness` against that address rather than against your instance's primary verified wallet. It does not appear in the transaction and does not gate it. Anything that is not a valid address is ignored.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  Hash of the transaction you broadcast for this operation. Send it to confirm the transaction and record the result. Omit it to receive the calldata. A value that is not a `0x`-prefixed 64-character hex string is ignored, and the call answers as if it were omitted.
</ParamField>

## What One Call Realizes

On the health trigger a liquidation does not close the whole loan. The debt one call can discharge is capped by `closeFactor`, and the collateral seized for it is marked up by `liquidationPenalty` at the current price. A deeply underwater loan therefore needs several liquidations to clear. When the collateral runs out before the debt does, all of it is seized. The remaining debt is then written off at once, against the insurance fund first and the pool second.

On the term trigger the whole debt is realized in one call, marked up by the loan's term penalty instead of the liquidation penalty. The seizure takes only the collateral that requires, and the rest goes back to the borrower in the same transaction. Collateral that falls short is taken whole and the remaining debt is written off the same way.

On the auction path the venue takes `closeFactor` of the collateral against the same share of the debt, or all of it when the collateral is worth less than that debt. A term realization routed to an auction sizes its slice at the auction's floor price rather than at the oracle price.

There is no auto-sell for external securities. Once collateral reaches the router it stays there until an operator sells it and settles the proceeds. See [Settle Liquidation](/endpoints/lending/settle-liquidation). An auction sells only to verified bidders who take the price the venue quotes through [Bid on Auction](/endpoints/lending/bid-on-auction).

## Error Codes

Errors raised while building calldata carry the full `readiness` object alongside `code` and `message`, so the failing gate can be inspected without a second call. The examples above shorten it. The loan-level refusals also carry `loan`. When the loan itself cannot be read, the calldata is returned and the contract decides.

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `MARKET_NOT_CONFIGURED` | `400` | The market has no address or no oracle recorded |
| `INVALID_LOAN_ID` | `400` | `loanId` is not a positive integer |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | A `signedPrice` field has the wrong type |
| `LIQUIDATION_NOT_READY` | `400` | The router is not authorized on the market, or the collateral token will not let the router hold the seized tokens |
| `COLLATERAL_TRANSFER_RESTRICTED` | `400` | The collateral token's compliance rules block the seizure transfer to the router |
| `PRICE_STALE` | `400` | The oracle price is stale, so `liquidate` reverts. Push a NAV first; a `signedPrice` does not lift this refusal |
| `AUCTION_VENUE_NOT_ADMITTED` | `400` | Dutch auctions are on, but the collateral token does not admit the auction venue as a holder |
| `LOAN_IN_AUCTION` | `400` | An auction is already realizing this loan. The error carries `loan.auction` |
| `LOAN_NOT_ACTIVE` | `400` | The loan is closed, so there is nothing to realize. The error carries `loan` |
| `LOAN_NOT_LIQUIDATABLE` | `400` | The loan's health factor is at or above `1.0` and it carries no enforceable term. The error carries `loan`, including its `term` when it has one |
| `TX_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | The confirmed transaction liquidates a different loan |
| `LIQUIDATION_FAILED` | `400` | Confirming failed for a reason no more specific code covers, such as a chain read that did not answer |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |

Confirming with `txHash` can also return any [transaction verification error](/endpoints/introduction#confirm-a-transaction).

<Tip>
  A missing `LIQUIDATOR_ROLE` shows as `liquidatorRoleReady: false` in `readiness` rather than as an error. Resolve it through [Set Liquidator Role](/endpoints/lending/set-liquidator-role) when the market admin drives the grant, or let a [connected liquidation bot](/endpoints/lending/connect-bot) hold it.
</Tip>
