---
title: 'Get Market Metrics'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/metrics'
description: 'Read live pool metrics directly from the market contract'
---

Returns current liquidity, borrows, utilization, rates, fee balances and liquidation counters read from the chain at request time. This is the endpoint to poll for a live market dashboard. A healthy read is shared for up to 15 seconds, and any successful write request drops it, so the next poll after a write reads the chain again.

<Warning>
  When the chain is unavailable the endpoint returns `200` with `isOnChain: false` rather than an error status, and every figure is a placeholder: zeros, and a `borrowRate` of `"200"`. Branch on `isOnChain` before rendering any figure.

  `paused` is `null`, never `false`, when the pause flag could not be read. Rendering `null` as `false` can show a paused market as live.
</Warning>

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/lending/list-markets), not its contract address. See [Market IDs](/endpoints/lending/list-markets#market-ids).</ParamField>

## Response Fields

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="isOnChain" type="boolean">Whether the contract read succeeded. When `false`, every other field is a placeholder and must not be displayed as real data.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="paused" type="boolean">Whether the market contract is paused, or `null` when unreadable.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="liquidationContract" type="string">Liquidation router the market is wired to, or `null` if unreadable.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="priceSource" type="string">`NAV`, `MARKET`, `ORACLE` or `FIXED`, read from the contract. `null` when unreadable.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="totalDeposits" type="string">Total pool liquidity, in borrow asset units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="totalBorrows" type="string">Total outstanding principal, in borrow asset units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="availableLiquidity" type="string">Free liquidity, in borrow asset units: the pool's cash less every booked liability (fee balances, escrowed surplus, held sale proceeds), capped at liquidity minus borrows.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="utilizationRate" type="string">Utilization in basis points. `2500` is 25 percent.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="borrowRate" type="string">Annualized borrow rate in basis points. On a `FIXED` market it is the rate every new loan is stamped with. Otherwise it is the interest model's rate at the current utilization.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="curveBorrowRate" type="string">The interest model's rate at the current utilization, in basis points. On a `FIXED` market it prices only loans that carry no stamp, and `borrowRate` differs from it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="supplyRate" type="string">Annualized supply rate in basis points, derived from `borrowRate` as described below. It can carry up to four decimal places.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="totalShares" type="string">Total LP shares outstanding, always formatted at 18 decimals regardless of the borrow asset.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="totalCollateral" type="string">Total collateral locked, in collateral token units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="activeLoans" type="integer">Loans Trusset's position records hold as `ACTIVE`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="totalLoansCreated" type="integer">Lifetime loan counter from the contract.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="activeAuctions" type="integer">Dutch auctions currently accepting bids on the market's auction venue.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="totalAuctions" type="integer">Lifetime auction counter from the auction venue. `0` when the market has none.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="pendingLiquidations" type="integer">Recorded liquidations not yet settled or written off.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="pendingLiquidationDebt" type="string">Debt awaiting settlement proceeds, in borrow asset units, from the contract.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="auctionModule" type="string">The market's auction venue, lowercased, or `null` when the market has none.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="readFailed" type="boolean">Present and `true` when a side read failed. The named figures are still the contract's, but a counter may be short. Absent on a clean read.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

### Rate offering

Present when the market's interest model could be read. These describe how the market prices new loans.

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="rateMode" type="string">`CURVE`, `CLAMPED` or `FIXED`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="rateFloor" type="string">Floor in basis points. `"0"` on `CURVE`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="rateCap" type="string">Cap in basis points. `"0"` on `CURVE`.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="maxFixedRate" type="string">Ceiling on any fixed rate the rate model owner can set, in basis points, or `null` when none is set. It can only move down.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="fixedStampRateBps" type="integer">The rate a loan opened right now would be stamped with. `0` when the model stamps nothing.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="stampsFixedRate" type="boolean">Whether the model stamps a rate onto each new loan. When `true`, `borrowRate` equals `fixedStampRateBps`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

### Terms and exit

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="termConfig" type="object">
      The market's term offering read from the contract, or `null` when unreadable. A market with `maxLoanDuration` and `terminalDueDate` both `"0"` makes open-ended loans.
      <Expandable>
        <ResponseField name="maxLoanDuration" type="string">Seconds a loan may run.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="termGracePeriod" type="string">Seconds after the due date before an unpaid loan can be liquidated on its term.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="terminalDueDate" type="string">Unix seconds every loan falls due by, `"0"` when unset.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="marketDueFloor" type="string">Market-wide extension floor in unix seconds, `"0"` when unset.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="termPenaltyBps" type="integer">Penalty on a term liquidation, in basis points.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="allowExtension" type="boolean">Whether a borrower may be granted more time.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="termStamping" type="string">`PER_LOAN` when the implementation stamps the grace period and term penalty onto each loan at signing, so a term change reaches new loans only. `MARKET_CONFIG` when open loans follow the live grace period and penalty. `UNKNOWN` when it could not be read.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="maxLoanDuration" type="string">Same value as `termConfig.maxLoanDuration`, flattened onto the response. `termGracePeriod`, `terminalDueDate`, `marketDueFloor`, `termPenalty` and `allowExtension` are flattened alongside it. `terminalDueDate` and `marketDueFloor` are `null` rather than `"0"` when unset here, and `termPenalty` is basis points.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="latestDueAt" type="string">Unix seconds of the furthest due date stamped on any loan so far. It only moves forward, so it can name a loan that has since closed. `"0"` when no loan has carried a term.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="exitHorizon" type="object">
      When the book could in principle have turned liquid, absent new borrows. `null` when unreadable.
      <Expandable>
        <ResponseField name="horizon" type="string">Unix seconds: the latest due date plus the longest grace period. `null` when no loan carries a term.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="bounded" type="boolean">`false` while any open-ended principal is outstanding. No horizon exists then, and withdrawals depend on borrower repayment.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="applies" type="boolean">`true` when the horizon is bounded, set and the book holds loans.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="basis" type="string">Present when `applies` is `true`. `OPEN_LOANS` when the horizon was tightened to the open loans' own due dates, `HIGH_WATER_MARK` when it rests on `latestDueAt`.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="newLoansClosed" type="boolean">`true` when the market's terminal due date has passed. The contract then refuses every new loan, while repayment, collateral release and withdrawals stay open. `null` when unreadable.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="newLoansClosedReason" type="string">Why, in words, when `newLoansClosed` is `true`. Otherwise `null`.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

### Liquidity routing

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="borrowableLiquidity" type="string">What a new loan can draw from the pool itself right now, in borrow asset units: `availableLiquidity` less the reserve earmark. Vault liquidity is not in this figure.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="useVaultLiquidity" type="boolean">Whether the market may draw on its registered vaults at the moment a loan opens and its own pool runs short. `null` when unreadable.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="vaultOnlyLiquidity" type="boolean">`true` on current markets, where only the operator wallet and the vaults the market admin registered may add liquidity directly. `false` on the earlier generation. `null` when unreadable.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reserveRatio" type="string">Share of the pool's liquidity held back from borrowing, in basis points. Withdrawals may still consume it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="reserveRatioBps" type="integer">The same value as an integer.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

<Note>
  Gate a borrow control on `borrowableLiquidity` plus, on a market with `useVaultLiquidity`, what its vaults can still draw. [Open Loan](/endpoints/lending/open-loan) checks both before it returns calldata and refuses a loan neither can fund with `INSUFFICIENT_LIQUIDITY`.
</Note>

### Fees

<ResponseField name="data" type="object">
  <Expandable>
    <ResponseField name="feeShareGeneration" type="string">`FIXED_SHARES` on current markets, which pay the fixed 8 percent operator share and 5 percent distributor share. `OFFERED_SHARE` on the earlier generation, which pays the operator share offered at deployment and no distributor share. `null` when unreadable.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="distributorShare" type="string">The distributor share in basis points: `"500"` on `FIXED_SHARES`, `"0"` on `OFFERED_SHARE`, `null` when unreadable.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="operatorShare" type="string">Present only when the stored operator share had to be corrected from the factory on this read. [Get Market](/endpoints/lending/get-market) always carries it.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="operatorWallet" type="string">The wallet operator fees are paid to, lowercased, or `null` before a lender of record takes the market.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="operatorFeesPayable" type="string">Operator fees accrued and not yet swept, in borrow asset units. `null` when unreadable. `operatorFeesPayableRaw` carries the same figure in base units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="distributorFeesPayable" type="string">Distributor fees accrued and not yet swept, in borrow asset units. `distributorFeesPayableRaw` carries base units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="protocolFeesPayable" type="string">The insurance fund's share of interest, accrued and not yet swept into the fund, in borrow asset units. `protocolFeesPayableRaw` carries base units.</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="infrastructureFeesPayable" type="string">Trusset infrastructure fees accrued and not yet swept, in borrow asset units. `infrastructureFeesPayableRaw` carries base units.</ResponseField>
  </Expandable>
</ResponseField>

These are balances waiting on the market, and anyone may sweep them. See [Sweep Operator Fees](/endpoints/lending/sweep-operator-fees), [Sweep Distributor Fees](/endpoints/lending/sweep-distributor-fees), [Sweep Protocol Fees](/endpoints/lending/sweep-protocol-fees) and [Sweep Infrastructure Fees](/endpoints/lending/sweep-infrastructure-fees). The split itself is on [Get Fee Split](/endpoints/lending/get-fee-split).

## Rate Derivation

The interest model's rate at the current utilization is `curveBorrowRate`. When the contract reports a zero rate, it is computed from a default curve instead. `borrowRate` is that figure, unless the model stamps a fixed rate onto new loans, in which case it is the stamp.

`supplyRate` is `borrowRate * utilization * lpFactor`, where `lpFactor` is the share of repaid interest that reaches the liquidity providers. The chain answers for that share. On a `FIXED_SHARES` market the 15 percent Trusset infrastructure fee comes off first. The 8 percent operator share and the 5 percent distributor share are taken from the remainder, and the market's `protocolFeeRate` from what is left after them. On an `OFFERED_SHARE` market the operator share offered at deployment takes the place of the two fixed shares.

When the chain has never answered for the factor, the calculation falls back to a flat `0.75`. That is an estimate, not the market's economics.

All rates are basis points, so divide by 100 to get a percentage.

<Note>
  On a `FIXED` market an outstanding loan pays the rate stamped when it opened. That can differ from today's stamp if the rate model owner has changed it since. `borrowRate` and `fixedStampRateBps` describe the loan that would open now.
</Note>

<RequestExample>
```bash cURL
curl "https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/{marketId}/metrics" \
  -H "X-API-Key: trusset_your_key_here"
```
```typescript TypeScript
const res = await fetch(
  `https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/${marketId}/metrics`,
  { headers: { 'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here' } }
);
const { data } = await res.json();
if (!data.isOnChain) throw new Error('Market metrics unavailable');
console.log(`Borrow APR ${(Number(data.borrowRate) / 100).toFixed(2)}%`);
```
</RequestExample>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `NO_MARKET_ADDRESS` | `400` | The market has no on-chain address recorded |
| `MISSING_MARKET_ID` | `400` | `marketId` is longer than 100 characters |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
