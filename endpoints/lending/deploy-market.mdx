---
title: 'Deploy Market'
api: 'POST /lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/deploy'
description: 'Deploy a lending market for an imported security token'
---

Deploys a complete market in one factory transaction: the lending module, its price oracle, interest rate model, insurance fund, collateral adapter, and a liquidation router dedicated to this market. The same transaction attests the market's asset data. The hash of a canonical bundle built from the token's profile travels inside the deployment, signed for by the deploying wallet.

The market is deployed **seeking a lender of record**. It names no admin, no issuer and no collateral agent. The deployment nominates `candidates`, or sets `openToAnyCurator` to offer the market beyond them. The market stays inert until an institution takes the role through [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role). See [Lender of record](/endpoints/lending/introduction#lender-of-record).

Sign with the wallet named in `signer`, this instance's registered wallet. The factory records the sending wallet as the market's deployer and as the attestor of its asset data. Only that wallet or the factory owner can nominate candidates later.

The deployment sets the opening rate offering and term offering. After adoption, the issuer the lender of record names can change both. A new rate mode reaches open variable-rate loans from their next accrual, while a stamped fixed rate stays with the loan it was stamped on. A new term offering reaches new loans only. See [Set Rate Mode](/endpoints/lending/set-rate-mode) and [Set Term Config](/endpoints/lending/set-term-config), and read the ranges and the factory's own limits first with [Get Deployment Options](/endpoints/lending/get-deployment-options).

A collateral token can back several markets as long as they differ in collateral mode or in settlement token (stablecoin). A second market for the same token, mode and settlement asset returns `409 MARKET_EXISTS`.

<Warning>
  A freshly deployed market cannot take loans or liquidity. Two things are outstanding. A candidate must take the lender-of-record role, and the collateral token's agent or identity registry operator must authorize the market's adapter and router on the token. That party is typically neither Trusset nor the deployer. Call [Get Setup Steps](/endpoints/lending/get-setup-steps) after deployment for the outstanding items and their calldata.
</Warning>

## Body Parameters

<ParamField body="collateralTokenAddress" type="string" required>
  Security token accepted as collateral. Must be imported into this instance with a published profile, and must not be the same token as the borrow asset. Required on the confirm call as well.

  `CUSTODY` mode needs only an ERC-20 surface (`balanceOf` and `decimals`), so ERC-20, ERC-1400 and ERC-3643 tokens all qualify. `FREEZE` mode also needs the ERC-3643 freeze surface (`isAgent` or `getFrozenTokens`). See [Collateral token standards](/endpoints/lending/introduction#collateral-token-standards).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="initialPrice" type="string">
  Opening collateral price as a decimal string, for example `"104.82"`. Must be greater than zero, with at most 18 decimal places. It is denominated in the borrow asset and parsed at its decimals. A market settling in USDC therefore accepts at most 6 decimal places here.

  Required for the `NAV` and `MARKET` price sources. An `ORACLE` market takes its first price from the feed and ignores this field.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="candidates" type="array" default="[]">
  Addresses nominated to become the market's lender of record. Up to 20 entries, each a checksum-valid address that is not the zero address. Duplicates are dropped. Nominating stays possible after deployment through [Nominate Candidate](/endpoints/lending/nominate-candidate). An empty list is a market nobody can take yet, not a market nobody can ever take.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="openToAnyCurator" type="boolean" default="false">
  Offer the market to claimants beyond the named candidates. The factory admits a claimant nobody nominated when the borrower register the deployer installed verifies it. Through this API the claimant must also be an account Trusset approved as a lender of record. The claim takes two transactions, announced and then completed after a delay. See [Accept Lender Role](/endpoints/operators/accept-lender-role#claim-an-open-market).

  An open market deployed with no `identityRegistry` is narrowed rather than refused. With no borrower register to admit them, claimants nobody nominated are refused, so only a nominee can take the market.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="mode" type="string" default="FREEZE">
  `FREEZE` or `CUSTODY`. See [Collateral modes](/endpoints/lending/introduction#collateral-modes). Cannot be changed after deployment.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="priceSource" type="string" default="NAV">
  `NAV`, `MARKET` or `ORACLE`. Cannot be changed after deployment.

  `NAV` is a price the issuer publishes. `MARKET` signals an observable market price: the contract then refuses Dutch auctions and holds the auction floor premium at 98 percent or more. `ORACLE` reads the external feed named in `oracleAddress` and takes no pushed price at all.

  A `MARKET` deployment also needs `identityRegistry`, because a lender of record can only adopt such a market with a borrower register installed.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="oracleAddress" type="string">
  The external feed an `ORACLE` market reads prices from. Required when `priceSource` is `ORACLE`, and refused for every other price source. The feed is probed before any calldata is handed out. It must answer `decimals()`, plus `latestRoundData()` or the `latestAnswer()` and `latestTimestamp()` pair. Its price must be positive and dated within the last 24 hours.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="borrowAssetAddress" type="string">
  Settlement token (stablecoin) for the market. Any ERC-20 with 6 to 18 decimals, validated on-chain. Omit to use the network's USDC. Cannot be changed after deployment. See [List Borrow Assets](/endpoints/lending/list-borrow-assets).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="identityRegistry" type="string">
  The borrower register, installed on the market by this transaction. Borrowers are examined against it on every loan. It must answer `isVerified(address)`, as an ERC-3643 identity registry, an ONCHAINID-based register and the Trusset register all do.

  Required for the `MARKET` price source and optional for `NAV` and `ORACLE`. Omitted, the collateral token's own compliance alone governs who may borrow. A register installed here stands at adoption: the lender of record cannot bring a different one. The market admin can change it afterwards with [Set Identity Gates](/endpoints/lending/set-identity-gates).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="providerRegistry" type="string">
  The register the deployer proposes for liquidity providers. It is recorded on the factory and installed by nothing: which provider register the market uses is the lender of record's decision at adoption. It must answer `isVerified(address)`, exactly like `identityRegistry`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="fallbackDecimals" type="integer" default="0">
  Decimals to assume if the collateral token's `decimals()` cannot be read on-chain. Integer between 0 and 36.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="txHash" type="string">
  Hash of the transaction you broadcast for this operation. Send it to confirm the transaction and record the result. Omit it to receive the calldata.
</ParamField>

## Rate offering

The market's interest model is deployed with the market, and the rate offering sets how it prices from the first loan. All values are basis points.

<ParamField body="rateMode" type="string" default="CURVE">
  `CURVE`, `CLAMPED` or `FIXED`.

  `CURVE` is the default utilization curve and takes no bounds. `CLAMPED` is that curve held between a floor and a cap. `FIXED` stamps one rate onto each loan when it opens, and that rate does not move for the life of the loan.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="rateFloorBps" type="integer">
  Lower bound for `CLAMPED`. Integer, 0 to 50000. Defaults to `0` under `CLAMPED`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="rateCapBps" type="integer">
  Upper bound for `CLAMPED`, and required by it. Integer, 1 to 50000, and at least `rateFloorBps`. A binding cap never suspends borrowing: the rate saturates at the cap.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="fixedRateBps" type="integer">
  The stamped rate for `FIXED`, and required by it. Integer, 1 to 50000.
</ParamField>

The three modes do not mix. Sending `rateFloorBps` or `rateCapBps` with `FIXED`, or `fixedRateBps` with `CLAMPED`, is refused rather than silently ignored. Sending any bound without a `rateMode` of `CLAMPED` or `FIXED` is refused too. A `FIXED` market also needs `maxLoanDurationDays`, because the term clock replaces the rate curve as the pull toward repayment.

The factory also bounds creator-set rates with its `creatorRateCeiling`, read at build time. [Get Deployment Options](/endpoints/lending/get-deployment-options) returns it as `creatorRateCeilingBps`.

## Term offering

A market with no term offering makes open-ended loans. Setting a duration, a terminal date, or both turns terms on, and [Open Loan](/endpoints/lending/open-loan) then returns the due date in its `offering` block before the borrower signs.

<ParamField body="maxLoanDurationDays" type="integer">
  How long a loan may run before it falls due. Integer number of days, 1 to 3650.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="terminalDueDate" type="integer | string">
  A hard date every loan on this market falls due by, whatever its own duration. A unix timestamp in seconds, or an ISO date. Must be in the future.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="termGracePeriodDays" type="integer">
  Days after the due date before the term penalty applies. Integer, 1 to 30. Omitted on a market with terms, the contract applies its 3-day default. Sending it without `maxLoanDurationDays` or `terminalDueDate` is refused.
</ParamField>

## Fee shares

Every market pays its operator a fixed 8 percent and its distributors a fixed 5 percent of repaid interest, both taken after the 15 percent Trusset infrastructure fee. The factory holds both as constants and no party can change them. A deployment therefore carries no share offer, and a request that sends `operatorShareBps` is refused with `OPERATOR_SHARE_FIXED`.

The operator share is paid to the wallet that takes the lender-of-record role. The calldata response echoes both shares under `feeShares`.

## Asset data

The deployment builds the market's asset data bundle from the token as this instance imported it. The bundle holds the chain, factory, collateral mode, price source and settlement asset, the token's recorded facts, and the issuer's published profile. The bundle's `contentHash` rides inside `deployMarket`, so the signature that creates the market also attests the data. A token without a published profile is refused with `TOKEN_PROFILE_REQUIRED`.

The calldata response returns the exact bytes being attested under `assetData`, so the deploying wallet sees what it signs for. [Preview Asset Data](/endpoints/lending/preview-asset-data) returns the same bundle before anything is built. A lender of record reviews it and names the hash it read when it takes the role.

Confirm by sending `txHash` together with `collateralTokenAddress`. Every other field is an optional cross-check against the deployment the receipt proves: a supplied `mode` or `borrowAssetAddress` that differs returns `TX_WRONG_ARGS`. Drop any `confirmWith.body` field that is `null` before sending it, because this endpoint's schema refuses `null` for an address.

Confirming returns `201` with the recorded market and its readiness state.

```typescript TypeScript
const base = 'https://api.trusset.org/lending-external-securities-v2/api/markets/deploy';
const headers = {
  'X-API-Key': 'trusset_your_key_here',
  'Content-Type': 'application/json'
};

const build = await fetch(base, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({
    collateralTokenAddress: '0x9f8c1d4b2e7a3056c1b8f4d29e0a7c3518b6d24f',
    initialPrice: '104.82',
    candidates: [candidateAddress],
    mode: 'CUSTODY',
    borrowAssetAddress: '0x6B175474E89094C44Da98b954EedeAC495271d0F'
  })
});
const { data: calldata } = await build.json();

const tx = await wallet.sendTransaction(calldata.transaction);
await tx.wait();

const confirm = await fetch(base, {
  method: 'POST',
  headers,
  body: JSON.stringify({
    txHash: tx.hash,
    collateralTokenAddress: calldata.collateralTokenAddress
  })
});
const { data: recorded } = await confirm.json();
```

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | `400` | A field failed schema validation, including a rate or term offering that mixes modes, a missing `initialPrice` for `NAV` or `MARKET`, and `oracleAddress` sent or missing against the price source. `error.details` names each offending field |
| `OPERATOR_SHARE_FIXED` | `400` | `operatorShareBps` was sent. The shares are fixed by the contracts |
| `TOKEN_NOT_IMPORTED` | `400` | `collateralTokenAddress` is not a security token this instance imported or issued |
| `BORROW_ASSET_IS_COLLATERAL` | `400` | `borrowAssetAddress` and `collateralTokenAddress` are the same token |
| `COLLATERAL_TOKEN_NOT_CONTRACT` | `400` | No deployed bytecode at `collateralTokenAddress` on this network |
| `COLLATERAL_TOKEN_NOT_ERC20` | `400` | The token does not answer `balanceOf` and `decimals`, so the factory would refuse it |
| `FREEZE_MODE_UNSUPPORTED_TOKEN` | `400` | `mode` is `FREEZE` but the token exposes no ERC-3643 freeze surface. Deploy in `CUSTODY` mode instead |
| `CUSTODY_MODE_EXCLUDED` | `400` | The factory excludes custody mode for this token. Deploy a `FREEZE` market instead |
| `INVALID_BORROW_ASSET` | `400` | `borrowAssetAddress` is not a valid address |
| `BORROW_ASSET_NOT_CONTRACT` | `400` | No deployed bytecode at `borrowAssetAddress` |
| `BORROW_ASSET_NOT_ERC20` | `400` | `decimals()` or `balanceOf()` could not be read on the borrow asset |
| `BORROW_ASSET_DECIMALS_UNSUPPORTED` | `400` | Borrow asset decimals outside 6 to 18 |
| `INVALID_IDENTITY_REGISTRY` | `400` | `identityRegistry` or `providerRegistry` fails its EIP-55 checksum |
| `INVALID_ORACLE_ADDRESS` | `400` | `oracleAddress` is the zero address or fails its checksum |
| `INVALID_INITIAL_PRICE` | `400` | `initialPrice` carries more decimal places than the borrow asset supports, or rounds to zero at its scale |
| `INVALID_RATE_OFFERING` | `400` | A `CLAMPED` cap below its floor |
| `RATE_ABOVE_CREATOR_CEILING` | `400` | The rate offering exceeds the factory's creator rate ceiling. The message names the ceiling |
| `INVALID_TERM_OFFERING` | `400` | `terminalDueDate` is not a unix timestamp or ISO date, or is not in the future |
| `DEPLOY_FAILED` | `400` | The deployment could not be prepared for a reason that carries no code of its own. `message` says why |
| `MARKET_EXISTS` | `409` | This instance already runs a market for this token, mode and settlement asset that the factory holds. `error` carries `action: "USE_EXISTING_MARKET"`, `marketId` and `archiveWith` |
| `MARKET_UNRECORDED` | `409` | A matching market was already deployed by this instance's wallet and never recorded. `details` carries `marketAddress`, `deployer` and `txHash`. Record it by sending that `txHash` with `collateralTokenAddress` to this endpoint instead of deploying again |
| `WALLET_NOT_CONFIGURED` | `412` | This instance has no verified wallet to sign the deployment |
| `TOKEN_PROFILE_REQUIRED` | `422` | The token has no published profile, so there is no asset data to attest. `details.editProfile` names where to add it |
| `IDENTITY_REGISTRY_REQUIRED` | `422` | `priceSource` is `MARKET` and no `identityRegistry` was given |
| `IDENTITY_REGISTRY_INCOMPATIBLE` | `422` | `identityRegistry` or `providerRegistry` does not answer `isVerified(address)`. `details` names the field and the register |
| `ORACLE_FEED_NOT_A_CONTRACT` | `422` | No contract at `oracleAddress` |
| `ORACLE_FEED_UNUSABLE` | `422` | The feed does not answer `decimals()`, answers neither price shape, or answers a non-positive or undated price |
| `ORACLE_FEED_STALE` | `422` | The feed's price is older than 24 hours |
| `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE` | `503` | The collateral token could not be read on-chain, so no transaction was prepared |
| `IDENTITY_REGISTRY_UNREADABLE` | `503` | A named register could not be reached to check it. Retry shortly |
| `ORACLE_FEED_UNVERIFIABLE` | `503` | The feed could not be checked right now. Retry shortly |
| `FACTORY_READ_FAILED` | `503` | The factory could not be read to validate the offering, to confirm the fixed shares, or to tell whether an existing market is still operable. Nothing was built. Retry shortly |
| `FACTORY_UPGRADE_REQUIRED` | `503` | The factory runs an implementation without a liquidation router, without asset data attestation, or without fixed fee shares. Nothing was built |

Confirming with `txHash` can also return:

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `TX_WRONG_ARGS` | `400` | The transaction deployed a market for a different collateral token, collateral mode or settlement asset than the one supplied |
| `TX_NOT_VERIFIED` | `400` | The transaction deployed no market |
| `DEPLOYMENT_NOT_INSTANCE_BOUND` | `403` | Neither the signer nor any party of the deployment is a verified wallet of this instance |
| `MARKET_EXISTS` | `409` | This market is already recorded in this instance |
| `FACTORY_MISMATCH` | `409` | The transaction did not target the lending factory this instance deploys to |
| `ADAPTER_PLEDGEE_UNSUPPORTED` | `422` | The collateral adapter does not record a pledgee per lock, so encumbrances would name no attributable beneficiary |
| `DEPLOYMENT_UNREADABLE` | `503` | The deployed market could not be read from the factory |
| `FACTORY_READ_FAILED` | `503` | The factory could not be reached to resolve the market. Retry shortly |
| `BORROW_ASSET_UNREADABLE` | `503` | The market's settlement asset could not be read and none was supplied |

A supplied `borrowAssetAddress` is validated on the confirm call too, so the four borrow asset codes above can also appear there. Confirming can also return any [transaction verification error](/endpoints/introduction#confirm-a-transaction).
