---
title: 'Get Liquidation Desk'
api: 'GET /lending-external-securities-v2/api/liquidations/{marketId}/desk'
description: 'Everything the liquidation desk of one market needs, in one read'
---

Returns, in one read, what a lender of record needs to work the market's liquidations: the loans that can be realized now, the loans close to it, the running and ended Dutch auctions, the records awaiting sale, how the records compare with the chain, and whether the lender of record's liquidator is ready. Every figure is read from the chain. A part that could not be read says so; it never reads as an all-clear.

Poll it with the `cursor` the previous read returned. While nothing on the market moved and nothing needs work, the answer is `unchanged: true` with no body, which costs one small batched read. `pollSeconds` says when to read again: `120` on a quiet desk, `30` while anything needs work.

## Path Parameters

<ParamField path="marketId" type="string" required>The market's record ID, as `id` on [List Markets](/endpoints/lending/list-markets), not its contract address. At most 100 characters.</ParamField>

## Query Parameters

<ParamField query="cursor" type="string">The `cursor` of the previous read. Ignored when longer than 64 characters.</ParamField>

<ParamField query="watchBand" type="number" default="1.25">Health factor below which a loan is listed in `loans.watchLoans`, from `1.01` to `3`. Other values fall back to the default.</ParamField>

<ParamField query="graceDays" type="integer" default="7">Days ahead in which a termed loan whose grace period ends is listed in `loans.watchLoans`, from `0` to `90`.</ParamField>

## Response Fields

    <ResponseField name="pollSeconds" type="integer">Seconds until the next read is worth making.</ResponseField>

    <ResponseField name="pending" type="object">`rows`, `readFailed` and `truncated`: the records awaiting sale, as on [Get Pending Liquidations](/endpoints/lending/get-pending-liquidations).</ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="sync" type="object">

        <ResponseField name="watchBehind" type="boolean">Whether the background watch reports this market behind.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="watchGaveUpAt" type="string">ISO 8601 time the background adoption stopped retrying, or `null`.</ResponseField>
        <ResponseField name="lastSyncedAt" type="string">ISO 8601 time of this comparison.</ResponseField>
      </Expandable>
    </ResponseField>
    <ResponseField name="lenderOfRecord" type="object">`ready` (whether a wallet or connected bot of the lender of record holds `LIQUIDATOR_ROLE`, `null` when unread), `via` (`WALLET` or `BOT`), `holder`, and `routerAdmin`, the lender-of-record wallet that holds `DEFAULT_ADMIN_ROLE` on the router and signs router-side setup.</ResponseField>

## Error Codes

| Code | HTTP | Cause |
|---|---|---|
| `NO_MARKET_ADDRESS` | `400` | The market has no on-chain address yet |
| `MARKET_NOT_FOUND` | `404` | No market with this ID on your instance |
| `CHAIN_READ_FAILED` | `503` | The desk could not be read, so whether anything is open is unknown. Retry in a moment |
