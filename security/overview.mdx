Trusset is non-custodial infrastructure. It holds no client keys and no client assets.

## DORA, ISO, TÜV, GDPR, and audit documentation

Trusset provides a searchable database of regularly updated compliance documents. Those documents are currently being reviewed by TÜV as part of the final certification process. You can find the database in the [Issuer App](https://issuers.trusset.org/) under "Compliance Data Room".

<Tip>This allows issuers to forward the required infrastructure documentation to their authorities on request, with a full audit trail.</Tip>

## Incident management

Security incidents are visible and managed on the [Trusset status page](https://status.trusset.org/), covering the following services:

- Trusset APIs - [api.trusset.org](https://api.trusset.org)
- Trusset Issuer App - [issuers.trusset.org](https://issuers.trusset.org)
- Trusset Custodian App - [custodians.trusset.org](https://custodians.trusset.org)
- Trusset Whitelabel Infrastructure - [app.trusset.org](https://app.trusset.org)

## Secret management

Development and Staging secrets are managed via [Doppler](https://www.doppler.com/). Production secrets are managed via [HashiCorp Vault](https://www.hashicorp.com/products/vault), deployed in a hardened Docker container.

## Contract updates

Trusset cannot update global contracts (e.g. the Identity Register) as a single party. Global contract updates require multisignature approval from the TrussetDAO, consisting of all dedicated issuers in the Trusset ecosystem, and the Trusset Board.

## Audits

Softstack audited the full smart contract protocol in May 2026. Read the [audit report](https://www.trusset.org/audits/trusset-audit.pdf).

## Audit policy

Once a year, Trusset renews all security audits with a chosen third-party auditor based in the EU.

ISO certification with TÜV is in progress and not yet complete. Once it is granted, it enters the same annual renewal cycle.

## App updates

Updates undergo a strict, 3-step process that is defined internally and in the Trusset compliance documentation.
